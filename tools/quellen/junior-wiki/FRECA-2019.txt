The '''2019 Formula Regional European Championship''' was a multi-event, [[Formula Three|Formula 3]] [[Open-wheel car|open-wheel single seater]] [[Motorsport|motor racing]] championship held across Europe. The championship features a mix of professional and amateur drivers, competing in Formula 3 cars that conform to the [[FIA]] Formula 3 regulations for the championship. In the inaugural season of the championship, [[Frederik Vesti]] was the drivers' champion.

The season commenced on 14 April at [[Circuit Paul Ricard]] and concluded on 20 October at [[Autodromo Nazionale Monza]], after eight meetings.

==Teams and drivers==
All teams and drivers competed with the [[Tatuus]]-[[Alfa Romeo]] [[Tatuus F.3 T-318|F.3 T-318]].<ref>{{cite news|url=http://www.acisport.it/en/FORMULAREGIONAL/news/2019/52979/tatuus-f3-ready-for-scuderia-df-corse-by-corbetta|title=Tatuus F3 ready for Scuderia DF Corse by Corbetta|publisher=ACI Sport|accessdate=7 February 2019}}</ref>

==Race calendar==

The provisional calendar was announced on 31 October 2018.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Costa |first1=Massimo |title=Esclusivo – Il calendario 2019 della European F3 Regional Championship |url=https://www.italiaracing.net/newsint.aspx?id=236258&cat=1089 |accessdate=2 November 2018 |work=ItaliaRacing.net |publisher=Inpagina |date=2 November 2018}}</ref> The schedule was altered on 9 November 2018 with [[Hockenheimring]] replaced by [[Circuit Paul Ricard]],<ref>{{cite news |last1=Costa |first1=Massimo |title=Calendario 2019 – Ecco le date e l'ingresso di Le Castellet |url=https://www.italiaracing.net/newsint.aspx?id=236298&cat=1089 |accessdate=9 November 2018 |work=ItaliaRacing.net |publisher=Inpagina |date=8 November 2018}}</ref> and on 1 December 2018, with the round at [[Autodromo Enzo e Dino Ferrari]] moved from its original 9 June date.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Costa |first1=Massimo |title=Definito il calendario 2019 – Cinque i circuiti della F1 |url=https://www.italiaracing.net/Definito-il-calendario-2019-Cinque-i-circuiti-della-F1/236464/1089 |accessdate=10 December 2018 |work=ItaliaRacing.net |publisher=Inpagina |date=1 December 2018}}</ref> The last two race weekends of the season at Monza and Mugello were swapped.<ref>{{cite news|url=http://www.acisport.it/en/FORMULAREGIONAL/news/2019/51904/dates-reversed-between-mugello-and-monza/Paul%20Ricard|title=Dates reversed between Mugello and Monza|publisher=ACI Sport|date=9 January 2019}}</ref>  The cancelled race from [[Vallelunga Circuit]] was rescheduled and run at [[Autodromo Enzo e Dino Ferrari]] in Round 5 as a fourth race.<ref>{{cite news|last1=Waring|first1=Bethonie|url=http://www.formulascout.com/fregional-and-italian-f4-to-run-postponed-races-at-imola/51364|title=FRegional and Italian F4 to run postponed races at Imola|date=11 June 2019}}</ref>
