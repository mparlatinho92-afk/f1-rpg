The '''2024 Formula Regional European Championship by Alpine''' was a multi-event, [[Formula Regional]] [[Open-wheel car|open-wheel single seater]] [[Motorsport|motor racing]] championship held across Europe. The championship featured a mix of professional and amateur drivers, competing in Formula Regional cars that conform to the [[FIA]] Formula Regional regulations for the championship. This was the sixth season of the championship and the fourth after a merger with [[Formula Renault Eurocup]] which resulted in the change of the engine supplier to [[Automobiles Alpine|Alpine]].

[[Prema Racing]] won the Teams' Championship for the third consecutive year, clinching it at the first race of the penultimate round. One race later and with two races to spare, their driver [[Rafael Câmara]] won the Drivers' Championship. [[Race Performance Motorsport|RPM]] driver [[Noah Strømsted]] won the Rookie Championship at the final race of the season.

=== Team changes ===
[[Arden International|Arden Motorsport]] withdrew from the championship, instead electing to focus on their GB3 and GB4 efforts.<ref name="ArdenExit"/>

Monolite Racing, a mainstay in the series since [[2020 Formula Regional European Championship|2020]], also elected to withdraw after talks with GB3 race winner [[James Hedley]] and [[Jacques Villeneuve]] protégé Kevin Foster did not materialise.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://formulascout.com/monolite-racing-expected-to-withdraw-from-frec/117779|title=Monolite Racing expected to withdraw from FREC|website=Formula Scout|last=Gascoigne|first=Roger|date=17 April 2024|access-date=29 April 2024}}</ref><ref name="MonoliteFS">{{cite web|url=https://feederseries.net/2024/04/29/freca-testing-review-are-prema-and-r-ace-gp-still-the-teams-to-beat/|title=FRECA testing review: Are Prema and R-ace GP still the teams to beat?|website=Feeder Series|last=Wolff|first=Perceval|date=29 April 2024|access-date=29 April 2024}}</ref>

[[Iron Dames]], a female talent development project owned by [[DC Racing Solutions]], entered the championship with two cars.<ref name="IronDames"/> Their cars were operated by [[Prema Racing]].

[[MP Motorsport]] promoted [[Valerio Rinicella]] from their Spanish and UAE F4 outfit after he came third and fourth respectively in 2023.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Allen |first=Peter |date=2023-12-18 |title=Valerio Rinicella stays with MP Motorsport for FRegional step |url=https://formulascout.com/valerio-rinicella-stays-with-mp-motorsport-for-fregional-step/114127 |access-date=2023-12-18 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-GB}}</ref> He replaced [[Sami Meguetounif]], who stepped up to Formula 3 with Trident.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Allen |first=Peter |date=2023-12-14 |title=Trident F3 team signs Sami Meguetounif for 2024 |url=https://formulascout.com/trident-f3-team-signs-sami-meguetounif-for-2024/114067 |access-date=2023-12-22 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-GB}}</ref> The team also recruited [[Nikhil Bohra]], who moved over from Trident after coming 12th with the Italian team in 2023 to fill the seat of the late [[Dilano van 't Hoff]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2024-01-04 |title=MP Motorsport signs Nikhil Bohra for FRegional Europe and Middle East |url=https://formulascout.com/mp-motorsport-signs-nikhil-bohra-for-fregional-europe-and-middle-east/114687 |access-date=2024-01-04 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-GB}}</ref> MP's lineup was completed by [[Nikita Bedrin]], who embarked on the 2024 campaign alongside an FIA F3 campaign with PHM Racing after four guest appearances in 2023 with Monolite and VAR. His dual campaign saw him miss the rounds at Spa, Hungaroring and Paul Ricard to prioritize F3. He replaced Victor Bernier, who joined Martinet by Alméras in the Porsche Supercup.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Bruin |first=Lois de |date=2024-03-30 |title=Bedrin hooks up with MP Motorsport for full FRECA season |url=https://www.mpmotorsport.com/bedrin-hooks-up-with-mp-motorsport-for-full-freca-season/ |access-date=2024-03-30 |website=MP Motorsport |language=en-US |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240330155526/https://www.mpmotorsport.com/bedrin-hooks-up-with-mp-motorsport-for-full-freca-season/ |archive-date=30 March 2024 |url-status=dead}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Hub |first=Porsche Motorsport |title=Introducing team Martinet by Alméras in the Porsche Supercup 2024 {{!}} Porsche Motorsport Hub |url=https://motorsports.porsche.com/international/en/article/2024/05/08/pmsc-2024-team-up-martinet-by-almeras |access-date=2024-05-09 |website=motorsports.porsche.com |language=en}}</ref>

[[Trident Motorsport|Trident]] signed two new drivers to replace Eurocup-3-bound [[Owen Tangavelou]] and MP-bound [[Nikhil Bohra]]. The team recruited [[Alpine Academy]] driver [[Nicola Lacorte]], who stepped up to the category after coming ninth in both the [[2023 Italian F4 Championship|Italian F4]] and the [[2023 Euro 4 Championship|Euro 4 Championship]] in 2023, and [[Ruiqi Liu]], who also contested multiple Formula 4 championships in 2023, culminating in a fourth place in the [[2023 Formula Winter Series|Formula Winter Series]] with [[US Racing]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Nicola Lacorte joins Trident for 2024 Freca |url=https://www.tridentmotorsport.com/en/news/nicola-lacorte-joins-trident-for-2024-freca.html|access-date=2023-12-12 |website=Trident Motorsport |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|title=Trident completes 2024 lineup with Ruiqi Liu|url=https://www.tridentmotorsport.com/en/news/trident-completes-2024-lineup-with-ruiqi-liu.html|access-date=2023-12-22 |website=Trident Motorsport |language=en}}</ref>

[[ART Grand Prix]] promoted two drivers to FIA F3 in [[Laurens van Hoepen]], who remained with their outfit, and [[Charlie Wurz]], who joined Jenzer.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2023-12-12 |title=Laurens van Hoepen to race for ART GP in FIA F3 Championship |url=https://formulascout.com/laurens-van-hoepen-to-race-for-art-gp-in-fia-f3-championship/114020 |access-date=2023-12-22 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-GB}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Press Release 12 / 2023 |url=https://www.jenzermotorsport.ch/en/?view=article&id=1585:press-release-12-2023&catid=47 |access-date=2024-05-09 |website=www.jenzermotorsport.ch}}</ref> Marcus Amand also left the team to join Schumacher CLRT in the [[2024 Porsche Carrera Cup France|Porsche Carrera Cup France]].<ref name=":1">{{Cite instagram |postid=C4OTbEHtc1H |user=schumacher.clrt |title=Super happy to annonce the line up for the @carreracupfrance! |date=7 March 2024 |access-date=23 March 2024}}</ref> [[Alessandro Giusti]] replaced van Hoepen, moving over from G4 Racing after taking three victories and sixth place with the team in his rookie season in 2023.<ref name=":2">{{Cite web |date=2024-01-17 |title=Alessandro Giusti joins ART Grand Prix for FRECA's 2024 season! |url=https://www.art-grandprix.com/en/yaroslav-veselaho-to-join-art-grand-prix-for-2024-freca-season/ |access-date=2024-01-25 |website=ART Grand Prix |language=en-US}}</ref> [[2023 French F4 Championship|2023 French F4]] champion [[Evan Giltaire]] remained with ART after already joining the team as a guest driver for the last two rounds of the 2023 season in place of Wurz.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2024-01-27 |title=Evan Giltaire joins ART Grand Prix for this new FRECA season!|url=https://www.art-grandprix.com/en/evan-giltaire-joins-art-grand-prix-for-this-new-freca-season/|access-date=2024-01-27|website=ART Grand Prix |language=en-US}}</ref> [[F1 Academy]] runner-up [[Léna Bühler]] completed ART's lineup, returning to the championship where she drove for R-ace GP in 2021 and 2022 as part of an agreement allowing FRECA teams to run a fourth car for one of the top three F1 Academy finishers.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2024-01-26 |title=Léna Bühler to represent Sauber Academy in Formula Regional European Championship |url=https://www.sauber-group.com/news/news1 |access-date=2024-01-26 |website=Sauber |language=en-US}}</ref>

[[Saintéloc Racing]] fielded an all-new lineup after [[Lucas Medina]], [[Emerson Fittipaldi Jr.]] and [[Esteban Masson]] left the team, with Fittipaldi Jr. moving to the Eurocup-3, Masson joining Akkodis ASP in the [[FIA World Endurance Championship|World Endurance Championship]] and Medina joining Team Virage in the [[2024 Ligier European Series|Ligier European Series]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Allen |first=Peter |date=2024-01-09 |title=Emmo Fittipaldi Jr joins MP Motorsport for Eurocup-3 and FRMEC |url=https://formulascout.com/emmo-fittipaldi-jr-joins-mp-motorsport-for-eurocup-3-and-frmec/114790 |access-date=2024-05-09 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Akkodis ASP Team presents their two FIA WEC crews |url=https://en.endurance-info.com/auto/article/109813-akkodis-asp-team-presents-their-two-fia-wec-crews |access-date=2023-12-22 |website=Endurance Info |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Team Virage Take Double Win At Barcelona Opener |url=https://www.dailysportscar.com/2024/04/15/team-virage-take-double-win-at-barcelona-opener.html |access-date=2024-05-09 |website=www.dailysportscar.com |date=15 April 2024 }}</ref> The team signed three F4 graduates: [[Matteo De Palo]], who competed in four different Formula 4-level series in 2023 and came fifth in the [[2023 F4 Spanish Championship|Spanish championship]], [[2023 French F4 Championship|French Formula 4]] runner-up [[Enzo Peugeot]] and [[Théophile Naël]], who won the Spanish F4 championship and already completed a FRMEC campaign, both also with Saintéloc.<ref>{{Cite web |date=1 December 2023|title=Nael, de Palo and Peugeot form Sainteloc's all-rookie 2024 FREC line-up|url=https://formulascout.com/nael-de-palo-and-peugeot-join-sainteloc-in-all-rookie-frec-line-up/113752|access-date=1 December 2023 |website=formulascout.com}}</ref>

KIC Motorsport saw their only full-time driver [[Maya Weug]] leave the team to join [[Prema Racing]] in [[2024 F1 Academy season|F1 Academy]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Ferrari junior Maya Weug completes PREMA Racing's 2024 line-up |url=https://www.f1academy.com/ |access-date=2024-04-01 |website=F1® Academy |language=en}}</ref> The team recruited Costa Toparis, who drove for [[Evans GP]] in FRMEC at the start of the year and received technical assistance from the Australian team.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://formulascout.com/costa-toparis-joins-kic-motorsport-in-evans-gp-supported-frec-move/117563|title=Costa Toparis joins KIC Motorsport in Evans GP-supported FREC move|website=Formula Scout|last=Wood|first=Ida|date=2 April 2024|access-date=2 April 2024}}</ref> [[Nandhavud Bhirombhakdi]], who competed in various [[Formula 4]] championships in the previous two years, and [[Alex Sawer]], who came fifth in the inaugural [[2023 F4 Indian Championship|Indian F4 Championship]], joined him.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://formulascout.com/kic-motorsport-completes-its-frec-line-up-with-alex-sawer/117720|title=KIC Motorsport completes its FREC line-up with Alex Sawer|last=Wood |first=Ida|website=Formula Scout|language=en-GB|date=10 April 2024|access-date=10 April 2024}}</ref>'''<ref>{{Cite web |date=2 April 2024 |title=Bhirombhakdi and Pierre join FREC grid with KIC Motorsport and R-P-M|url=https://formulascout.com/bhirombhakdi-and-pierre-join-frec-grid-with-kic-motorsport-and-r-p-m/117570|access-date=3 April 2024|last=Wood |first=Ida|website=Formula Scout|language=en-GB}}</ref>'''

Newcomers [[Iron Dames]] fielded an all-female lineup consisting of [[2023 F1 Academy season|2023 F1 Academy]] champion [[Marta García (racing driver)|Marta García]], who was previously slated to drive a fourth Prema entry,<ref>{{Cite web |date=2023-10-27 |title=Marta Garcia joins PREMA for 2024 Formula Regional European Championship by Alpine |url=https://premaracing.com/en/news/19228-marta-garcia-joins-prema-for-2024-formula-regional-european-championship-by-alpine |access-date=2023-10-26 |website=Prema Racing |language=en}}</ref> and Mercedes junior [[Doriane Pin]], who also competed in F1 Academy with Prema Racing.<ref name="IronDames"/>

=== Mid-season changes ===
Both [[Roman Biliński]] and [[Doriane Pin]] were absent from the third round at Zandvoort as they were recovering from injuries: Pin fractured her ribs in an incident during the Spa round, while Biliński suffered a road accident that saw him get hospitalized.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Doriane Pin forced to withdraw from 92nd 24 Hours of Le Mans |url=https://www.24h-lemans.com/en/news/doriane-pin-forced-to-withdraw-from-92nd-24-hours-of-le-mans-58677 |date=30 May 2024 |access-date=7 June 2024 |website=[[24 Hours of Le Mans]] |publisher=[[Automobile Club de l'Ouest]] |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://formulascout.com/road-accident-rules-bilinski-out-of-frecs-zandvoort-round/119846|title=Road accident rules Bilinski out of FREC's Zandvoort round|website=Formula Scout|last=Wood|first=Ida|date=5 June 2024|access-date=5 June 2024|language=en-GB}}</ref> Biliński later disclosed that he underwent surgery to repair two broken vertebrae, which kept him out of the cockpit for an extended period.<ref>{{Cite web |url=https://formulascout.com/bilinski-reveals-broken-back-diagnosis-forcing-him-out-of-frec-action/120082|title=Bilinski reveals broken back diagnosis forcing him out of FREC action|website=Formula Scout|language=en-US|last=Wood|first=Ida|date=11 June 2024|access-date=11 June 2024}}</ref> KIC Motorsport also altered its lineup, with GB3 race winner [[John Bennett (racing driver)|John Bennett]] called up to replace Costa Toparis.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.italiaracing.net/Zandvoort-libere-1-Camara-si-conferma-leader-/250451/1096|title=Zandvoort, libere 1: Camara si conferma leader|website=Italiaracing.net|last=Costa|first=Massimo|language=it|date=7 June 2024|access-date=7 June 2024}}</ref>

Ahead of the fourth round, KIC Motorsport announced Chinese driver Gao Yujia, 31st in FRMEC in his first Formula Regional appearance, would pilot the team's No. 43 car previously occupied by Toparis and Bennett for the remainder of the season.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Cheng |first=B. E. C. |title=中国车手高宇嘉加盟KIC Motorsport 参与2024赛季FRECA |url=http://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=Mzg2MjgwOTE2Mw==&mid=2247506478&idx=1&sn=54d3a5690cd21178a1d9f8e0d15fc686&chksm=ce00a70af9772e1ca1d5878438ae7a061012c5d1119db274376ffc768a860be4cf06f450d2d4#rd |access-date=2024-06-15 |website=Weixin Official Accounts Platform}}</ref> Trident announced that [[Michael Belov]] would return to the championship to replace the injured Biliński at the Hungaroring, marking the fourth year where he would compete in the series.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Allen |first=Peter |date=2024-06-19 |title=Belov replaces Bilinski in Trident FREC trio |url=https://formulascout.com/belov-replaces-bilinski-in-trident-frec-trio/120276 |access-date=2024-06-19 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> At MP Motorsport, Isaac Barashi stepped in for Bedrin, returning to the team that he came 30th in FRMEC with.<ref>{{Cite web |title=2024 - FRECA - Rd 04 - HUNGARORING Provisional Entry List |url=https://formularegionaleubyalpine.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/2024-FRECA-Rd04-HUNGARORING-02-Provisional-Entry-List.pdf}}</ref>

[[Jesse Carrasquedo Jr.]] left G4 Racing and the championship ahead of round 5 to join [[Campos Racing]] in [[2024 Eurocup-3 season|Eurocup-3]]. He was replaced by [[F4 Brazilian Championship|Brazilian F4]] race-winner [[Álvaro Cho]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2024-07-08 |title=G4 Racing signs Brazilian F4 race-winner Alvaro Cho for FREC |url=https://formulascout.com/g4-racing-signs-brazilian-f4-race-winner-alvaro-cho-for-frec/121207 |access-date=2024-07-08 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Doriane Pin returned from Mugello onwards.

Barashi once again stepped in to replace Bedrin at Paul Ricard.<ref>{{Cite web |title=2024 - FRECA - Rd 06 - PAUL-RICARD: PROVISIONAL ENTRY LIST V1 |url=https://formularegionaleubyalpine.com/corsa/le-castellet-2024/2024-freca-rd06-paul_ricard-02-provisional-entry-list/}}</ref>

Ahead of the round at Imola, [[Léna Bühler]] announced she would end her campaign with ART Grand Prix early. KIC meanwhile announced the return of [[Maya Weug]] for that round in place of Gao Yujia. She had last competed with the team in 2023, before moving to F1 Academy for 2024.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Scout |first=Formula |date=2024-09-04 |title=Buhler leaves ART GP in FREC as Weug returns with KIC Motorsport |url=https://formulascout.com/buhler-leaves-art-gp-in-frec-as-weug-returns-with-kic-motorsport/123378 |access-date=2024-09-04 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Biliński returned after recovering from his injury, while G4 Racing's [[Álvaro Cho]] and KIC's Alex Sawer were absent.<ref>{{Cite web |title=2024 FRECA-Rd07-IMOLA -#02-Provisional Entry List |url=https://formularegionaleubyalpine.com/corsa/imola-2024/2024-freca-rd07-imola-02-provisional-entry-list/}}</ref> Sawer was initially announced to return for the round at Spielberg, but withdrew.

In the days leading up to the penultimate round, G4 Racing announced that FR Americas podium finisher Jett Bowling would join the team for the remainder of the season.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2024-09-23 |title=Jett Bowling joins G4 Racing's FRegional Europe line-up |url=https://formulascout.com/jett-bowling-joins-g4-racings-fregional-europe-line-up/124108 |access-date=2024-09-23 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> KIC also announced a new driver signing for Barcelona in [[Enzo Scionti]], who drove for Monolite Racing in 2023 where he came 31st.<ref>{{Cite web |date=25 September 2024 |title=Feeder Series on X: "DRIVER ANNOUNCEMENT {{!}} 🇺🇸 Enzo Scionti returns to FRECA with KIC Motorsport for this weekend's round at Barcelona!" |url=https://x.com/feeder_series/status/1838932050167857580 |access-date=25 September 2024 |website=X}}</ref>

== Season report ==

=== Opening rounds ===
FRECA’s 2024 season began where 2023 finished: at the [[Hockenheimring]]. [[Prema Racing|Prema]]’s [[Rafael Câmara]] claimed pole position for the opening race of the year.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gascoigne |first=Roger |date=2024-05-11 |title=Camara blitzes pole for first FREC race of 2024 at Hockenheim |url=https://formulascout.com/camara-blitzes-pole-for-first-frec-race-of-2024-at-hockenheim/118692 |access-date=2024-11-04 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> A slow start saw him drop to third behind the [[R-ace GP]] pair of [[Enzo Deligny]] and [[Tuukka Taponen]], but he only needed eight corners to retake the lead. Taponen then took second place from Deligny, before Câmara’s teammate [[James Wharton (racing driver)|James Wharton]] was able to pass both of them and move into second. The top three finished that way, but Wharton was later penalised one position, handing second place back to Taponen.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gascoigne |first=Roger |date=2024-05-11 |title=Camara starts FREC season with victory at Hockenheim |url=https://formulascout.com/camara-starts-frec-season-with-victory-at-hockenheim/118738 |access-date=2024-11-04 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> [[Van Amersfoort Racing|VAR]]’s [[Ivan Domingues]] surprised in qualifying for the second race by taking pole position,<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gascoigne |first=Roger |date=2024-05-12 |title=VAR's Ivan Domingues takes surprise FREC race two pole at Hockenheim |url=https://formulascout.com/vars-ivan-domingues-takes-surprise-frec-race-two-pole-at-hockenheim/118793 |access-date=2024-11-04 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> but Wharton started the race in first after his previously disallowed lap times were reinstated. [[ART Grand Prix|ART]]’s [[Evan Giltaire]] took the lead, while Wharton dropped down, collided with VAR’s [[Pedro Clerot]] and retired. Giltaire held off Câmara through four safety car interruptions to win his first FRECA race, with Domingues third. Câmara ended the first round with a 18-point lead over Giltaire.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gascoigne |first=Roger |date=2024-05-12 |title=Giltaire delivers ART GP its first FREC win since 2022 at Hockenheim |url=https://formulascout.com/giltaire-delivers-art-gp-its-first-frec-win-since-2022-at-hockenheim/118817 |access-date=2024-11-04 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

[[Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps|Spa]] hosted round two, and [[Trident Motorsport|Trident]]’s [[Roman Biliński]] took pole position for the first race in a wet qualifying session.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2024-05-25 |title=Bilinski takes pole, Wharton escapes crash in FREC's wet Spa qualifying |url=https://formulascout.com/bilinski-takes-pole-wharton-escapes-crash-in-frecs-wet-spa-qualifying/119394 |access-date=2024-11-05 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> He survived the first lap in the lead while Câmara, who had started sixth, had already climbed up to fourth. The Brazilian then fought with [[Race Performance Motorsport|RPM]]’s [[Noah Strømsted]] for third, before both were able to pass R-ace GP’s [[Zachary David]] for second. The pair continued forward and demoted Biliński to third, before Câmara took the lead. He held on to win the race, while Strømsted was handed a post-race penalty that dropped him to fourth.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2024-05-25 |title=Camara climbs from sixth to win closely fought FREC opener at Spa |url=https://formulascout.com/camara-climbs-from-sixth-to-win-closely-fought-frec-opener-at-spa/119445 |access-date=2024-11-05 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Câmara went on to take pole position for the second race<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2024-05-26 |title=Camara handles traffic best to comfortably claim FREC pole at Spa |url=https://formulascout.com/camara-handles-traffic-best-to-comfortably-claim-another-frec-pole/119488 |access-date=2024-11-05 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> and began the race fighting off Wharton before Taponen went past both of them into Les Combes. Câmara retook the lead on lap four, with the Finn then dropping behind Strømsted and Wharton, who had a lock-up into La Source earlier that removed him out of contention for the win. With three wins out of four races, Câmara had already gapped his closest challenger, Giltaire, by 60 points in the standings after just two weekends.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2024-05-26 |title=Camara grows colossal FREC points lead with Spa race two win |url=https://formulascout.com/camara-grows-colossal-frec-points-lead-with-spa-race-two-win/119544 |access-date=2024-11-05 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

Next up was [[Circuit Zandvoort|Zandvoort]], where Wharton was fastest in qualifying for race one.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gascoigne |first=Roger |date=2024-06-08 |title=Wharton pips Camara as Prema dominates FREC qualifying at Zandvoort |url=https://formulascout.com/wharton-pips-camara-as-prema-dominates-frec-qualifying-at-zandvoort/119911 |access-date=2024-11-06 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Heavy rain prompted a safety car start, with the first half of the race completed under yellow. When the field was released, Wharton quickly built up a gap before spinning at Hugenholtz and dropping to 16th. That promoted Taponen into the lead ahead of Câmara and ART’s [[Alessandro Giusti]] before the safety car came out again. Taponen was able to hold Câmara behind at the final restart to claim his maiden FRECA win.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Allen |first=Peter |date=2024-06-08 |title=Taponen takes first FREC win at wet Zandvoort after Wharton spin |url=https://formulascout.com/taponen-takes-first-frec-win-at-wet-zandvoort-after-wharton-spin/119956 |access-date=2024-11-06 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Qualifying for the second race was topped by Câmara by over half a second.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2024-06-09 |title=Camara dominates qualifying for FREC's second Zandvoort race |url=https://formulascout.com/camara-dominates-qualifying-for-frecs-second-zandvoort-race/119979 |access-date=2024-11-06 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> VAR’s [[Brando Badoer]] started second, while Giusti fought off Strømsted for third. After an early safety car, the top three gapped the rest of the field and their positions remained unchanged until the end of the race. Taponen tried taking fourth from Strømsted, but found no way past before then losing out to Clerot and dropping to fifth. Still, he now was second in the championship standings, albeit with a massive 73-point disadvantage to runaway leader Câmara.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Lopez |first=Alejandro Alonso |date=2024-06-09 |title=Camara controls race two at Zandvoort for fourth FREC win of 2024 |url=https://formulascout.com/camara-controls-race-two-at-zandvort-for-fourth-frec-win-of-2024/120015 |access-date=2024-11-06 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

=== Mid-season rounds ===
Round four was held at the [[Hungaroring]] and it began in wet conditions with Taponen topping the first qualifying session.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2024-06-22 |title=Taponen takes pole at Hungaroring in soaking wet FREC qualifying |url=https://formulascout.com/taponen-takes-pole-at-hungaroring-in-soaking-wet-frec-qualifying/120392 |access-date=2024-11-07 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> He comfortably controlled the first race to take a lights-to-flag victory, while the other podium places were fiercely contested. David and Badoer were second and third for the opening part of the race, before Badoer attacked David for second place and other cars behind joined the fight. Both managed to stay in their positions, before a post-race penalty for David handed Badoer second place and promoted Câmara onto the podium.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Waring |first=Bethonie |date=2024-06-22 |title=Taponen converts Hungaroring FREC pole to victory |url=https://formulascout.com/taponen-converts-hungaroring-frec-pole-to-victory/120427 |access-date=2024-11-07 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Taponen’s advantage continued onto the next day as he took another pole position.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2024-06-23 |title=Taponen tops the times again in qualifying for FREC race two |url=https://formulascout.com/taponen-tops-the-times-again-in-qualifying-for-frec-race-two/120506 |access-date=2024-11-07 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Prema’s [[Ugo Ugochukwu]] rose past Badoer and Câmara from fourth to second at the start, but ran off track later to allow both of them back past. The positions remained static from thereon, but post-race penalties changed the podium again. Câmara dropped to sixth, allowing Ugochukwu into third. Another faultless victory by Taponen saw him reduce Câmara’s championship lead to 49 points.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Waring |first=Bethonie |date=2024-06-23 |title=Taponen makes it two FREC wins from two at the Hungaroring |url=https://formulascout.com/taponen-makes-it-two-frec-wins-from-two-at-the-hungaroring/120560 |access-date=2024-11-07 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

At [[Mugello Circuit|Mugello]], Taponen secured his third consecutive pole position for the first race.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2024-07-13 |title=R-ace GP's Taponen leads VAR trio in FREC qualifying at Mugello |url=https://formulascout.com/r-ace-gps-taponen-leads-var-trio-in-frec-qualifying-at-mugello/121340 |access-date=2024-11-11 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Badoer started alongside him but was unable to overtake at the start and soon had to defend against Clerot following an early race restart. Despite the pressure, Clerot could not find a way past. Badoer maintained close pursuit of Taponen for several laps before the Finn extended his lead and secured a comfortable victory. Clerot, who initially held third, was overtaken by Domingues on the penultimate lap, while Câmara finished in fifth place.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2024-07-13 |title=Taponen handles the Mugello heat for another FREC win |url=https://formulascout.com/taponen-handles-the-mugello-heat-for-another-frec-win/121374 |access-date=2024-11-11 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Badoer ended Taponen’s pole position streak by setting the fastest time in qualifying for race two.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2024-07-14 |title=Brando Badoer finally gets his first FREC pole by 0.043 seconds |url=https://formulascout.com/brando-badoer-finally-gets-his-first-frec-pole-by-0-043-seconds/121415 |access-date=2024-11-11 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Wharton, starting alongside Badoer, claimed the lead at the first corner, with Câmara following through two corners later. Badoer managed to reclaim second place and pursued Wharton closely but could not mount a challenge, resulting in Wharton’s first FRECA victory. Câmara, initially in third, fell to fifth following a post-race penalty, which promoted Clerot to third and Taponen to fourth, 24 points behind in the championship.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2024-07-14 |title=Wharton wins in FREC for first time in Mugello race two |url=https://formulascout.com/wharton-wins-in-frec-for-first-time-in-mugello-race-two/121450 |access-date=2024-11-11 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

Round six of the championship at [[Circuit Paul Ricard|Paul Ricard]] commenced with Câmara securing pole position for the opening race.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gascoigne |first=Roger |date=2024-07-20 |title=Camara flies to his fourth FREC pole at Paul Ricard |url=https://formulascout.com/camara-flies-to-his-fourth-frec-pole-at-paul-ricard/121683 |access-date=2024-11-12 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> [[Michael Belov]], filling in for the injured Biliński at Trident, lined up beside him on the front row. The race concluded without significant changes at the front, as Câmara clinched his fifth victory of the season, while Badoer maintained third place to achieve his sixth consecutive podium.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gascoigne |first=Roger |date=2024-07-20 |title=Camara dominates at Paul Ricard for fifth FREC win |url=https://formulascout.com/camara-dominates-at-paul-ricard-for-fifth-frec-win/121709 |access-date=2024-11-12 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> In the second qualifying session, held under improving track conditions, Câmara secured pole again by setting his lap time last.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gascoigne |first=Roger |date=2024-07-21 |title=Camara grabs last-second FREC pole at drying Paul Ricard |url=https://formulascout.com/camara-grabs-last-second-frec-pole-at-drying-paul-ricard/121788 |access-date=2024-11-12 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Heavy rain before the second race led to a safety car start. Upon its withdrawal, Giusti executed an impressive restart from the third row, advancing to second place. A spin from Badoer brought the safety car back out, and on the next restart, Giusti moved into the lead. Câmara then fell down the order, ultimately finishing sixth. The race concluded with Strømsted and G4 Racing’s [[Kanato Le]] completing the podium. With Taponen failing to finish, Câmara extended his championship lead to 52 points.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gascoigne |first=Roger |date=2024-07-21 |title=Giusti masters conditions to win wet FREC race two at Paul Ricard |url=https://formulascout.com/giusti-masters-conditions-to-win-wet-frec-race-two-at-paul-ricard/121842 |access-date=2024-11-12 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

In its return to Italy, the championship saw Câmara maintain an unbeaten streak in qualifying, securing pole position for the first race at [[Imola Circuit|Imola]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2024-09-07 |title=Camara on FREC pole at Imola, Ugochukwu's disqualification confirmed |url=https://formulascout.com/camara-on-frec-pole-at-imola-ugochukwus-disqualification-confirmed/123445 |access-date=2024-11-13 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> However, after jumping the start, he received a five-second penalty, while Taponen advanced to second place, overtaking Giusti. Wharton also moved past Giusti before a safety car paused the race. With Taponen and Wharton then fighting over second behind, Câmara managed to extend his lead over the field and crossed the finish line eight seconds ahead, thus overcoming the time penalty.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2024-09-07 |title=Camara overcomes penalty to clinch sixth FREC win of 2024 at Imola |url=https://formulascout.com/camara-overcomes-penalty-to-clinch-sixth-frec-win-of-2024-at-imola/123472 |access-date=2024-11-13 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> In the second race's qualifying, Câmara achieved his fourth consecutive pole position.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2024-09-08 |title=Camara storms to another FREC pole at Imola with late improvements |url=https://formulascout.com/camara-storms-to-another-frec-pole-at-imola-with-late-improvements/123515 |access-date=2024-11-13 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Going into the first corner, Taponen rear-ended Câmara, resulting in the Finn's retirement and Câmara dropping to the back of the field. Giusti seized the lead, with Strømsted and David following, while Wharton later managed to take third during a restart. Giusti claimed victory, and Câmara executed an impressive comeback aided by ten retirements to finish ninth, solidifying a 61-point lead over Taponen.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2024-09-08 |title=Giusti wins and Taponen spins Camara in FREC's second Imola race |url=https://formulascout.com/giusti-wins-and-taponen-spins-camara-in-frecs-second-imola-race/123545 |access-date=2024-11-13 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

=== Closing rounds ===
Wharton interrupted Câmara’s qualifying streak by securing pole position for the first race at the [[Red Bull Ring]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2024-09-14 |title=Wharton pips Giusti to pole by 0.017s for FREC's first Red Bull Ring race |url=https://formulascout.com/wharton-pips-giusti-to-pole-by-0-017s-for-frecs-first-red-bull-ring-race/123768 |access-date=2024-11-18 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Giusti challenged Wharton at the start, but Wharton held onto the lead, with Biliński moving into third place. This order remained unchanged during two safety car restarts, while Giltaire climbed through the field. On lap seven, Giltaire overtook Biliński for third and soon closed in on Giusti to secure second place. Despite him pressuring Wharton, the Australian prevailed and won the race.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2024-09-14 |title=Wharton converts pole into second FREC victory after late Giltaire attack |url=https://formulascout.com/wharton-converts-pole-into-second-frec-victory-after-late-giltaire-attack/123786 |access-date=2024-11-18 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> The following day, the track conditions were very wet, allowing Strømsted to claim pole position.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gascoigne |first=Roger |date=2024-09-15 |title=Stromsted takes FREC pole in heavy rain at the Red Bull Ring |url=https://formulascout.com/stromsted-takes-frec-pole-in-heavy-rain-at-the-red-bull-ring/123849 |access-date=2024-11-18 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> The track then dried for the race, where [[Théophile Naël]] of [[Saintéloc Racing]] took the lead, followed by Strømsted and Badoer. Naël maintained his lead through multiple safety car periods and resisted a late challenge from Strømsted, who ran wide on the penultimate lap, allowing Naël to achieve his first victory. Câmara recorded two non-points finishes, but with Taponen failing to score himself, the points gap remained unchanged.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Allen |first=Peter |date=2024-09-15 |title=Nael beats Stromsted to first win in eventful race for FREC contenders |url=https://formulascout.com/nael-beats-stromsted-to-first-win-in-eventful-race-for-frec-contenders/123891 |access-date=2024-11-18 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

At the penultimate round in [[Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya|Barcelona]], Wharton secured pole position for the first race.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2024-09-28 |title=Wharton pips Camara to FREC race one pole at Barcelona by 0.01s |url=https://formulascout.com/wharton-pips-camara-to-frec-race-one-pole-at-barcelona-by-0-01s/124308 |access-date=2024-11-19 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Câmara, starting alongside him, had a bad start and fell behind Taponen, enabling Wharton to establish an early lead. The Australian extended his advantage as Taponen defended second place from Câmara. The order at the front remained unchanged, with Wharton claiming victory by over six seconds, while Taponen's second-place finish kept his championship hopes alive for the time being.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Lopez |first=Alejandro Alonso |date=2024-09-28 |title=James Wharton dominates FREC race one at Barcelona |url=https://formulascout.com/james-wharton-dominates-frecs-race-one-at-barcelona/124341 |access-date=2024-11-19 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> In the second race, Wharton once again started from pole,<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2024-09-29 |title=Wharton doubles up in qualifying for FREC race two |url=https://formulascout.com/wharton-doubles-up-in-qualifying-for-frec-race-two/124367 |access-date=2024-11-19 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> and an early safety car following a crash allowed him to maintain his lead. Taponen retired in the opening incident, ensuring Câmara's championship win. He had a quiet race, finishing fourth, while Wharton converted his second pole into another victory. Giusti and Clerot battled for second place behind, with the Frenchman successfully holding the Brazilian behind. Wharton’s two wins moved him ahead of Taponen in the standings, 60 points adrift of champion-elect Câmara.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2024-09-29 |title=Camara clinches FREC crown as Wharton wins again at Barcelona |url=https://formulascout.com/camara-clinches-frec-crown-as-wharton-wins-again-at-barcelona/124389 |access-date=2024-11-19 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

The 2024 racing season concluded with a round at [[Monza Circuit]], where Ugochukwu set the fastest qualifying time on a wet track. The first race, also in the wet, began behind the safety car. Ugochukwu quickly created a gap to Biliński, who came under pressure from Câmara. On lap four, Câmara overtook Biliński and began chasing Ugochukwu, while a multi-car battle for third place unfolded. Wharton emerged victorious in that fight, securing third place, as Câmara closed in on Ugochukwu but was unable to prevent him from claiming his maiden win.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gascoigne |first=Roger |date=2024-10-26 |title=Ugochukwu holds off Camara for maiden FRegional Europe win |url=https://formulascout.com/ugochukwu-holds-off-camara-for-maiden-fregional-europe-win/125122 |access-date=2024-11-21 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> The final race of the season saw Naël on pole,<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gascoigne |first=Roger |date=2024-10-27 |title=Nael snatches final FREC pole of season from Camara at Monza |url=https://formulascout.com/nael-snatches-final-frec-pole-of-season-from-camara-at-monza/125140 |access-date=2024-11-21 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> but Câmara, starting third, made a strong start and took the lead. Giusti and Ugochukwu overtook Naël in the following laps before two safety car periods disrupted the race. After the first restart, Ugochukwu made a mistake that dropped him down the order, allowing Wharton to claim third place. An incident involving two simultaneous crashes led to a red flag, and the race was not resumed, securing Câmara his seventh win of the year.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gascoigne |first=Roger |date=2024-10-27 |title=Camara rounds off FREC season with win in red-flagged Monza finale |url=https://formulascout.com/camara-rounds-off-frec-season-with-win-in-red-flagged-monza-finale/125142 |access-date=2024-11-21 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

At the conclusion of the season, Câmara secured the championship with seven victories, 309 points, and a 73-point lead over his nearest rival—records since the series adopted a two-race weekend format in 2021. Despite strong mid-season performances from Taponen and a late-season surge by Wharton, who scored the most points in the final four rounds,<ref>{{Cite web |last=Brusa |first=Francesca |date=2024-11-13 |title=Why patience was the key to James Wharton's late-season FR Europe surge |url=https://feederseries.net/2024/11/13/why-patience-was-the-key-to-james-whartons-late-season-fr-europe-surge/ |access-date=2024-11-21 |website=Feeder Series |language=en}}</ref> Câmara's dominance was rarely in question. He established a commanding lead early in the campaign and maintained consistent results, including multiple wins and podium finishes, to decisively outpace his competitors. His only significant setback occurred at the Red Bull Ring, but with Taponen unable to capitalize and Wharton’s rise coming too late, Câmara secured the title in the penultimate round. Meanwhile, the championship's appeal remained strong despite the departure of two teams and continued growth of other series such as Eurocup-3 and GB3, with grid sizes consistently exceeding 30 entries.
