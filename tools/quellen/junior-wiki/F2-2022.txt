The '''2022 FIA Formula 2 Championship''' was a [[motor racing]] championship for [[Formula Two|Formula 2]] cars that was sanctioned by the [[Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile]] (FIA). The championship was the fifty-sixth season of Formula 2 racing and the sixth season ran under the [[FIA Formula 2 Championship]] moniker. It was an [[open-wheel racing]] category that served as the second tier of [[formula racing]] in the [[FIA Global Pathway]]. The category ran in support of selected rounds of the [[2022 Formula One World Championship|2022 FIA Formula One World Championship]]. As the championship was a [[spec series]], all teams and drivers competing in the championship ran the same car, the [[Dallara F2 2018]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.autosport.com/f2/news/131537/new-f2-car-revealed-to-feature-halo-device|title=New F2 car for 2018 revealed, featuring halo head protection device|last=Kalinauckas|first=Alex|website=[[autosport]].com|publisher=[[Motorsport Network]]|language=en|access-date=25 August 2019|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170901211431/https://www.autosport.com/f2/news/131537/new-f2-car-revealed-to-feature-halo-device|archive-date=1 September 2017|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref name="formula1.com">{{cite web |title=8 key questions on Formula 2 and Formula 3's new cost-cutting measures answered |url=https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article.8-key-questions-on-formula-2-and-formula-3s-new-cost-cutting-measures.4i9Z2CoPtJ6RMxRyh06e27.html |website=formula1.com |access-date=1 August 2021 |language=en | first=Greg | last=Stuart | date=6 November 2020}}</ref>

Prema Racing entered the championship as the reigning teams' champion, having secured their title at the penultimate race of the 2021 season in Saudi Arabia.

[[Felipe Drugovich]], driving for [[MP Motorsport]], took the drivers' championship at Monza, with three races to spare. MP Motorsport won the teams' championship at Abu Dhabi.

The season would see twelve different race winners, equalling the record previously set two years prior: champion Drugovich won five races (in [[Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya|Barcelona]] he won both races), and four races were won by [[Liam Lawson]]. Another three victories went to [[Théo Pourchaire]], [[Jack Doohan (racing driver)|Jack Doohan]] and [[Marcus Armstrong]]. [[Ayumu Iwasa]], [[Dennis Hauger]] and [[Logan Sargeant]] each scored two race wins. [[Jüri Vips]], [[Frederik Vesti]], [[Jehan Daruvala]] and [[Richard Verschoor]] each scored one victory during this season.

=== Team changes ===
After having ended their commitment in [[FIA Formula 3 Championship|FIA Formula 3]] beforehand, [[HWA Racelab]] ended their Formula 2 operations at the end of the 2021 season.<ref>{{Cite web|last=Scout|first=Formula|date=2021-10-14|title=HWA Racelab to leave Formula 2 at end of season|url=https://formulascout.com/hwa-racelab-to-leave-formula-2-at-end-of-season/85237|access-date=2021-10-16|website=Formula Scout|language=en-GB}}</ref> [[Van Amersfoort Racing]] joined the series, taking over their entry.<ref>{{Cite web|date=2021-12-13|title=Van Amersfoort Racing take over HWA RACELAB's entry for 2022|url=https://www.fiaformula2.com/Latest/ioKPIP7ruv3BLc5qcXXHc/van-amersfoort-racing-take-over-hwa-racelabs-entry-for-2022|access-date=2021-12-13|website=Formula 2|language=en-UK}}</ref>

[[Virtuosi Racing]] changed their name, having run with their sponsor UNI since 2019.

=== Driver changes ===
[[Prema Racing]] competed with a new driver lineup; reigning Formula 2 champion [[Oscar Piastri]] signed as a reserve driver for [[Alpine F1 Team]] and his teammate, [[Robert Shwartzman]], exited after two years in the series to become a test driver for [[Scuderia Ferrari]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.motorsport.com/f1/news/piastri-braced-for-no-racing-in-2022-to-focus-on-alpine-f1-reserve-role/6794586/|title=Piastri braced for no racing in 2022 to focus on Alpine F1 reserve role|website=motorsport.com|first1=Luke|last1=Smith|first2=Adam|last2=Cooper|date=18 November 2021|access-date=18 November 2021}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://formulascout.com/shwartzman-to-exit-f2-after-2021-but-future-still-unknown/87412|title=Shwartzman to exit F2 after 2021 but future still "unknown"|website=formulascout.com|first=Peter|last=Allen|date=9 December 2021|access-date=9 December 2021}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.ferrari.com/en-EN/articles/giovinazzi-and-schumacher-reserve-drivers-for-the-scuderia-shwartzman-test-driver|title=Giovinazzi and Schumacher reserve drivers for the Scuderia, Shwartzman test driver|website=[[Ferrari]]|date=2021-12-22|access-date=2021-12-25|archive-date=25 December 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211225160054/https://www.ferrari.com/en-EN/articles/giovinazzi-and-schumacher-reserve-drivers-for-the-scuderia-shwartzman-test-driver|url-status=dead}}</ref> They were replaced by [[2021 FIA Formula 3 Championship|reigning FIA Formula 3]] champion [[Dennis Hauger]] and fellow [[Red Bull Junior Team]] driver [[Jehan Daruvala]], who switched from [[Carlin Motorsport|Carlin]].<ref>{{Cite web|last=Wood|first=Ida|date=2022-01-13|title=Prema partners F3 champion Hauger with returning Daruvala in F2|url=https://formulascout.com/prema-partners-f3-champion-hauger-with-returning-daruvala-in-f2/88573|access-date=2022-01-14|website=Formula Scout|language=en-GB}}</ref>

[[Virtuosi Racing]]'s [[Zhou Guanyu]] left the team and the series after three years to graduate to [[2022 Formula One World Championship|Formula One]] with [[Alfa Romeo in Formula One|Alfa Romeo Racing]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article.breaking-alfa-romeo-announce-guanyu-zhou-as-valtteri-bottass-team-mate-for.3FGtpRMOhG1qZ4Qinr8ky6.html|title=Alfa Romeo announce Guanyu Zhou as Valtteri Bottas's team mate for 2022|website=formula1.com|date=16 November 2021|access-date=17 November 2021}}</ref> [[Felipe Drugovich]] also left the team to rejoin [[MP Motorsport]], with whom he had raced in {{F2|2020}}. Their seats were filled by [[Marino Sato]], who switched from [[Trident Racing|Trident]], and FIA Formula 3 runner-up and new [[Alpine Academy]] driver [[Jack Doohan (racing driver)|Jack Doohan]], who contested the final two rounds of Formula 2 in the previous season with MP Motorsport.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Doohan and Sato complete new-look Virtuosi line-up for 2022|url=https://www.fiaformula2.com/Latest/5PlJhEm4NxnKby8W3jgD2T/doohan-and-sato-complete-new-look-virtuosi-line-up-for-2022|access-date=2021-12-14|website=FIA_F2 - The Official F2 Website|language=en}}</ref>

[[Carlin Motorsport|Carlin]] driver [[Dan Ticktum]] left the series to join [[NIO 333 FE Team|NIO 333]] in [[2021–22 Formula E World Championship|Formula E]].<ref name="FE2022">{{Cite web|date=2021-11-25|title=Dan Ticktum joins NIO 333 for 2022 Formula E switch|url=https://www.motorsport.com/formula-e/news/ticktum-joins-nio-333-for-2022-formula-e-switch/6818675/|access-date=2021-11-25|website=[[Motorsport.com]]|language=en}}</ref> Carlin hired Red Bull junior and former [[Hitech Grand Prix]] driver [[Liam Lawson]] alongside FIA Formula 3 graduate and [[Williams Driver Academy]] member [[Logan Sargeant]] – who contested the penultimate round of Formula 2 in 2021 with [[HWA Racelab]] – to replace Ticktum and the departing Jehan Daruvala.<ref>{{Cite web|last=Wood|first=Ida|date=2022-01-14|title=Lawson leaves for Carlin, Vips stays at Hitech in F2|url=https://formulascout.com/lawson-leaves-for-carlin-vips-stays-at-hitech-in-f2/88590|access-date=2022-01-14|website=Formula Scout|language=en-GB}}</ref>
<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.carlin.co.uk/news/sargeant-joins-carlin-for-2022-fia-formula-2-championship|title=Sargeant joins Carlin for 2022 FIA Formula 2 Championship|website=Carlin|date=13 December 2021|access-date=13 December 2021|archive-date=13 December 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211213145430/https://www.carlin.co.uk/news/sargeant-joins-carlin-for-2022-fia-formula-2-championship|url-status=dead}}</ref>

[[Hitech Grand Prix]] signed DAMS departee [[Marcus Armstrong]], who left the [[Ferrari Driver Academy]], to replace [[Liam Lawson]].<ref>{{Cite web|last=Wood|first=Ida|date=2022-01-19|title=Armstrong keeps himself in F2 by finding Hitech seat|url=https://formulascout.com/armstrong-keeps-himself-in-f2-by-finding-hitech-seat/88780|access-date=2022-01-19|website=Formula Scout|language=en-GB}}</ref>

[[ART Grand Prix]] driver [[Christian Lundgaard]] left the team and the series after two seasons to join the [[2022 IndyCar Series|IndyCar Series]], racing for [[Rahal Letterman Lanigan Racing]].<ref>{{Cite web|date=2021-10-20|title=Christian Lundgaard to Join Rahal Letterman Lanigan Racing in a Multi-Year Agreement|url=https://rahal.com/2021/10/20/christian-lundgaard-to-join-rahal-letterman-lanigan-racing-in-a-multi-year-agreement/|access-date=2021-10-20|website=Rahal Letterman Lanigan|language=en-US|archive-date=20 October 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211020124819/https://rahal.com/2021/10/20/christian-lundgaard-to-join-rahal-letterman-lanigan-racing-in-a-multi-year-agreement/|url-status=dead}}</ref> He was replaced by FIA Formula 3 graduate and [[Mercedes Junior Team]] member [[Frederik Vesti]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.fiaformula2.com/Latest/3Ji4Wr3DaGP9JIyyngRVpw/art-grand-prix-promote-vesti-to-f2-for-2022|title=ART Grand Prix promote Vesti to F2 for 2022|website=[[FIA Formula 2 Championship]]|date=21 January 2022|access-date=21 January 2022}}</ref>

[[MP Motorsport]] partnered Felipe Drugovich with [[Clément Novalak]], who replaced [[Lirim Zendeli]] at the team for the final two rounds in 2021 after finishing third in FIA Formula 3.<ref>{{Cite web|date=2021-11-03|title=MP Motorsport announce Novalak for final two rounds of 2021 and full 2022 season|url=https://www.fiaformula2.com/Latest/3A0Y1T10FlMczxftIAQR7z/mp-motorsport-announce-novalak-for-final-two-rounds-of-2021-and-full-2022/|access-date=2021-11-03|website=Formula 2|language=en-UK}}</ref>

[[Campos Racing]] hired new [[Alpine Academy]] member [[Olli Caldwell]], who already competed with the team in the last two rounds of the 2021 season, on a full-time contract.<ref>{{Cite web|date=2022-02-14|title=Campos Racing sign new Alpine junior Caldwell for 2022|url=https://www.fiaformula2.com/Latest/4FvlVYemH90mlNC18rDo1U/campos-racing-sign-new-alpine-junior-caldwell-for-2022|access-date=2022-02-14|website=Formula 2|language=en-UK}}</ref>

[[DAMS]] signed Red Bull junior and FIA Formula 3 graduate [[Ayumu Iwasa]], who joined [[Roy Nissany]] and replaced the departing Marcus Armstrong.<ref>{{Cite web|date=2022-01-14|title=Honda's F1 protege Iwasa moved up to F2 with DAMS|url=https://formulascout.com/hondas-f1-protege-iwasa-moved-up-to-f2-with-dams/88575|access-date=2022-01-14|website=Formula Scout|language=en-GB}}</ref>

[[Trident Racing|Trident]] hired FIA Formula 3 graduate [[Calan Williams]] and [[Richard Verschoor]], who had competed in the previous Formula 2 season with [[MP Motorsport]] and [[Charouz Racing System]]. They replaced Virtuosi-bound Marino Sato and [[Bent Viscaal]], who left the championship to drive in the [[2022 European Le Mans Series|European Le Mans Series]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Australian driver Calan Williams will drive for Trident Motorsport in 2022 FIA Formula 2 Championship|url=https://www.tridentmotorsport.com/en/news/australian-driver-calan-williams-will-drive-for-trident-motorsport-in-the-2022-fia-formula-2-championship.html|access-date=2022-01-13|website=tridentmotorsport.com|language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|date=2022-02-22|title=F2 grid complete for 2022 as Trident sign Dutch racer Verschoor|url=https://www.fiaformula2.com/Latest/GocoU8cidwJxmUCtcFAlX/f2-grid-complete-for-2022-as-trident-sign-dutch-racer-verschoor|access-date=2022-02-22|website=Formula 2|language=en-UK}}</ref>

[[Charouz Racing System]] signed former [[Formula One Esports Series|Formula One Esports]] competitor and [[2021 Euroformula Open Championship|Euroformula Open]] graduate [[Cem Bölükbaşı]] to replace [[Guilherme Samaia]] alongside [[Enzo Fittipaldi]].<ref>{{Cite web|date=2022-01-12|title=Bölükbasi joins Charouz Racing System for maiden F2 campaign|url=https://www.fiaformula2.com/Latest/6mitzeFO2Bsrro7ovdXqf/boeluekbasi-joins-charouz-racing-system-for-maiden-f2-campaign|access-date=2022-01-12|website=Formula 2|language=en-UK}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|date=2022-02-16|title=Charouz Racing System retain Fittipaldi for 2022|url=https://www.fiaformula2.com/Latest/N69wAX1qkPqbn50e38cFF/charouz-racing-system-retain-fittipaldi-for-2022|access-date=2022-02-16|website=Formula 2|language=en-UK}}</ref>

New entrant [[Van Amersfoort Racing]] signed [[Jake Hughes]], who joined the category full-time after making temporary appearances with HWA Racelab in {{F2|2020}} and {{F2|2021}}.<ref>{{Cite web|last=Wood|first=Ida|date=2022-01-17|title=Jake Hughes lands full-time F2 seat with Van Amersfoort Racing|url=https://formulascout.com/jake-hughes-lands-full-time-f2-seat-with-van-amersfoort-racing/88720|access-date=2022-01-17|website=Formula Scout|language=en-GB}}</ref> He was partnered by [[Amaury Cordeel]], graduating from FIA Formula 3 after a single season in which he came 23rd.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Van Amersfoort Racing {{!}} VAR announces Amaury Cordeel for FIA F2 Championship 2022|url=http://www.vanamersfoortracing.nl/fia-f2/var-announces-amaury-cordeel-for-2022-fia-f2-championship.htm|access-date=2022-01-20|website=www.vanamersfoortracing.nl}}</ref>

==== Mid-season changes ====
Charouz Racing System driver Cem Bölükbaşı was ruled out of the third round at the [[Imola Circuit]] with a fractured rib.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://formulascout.com/bolukbasi-to-miss-f2s-imola-round-through-injury/92396|title=Bolukbasi to miss F2's Imola round through injury|website=Formula Scout|last=Wood|first=Ida|date=19 April 2022|access-date=20 April 2022}}</ref> He was replaced by [[David Beckmann]], who previously raced for the team during the first half of the {{F2|2021}} championship.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://formulascout.com/beckmann-replaces-bolukbasi-at-charouz-for-f2s-imola-round/92428|title=Beckmann replaces Bolukbasi at Charouz in F2 for Imola|website=Formula Scout|last=Wood|first=Ida|date=20 April 2022|access-date=20 April 2022}}</ref>

Bölükbaşı returned to Charouz for the fourth round at the [[Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://formulascout.com/fit-again-bolukbasi-to-return-to-charouz-f2-cockpit-in-barcelona/93884|title=Fit again Bolukbasi to return to Charouz F2 cockpit in Barcelona|website=Formula Scout|first=Roger|last=Gascoigne|date=18 May 2022|access-date=18 May 2022}}</ref>

Van Amersfoort Racing driver Amaury Cordeel was suspended for the seventh round at Silverstone after incurring 12 penalty points across the first six rounds,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.fia.com/sites/default/files/decision-document/2022%20Baku%20Event%20-%20OFFENCE%20-%20Car%2025%20-%20breach%20of%20Article%2039.3%20(Driver%20Penalty%20Points).pdf|title=OFFENCE - Car 25 - breach of Article 39.3 (Driver Penalty Points)|website=FIA|date=12 June 2022|access-date=12 June 2022}}</ref> the first such ban since [[Mahaveer Raghunathan]] in 2019. He was replaced by David Beckmann, who earlier in the season had filled in for the injured Bölükbaşı at Charouz.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2022-06-30 |title=Beckmann substitutes for VAR's suspended Cordeel at Silverstone |url=https://formulascout.com/beckmann-substitutes-for-vars-suspended-cordeel-at-silverstone/95582 |access-date=2022-06-30 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-GB}}</ref>

[[Roberto Merhi]] joined the championship at the Red Bull Ring to replace Ralph Boschung after the latter had withdrawn from multiple rounds due to persisting neck injuries. Merhi had last competed in 2018, driving for Campos Racing and MP Motorsport.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Allen |first=Peter |date=2022-07-06 |title=Merhi makes another F2 comeback in place of Boschung at Campos |url=https://formulascout.com/merhi-makes-another-f2-comeback-in-place-of-boschung-at-campos/95948 |access-date=2022-07-06 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-GB}}</ref>

Jake Hughes was forced to miss the ninth and tenth rounds due to contracting COVID-19, with David Beckmann again joining Van Amersfoort Racing to replace him.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2022-07-18 |title=Beckmann back in at VAR in F2 as Hughes catches COVID-19 |url=https://formulascout.com/beckmann-back-in-at-var-in-f2-as-hughes-catches-covid-19/96401 |access-date=2022-07-18 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-GB}}</ref> In order to prioritise his [[Formula E]] commitments, Hughes did not return to the series and Beckmann replaced him up until the season finale.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2022-08-23 |title=Hughes leaves VAR in F2, Beckmann get his seat for rest of season |url=https://formulascout.com/hughes-leaves-f2-beckmann-get-his-var-seat-for-rest-of-season/97748 |access-date=2022-08-23 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-GB}}</ref>

Campos Racing driver Olli Caldwell was the second driver to receive a race ban for incurring 12 penalty points and was suspended from the eleventh round at Spa-Francorchamps. He was replaced by Lirim Zendeli, who last raced in 2021 for MP Motorsport.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://formulascout.com/alpine-junior-caldwell-picks-up-f2-ban-for-spa/97029|title=Alpine junior Caldwell picks up F2 ban for Spa|website=Formula Scout|last=Allen|first=Peter|date=31 July 2022|access-date=31 July 2022}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=2022-08-22 |title=Zendeli to replace suspended Alpine junior Caldwell |url=https://www.motorsportweek.com/2022/08/22/zendeli-to-replace-suspended-alpine-junior-caldwell/ |access-date=2022-08-22 |website=Motorsport Week |language=en-GB}}</ref> Zendeli partnered Ralph Boschung, who returned to the series after his injury. Boschung's replacement Roberto Merhi returned to the [[2022 Super GT Series|Super GT Series]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.autosport.com/formula2/news/boschung-to-attempt-fia-formula-2-return-in-belgium/10356833/|title=Boschung to attempt FIA Formula 2 return in Belgium|website=[[Autosport]]|last=White|first=Megan|date=2022-08-23|access-date=2022-08-23}}</ref> Cem Bölükbaşı terminated his contract with Charouz Racing System by mutual consent prior to the Spa-Francorchamps round, his seat being taken by [[IndyCar Series]] racer [[Tatiana Calderón]], who returned to Formula 2 after competing for [[Arden International|BWT Arden]] in [[2019 Formula 2 Championship|2019]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.italiaracing.net/Tatiana-Calderon-ritorna-in-F2-rimpiazza-Bolukbasi-nel-team-Charouz/246587/4|title=Tatiana Calderon ritorna in F2: rimpiazza Bolukbasi nel team Charouz|website=italiaracing.net|last=Rubino|first=Jacopo|language=it|date=2022-08-24|access-date=2022-08-24}}</ref>

DAMS racer Roy Nissany became the third driver of 2022 to receive a race suspension for incurring 12 penalty points and was excluded from the thirteenth round at Monza.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://formulascout.com/nissany-banned-from-monza-f2-round-after-safety-car-clash/98229|title=Nissany banned from Monza F2 round after safety car clash|website=formulascout.com|first=Peter|last=Allen|date=4 September 2022|access-date=4 September 2022}}</ref> He was replaced by [[Luca Ghiotto]], who made his first F2 appearance since the end of the {{F2|2020}} season.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.motorsport.com/fia-f2/news/f2-veteran-ghiotto-makes-return-at-monza-replacing-nissany-at-dams/10365142/|title=F2 veteran Ghiotto makes return at Monza replacing Nissany at DAMS|website=motorsport.com|first=James|last=Newbold|date=7 September 2022|access-date=7 September 2022}}</ref>

Trident driver Calan Williams parted ways with his team before the Yas Marina season finale.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Trident and Williams part ways ahead of Abu Dhabi season finale|date=20 October 2022|url=https://www.fiaformula2.com/Latest/5dYnh0rGTyiaVubVniL3aU/trident-and-williams-part-ways-ahead-of-abu-dhabi-season-finale|access-date=20 October 2022|website=FIA_F2 - The Official F2 Website|language=en}}</ref> He was replaced by [[2022 FIA Formula 3 Championship|2022 FIA Formula 3]] Vice-champion [[Zane Maloney]], who made his championship debut.<ref>{{Cite web |last=MotorSport |first=Trident |title=Zane Maloney will compete in the last round of the Yas Marina season with Trident Motorsport |url=https://www.tridentmotorsport.com/en/news/zane-maloney-will-compete-in-the-last-round-of-the-yas-marina-season-with-trident-motorsport.html |access-date=2022-11-07 |website=Trident Motorsport |language=it}}</ref> [[Juan Manuel Correa]] returned to Formula 2 for the first time since his accident in 2019 to replace Beckmann at Van Amersfoort Racing.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Van Amersfoort Racing confirms Juan Manuel Correa for Yas Marina F2 finale |url=https://www.fiaformula2.com/ |access-date=2022-11-17 |website=FIA_F2 - The Official F2 Website |language=en}}</ref>

=== Calendar changes ===
The championship returned to a two-race format for its race weekends, after coming under criticism during the 2021 season that the gaps between the rounds were far too large.<ref>{{Cite web|date=2021-07-28|title=F2 boss wary of calendar gaps and 'complicated' weekend format|url=https://www.motorsportweek.com/2021/07/28/f2-boss-wary-of-calendar-gaps-and-complicated-weekend-format/|access-date=2022-02-15|website=Motorsport Week|language=en-GB}}</ref>

*The [[Imola Circuit]] featured on the calendar; the first time it has appeared on the F2 schedule.
*The [[Circuit Zandvoort]] also featured on the calendar after it was cancelled in 2020 and not included in 2021.
*The [[Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya|Barcelona]], [[Red Bull Ring]], [[Hungaroring]] and [[Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps|Spa-Francorchamps]] rounds returned after being initially dropped in 2021.
*The [[Sochi Autodrom]] was due to host a round on 24–25 September, but it was suspended in late February due to the ongoing [[2022 Russian invasion of Ukraine|Russian invasion of Ukraine]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.fiaformula2.com/Latest/6uWVmURiwWv6SOgesJa1Uw/formula-1-statement-on-the-russian-grand-prix|title=Formula 1 statement on the Russian Grand Prix|website=FIAFormula2.com|date=25 February 2022|access-date=26 February 2022}}</ref> It was eventually cancelled the following week along with the [[Russian Grand Prix]].<ref>{{cite web |title=FIA announces World Motor Sport Council decisions in relation to the situation in Ukraine |url=https://www.fia.com/news/fia-announces-world-motor-sport-council-decisions-relation-situation-ukraine |publisher=[[Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile]] |date=1 March 2022 |access-date=1 March 2022}}</ref> On the 18th May 2022, it was announced that a round at the [[Circuit Paul Ricard]], in support of the [[2022 French Grand Prix|French Grand Prix]], would replace the races at Sochi and would take place a week before the [[2022 Budapest Formula 2 round|Hungaroring round]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.fiaformula2.com/Latest/76H5tZrfMxijughydYNMGV/le-castellet-added-to-the-2022-fia-formula-2-calendar|title=Le Castellet added to the 2022 FIA Formula 2 calendar|website=FIAFormula2.com|date=18 May 2022|access-date=18 May 2022}}</ref>

==Regulation changes==

=== Sporting changes ===
The number of races per round was reduced from three to two, with a 120&nbsp;km/45 minute sprint race on Saturday and a 170&nbsp;km/60 minute feature race with a mandatory pit stop on Sunday. Starting grids for both races were decided in one qualifying session on Friday, with the top 10 drivers having their positions reversed for the sprint race.<ref>{{Cite web |title=FIA Formula 2 and FIA Formula 3 to change race weekend format in 2022 |url=https://www.fiaformula2.com/ |access-date=2022-07-02 |website=FIA_F2 - The Official F2 Website |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Formula 2 |url=https://www.fiaformula2.com/ |access-date=2022-07-02 |website=FIA_F2 - The Official F2 Website |language=en}}</ref> The championship received a reprofiled scoring format. The points for pole position and fastest lap were each cut in half; now awarding 2 points for pole, and 1 for the fastest lap in each race. No points were awarded for scoring reverse-grid pole, i.e. finishing 10th in qualifying. The sprint race points were changed significantly, with 10 points being awarded for the winner, 8 points for 2nd, then 6 for 3rd, and it descends all the way to 1 point for 8th. Changes were also applied for Formula 1's other feeder series, the [[FIA Formula 3 Championship]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.fiaformula2.com/Latest/4SPGqhb2NkAR1QRQkJvfQ7/fia-formula-2-and-fia-formula-3-announce-updated-points-allocations-for-2022|title = FIA Formula 2 and FIA Formula 3 announce updated points allocations for 2022}}</ref>

==Season report==
===Round 1: Bahrain===
{{Further|2022 Sakhir Formula 2 round}}
The season began at the [[Bahrain International Circuit]], where [[Jack Doohan (racing driver)|Jack Doohan]] set the fastest time in qualifying to take [[pole position]] for the feature race.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.fia.com/sites/default/files/decision-document/2022%20Sakhir%20Event%20-%20F2%20Qualifying%20Session%20-%20final%20classification.pdf|title=2022 Sakhir Event - F2 Qualifying Session Final Classification|date=18 March 2022|access-date=19 March 2022}}</ref> Tenth-place qualifier [[Felipe Drugovich]] started the sprint race from reverse-grid pole position,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.fia.com/sites/default/files/decision-document/2022%20Sakhir%20Event%20-%20F2%20Sprint%20Race%20-%20provisional%20starting%20grid.pdf|title=2022 Sakhir Event - F2 Sprint Race Provisional Starting Grid|date=18 March 2022|access-date=19 March 2022}}</ref> but dropped places at the start, allowing [[Richard Verschoor]] to take the lead. The race was briefly interrupted by a [[safety car]] as [[Marcus Armstrong]] stalled on track after being spun around by [[Jake Hughes]]. Verschoor maintained his lead to take [[Trident Racing|Trident]]'s first FIA Formula 2 race victory.

Pole-sitter Doohan lost the lead of the feature race to [[Jüri Vips]] at the start, but a slow pit stop later caused Vips to lose places. As Doohan left the pits after his stop, his front wing clipped [[Théo Pourchaire]] and broke, forcing him to stop again for a replacement. Pourchaire took the lead after every driver had pitted. A late-race safety car caused by a collision between Verschoor and [[Enzo Fittipaldi]] prompted some drivers to make extra pit stops. [[Dennis Hauger]] and [[Calan Williams]] were prematurely released from their pit boxes, causing both drivers to have a wheel detach and roll down the pit lane. Pourchaire held his lead at the restart to claim victory and the lead of the championship after the first round, one point ahead of [[Liam Lawson]].

===Round 2: Saudi Arabia===
{{Further|2022 Jeddah Formula 2 round}}
[[Felipe Drugovich]] took feature race pole position at the [[Jeddah Corniche Circuit]], whilst an engine failure for championship leader Pourchaire caused him to qualify 21st. Dennis Hauger started the sprint race from pole position. The race was twice interrupted by a safety car, firstly after [[Amaury Cordeel]] crashed and secondly when Jack Doohan and [[Logan Sargeant]] collided as the race restarted. Drivers were initially ordered to follow the safety car through the pit lane, however the pit lane was closed shortly after as the two stricken cars were located at the pit entry. Amidst the confusion, race leader Hauger was the only driver to enter the pits, losing his lead and later receiving a penalty for entering the pit lane when closed. Calan Williams led the race at the restart but was soon passed by Jake Hughes, who was himself then overtaken by Liam Lawson for the race victory.

Championship leader Lawson was running in third place in the feature race before the pit stops, but a loose wheel forced him into retirement. Drugovich won the race from pole position to take his fourth Formula 2 race win. [[Jehan Daruvala]] finished on the podium having started the race fourteenth. At the conclusion of the round, Drugovich led the drivers' championship by 11 points over Lawson.

===Round 3: Italy (Imola)===
{{Further|2022 Imola Formula 2 round}}
Jüri Vips set the fastest qualifying time at the [[Imola Circuit]] and Logan Sargeant took pole position for the sprint race. A poor start from Sargeant dropped him to fifth at the first corner, allowing Marcus Armstrong into the lead. The safety car was then briefly deployed to recover [[David Beckmann]]'s car, which had spun. Armstrong held his lead for the remainder of the race to claim his second Formula 2 victory, followed by Jehan Daruvala in second and [[Dennis Hauger]] third with his first Formula 2 podium finish.

Pole-sitter Vips dropped to fourth place at the start of the feature race whilst [[Roy Nissany]] passed five cars to take the lead at the first corner. Meanwhile, a start-line collision between Jack Doohan and Dennis Hauger resulted in a safety car period to recover Hauger's car. The safety car was deployed again on lap six when Vips spun and crashed. This allowed drivers who started the race on super-soft compound tyres to pit for medium tyres and gain a time advantage. Nissany remained in the lead of this group of cars until he crashed on lap 21, after which Théo Pourchaire led the group. The race ended under safety car conditions as Liam Lawson crashed with three laps remaining. Felipe Drugovich was the final car to make a pit stop on the penultimate lap, allowing Pourchaire into the lead to take his second victory of the season. [[Enzo Fittipaldi]] finished second having started fifteenth. At the end of the round, Pourchaire led the championship by two points over Drugovich.

===Round 4: Spain===
{{Further|2022 Barcelona Formula 2 round}}
Jack Doohan took his second feature race pole position in qualifying at the [[Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya]]. Calan Williams was set to start the sprint race from pole but stalled on the formation lap and started from the back. Felipe Drugovich took the lead at the first corner and maintained this position for the rest of the race, holding off [[Ayumu Iwasa]] and Logan Sargeant at the safety car restart which was caused by Jüri Vips spinning into the gravel. Drugovich's victory allowed him to reclaim the championship lead from Théo Pourchaire, who finished fifth.

Feature race pole-sitter Doohan held the lead until he made his pit stop on lap 12. Drugovich, who started the race in tenth position, gained positions at the start and ran with the soft-compound tyres for six laps longer than Doohan. Drugovich emerged from his pit stop only two places behind Doohan. He soon passed [[Frederik Vesti]] and [[Dennis Hauger]] in the same corner and then caught and passed Doohan for the lead with ten laps remaining. Drugovich claimed his third race win of the season and Doohan and Vesti their first Formula 2 podium finishes. Drugovich's championship lead had now extended to 26 points over Pourchaire.

===Round 5: Monaco===
{{Further|2022 Monte Carlo Formula 2 round}}
Liam Lawson initially set the fastest time in qualifying at the [[Circuit de Monaco]], however his lap time – and that of second-fastest Ayumu Iwasa – were deleted for [[Racing flags#Yellow flag|yellow flag]] procedure violations, awarding feature race pole position to Drugovich. Jake Hughes started the sprint race from pole position but stalled at the start line, allowing Dennis Hauger into the lead. Hauger held the position for the rest of the race to take his first Formula 2 victory. Championship leader Drugovich received a puncture on the opening lap and later retired from the race.

The feature race was interrupted by two safety car periods, firstly when Amaury Cordeel crashed and secondly to recover [[Clément Novalak]]'s car after a collision with Lawson. Second-placed Théo Pourchaire closely followed pole-sitter Drugovich for much of the race, with the two drivers building a seven-second gap to third-placed Jüri Vips by the end. Pourchaire was ultimately unable to pass and Drugovich claimed his third victory in four races, extending his championship lead to 32 points over Pourchaire.

===Round 6: Azerbaijan===
{{Further|2022 Baku Formula 2 round}}
Jüri Vips claimed his second feature race pole position of the season in qualifying at the [[Baku City Circuit]]. Jake Hughes started the sprint race from first place. Frederik Vesti took the lead at the start but was then passed by Jehan Daruvala after the first corner. Daruvala built a gap and held his position for much of the race, until his advantage was reduced by the first of three late-race safety cars after teammate Dennis Hauger crashed. At the restart, Enzo Fittipaldi hit the barriers whilst passing Vips at the first corner, causing the second safety car. When the race resumed, Vesti was able to retake the lead from Daruvala shortly before Richard Verschoor crashed, causing the third safety car. Racing restarted with one lap remaining. A multi-car collision at the first turn then eliminated [[Ralph Boschung]], Calan Williams, [[Cem Bölükbaşı]] and [[Marino Sato]] from the race, which ended under yellow-flag conditions. Vesti's victory was his first in Formula 2.

Pole-sitter Vips held his lead at the start of the feature race. A collision between Amaury Cordeel and [[Olli Caldwell]] on the opening lap caused the first safety car of the race. During the first pit-stop phase, third-placed Marcus Armstrong was able to emerge ahead of Vips and Liam Lawson, aided by a slow pit stop for Lawson. Vips reclaimed the lead on the following lap. The safety car was again deployed shortly after when Roy Nissany and Cem Bölükbaşı collided and hit the barriers. A number of incidents occurred as racing resumed, with Ayumu Iwasa, Théo Pourchaire and Lawson all receiving damage. Vips crashed at the narrow castle section with six minutes of the race remaining, which then ended behind the safety car. This allowed Dennis Hauger to claim his second win of the season. The round concluded with Felipe Drugovich extending his championship lead to 49 points over Pourchaire.

=== Round 7: Great Britain ===
{{Further|2022 Silverstone Formula 2 round}}
Logan Sargeant took his first pole position in Formula 2 at [[Silverstone Circuit]]. Jehan Daruvala started the sprint race from first place, which began in wet conditions behind the safety car. Daruvala held the lead at the start but dropped back throughout the race, handing the lead to Enzo Fittipaldi on lap five. Jack Doohan, who started fourth, soon passed both drivers to claim first place. Fifth-placed starter Ayumu Iwasa also gained positions and closed in on Doohan in the final laps, however Doohan maintained his position to take his first Formula 2 race win.

An accident involving Dennis Hauger and Roy Nissany, in which Hauger's car was launched by a sausage kerb into the [[Halo (safety device)|halo]] of Nissany's car, brought out the safety car on the opening lap of the feature race. Pole-sitter Sargeant held his lead at the restart and for the rest of the race to take his first Formula 2 victory, followed by Théo Pourchaire and Liam Lawson. Championship leader Felipe Drugovich overtook Frederik Vesti for fourth place on the final lap. After the seventh round, Drugovich's championship lead stood at 42 points over second-placed Pourchaire.

=== Round 8: Austria ===
{{Further|2022 Spielberg Formula 2 round}}
As the championship arrived at the [[Red Bull Ring]], Frederik Vesti claimed his maiden pole position, with Marcus Armstrong starting the sprint race from the front. Armstrong led the entire race distance to take his second win of the season, followed by Théo Pourchaire and Jack Doohan, who had taken third place from Richard Verschoor at the race start.

The feature race began with a damp track. A number of drivers, the highest-starting being eighth-placed Verschoor, opted to start on [[Racing slick|slick tyres]]. Pole-sitter Vesti lost the lead to Jüri Vips at the start before the safety car was deployed when Armstrong and Jehan Daruvala collided. When racing resumed, the track had dried enough that the slick-tyre runners had the advantage and Verschoor passed the cars in front to take the lead. Once all drivers had made their mandatory pit stop, Verschoor led, followed by Daruvala and [[Roberto Merhi]] (who had started the race in 21st place), both of whom had also started on slick tyres. Verschoor crossed the line first, and Merhi overtook Daruvala on the penultimate lap to finish second, but was demoted to fifth by a time penalty for track limits violations. Logan Sargeant, who was disadvantaged by starting on the wet-weather tyres, recovered to finish fourth and was elevated to the podium by Merhi's penalty.

After the race, Verschoor was disqualified for failing to provide the required fuel sample to the [[FIA]]. Second-placed Daruvala was issued a penalty as his team had illegally attempted to dry the track on his grid spot before the race start. Thus, Sargeant was awarded the win. At the end of the round, Felipe Drugovich remained in the lead of the drivers' championship, 39 points ahead of Sargeant, whose victory had promoted him ahead of Pourchaire in the standings.

=== Round 9: France ===
{{Further|2022 Le Castellet Formula 2 round}}
Logan Sargeant took feature race pole position in qualifying at [[Circuit Paul Ricard]]. In the sprint race, pole-sitter Jehan Daruvala held the lead at the start. Liam Lawson, who lost second place to Marcus Armstrong at the start, retook the position three laps later and began to close up to Daruvala. The safety car was deployed on lap nine when Enzo Fittipaldi collided with Roberto Merhi and stopped on track before Amaury Cordeel hit his stricken car. Lawson overtook Daruvala for the lead with five laps remaining and claimed his second race win of the season. Théo Pourchaire, who finished third, was penalised after the race for forcing Armstrong off the track. Championship leader Drugovich inherited the podium position.

Pole-sitter Sargeant lost positions at the feature race start, allowing Jack Doohan, who started fourth, into the lead. Doohan was then overtaken by Ayumu Iwasa later on the first lap shortly before the safety car was deployed to recover the cars of Marino Sato and Marcus Armstrong, who were involved in separate incidents. Doohan and third-placed Sargeant were later taken out of podium contention; Sargeant stalled during his pit stop and was unable to restart his car, and Doohan spun during a battle with Théo Pourchaire, allowing ART Grand Prix teammates Pourchaire and Frederik Vesti onto the podium behind winner Iwasa, who took his first Formula 2 race victory. Felipe Drugovich remained in the lead of the drivers' championship after the ninth round with a 39-point advantage over Pourchaire, who had reclaimed second place after Sargeant's retirement.

=== Round 10: Hungary ===
{{Further|2022 Hungaroring Formula 2 round}}
Ayumu Iwasa qualified on pole position for the feature race at the [[Hungaroring]]. Jack Doohan qualified tenth to secure sprint race pole position. Enzo Fittipaldi briefly took the lead at the start of the sprint race, but Doohan reclaimed the position shortly after when Fittipaldi locked up his brakes. A collision at the first corner between Felipe Drugovich, Logan Sargeant and Dennis Hauger eliminated the latter two from the race. Doohan led the rest of the race to claim his second win of the season.

Pole-sitter Iwasa dropped behind Marcus Armstrong and Théo Pourchaire at the start of the feature race, but Armstrong lost positions to both drivers during the pit stops, allowing Pourchaire to lead the group. Fittipaldi, previously in fourth place, extended his stint on the soft tyres and emerged from the pits ahead of Iwasa and Armstrong. Pourchaire took the chequered flag to claim his third victory of the season. Championship leader Drugovich was the first driver to make a pit stop and lost places in the final laps as his tyres wore out, allowing Pourchaire to close his championship lead to 21 points.

=== Round 11: Belgium ===
{{Further|2022 Spa-Francorchamps Formula 2 round}}
Drugovich once again was quickest in qualifying, thus securing his third pole position, for the first time since Monaco. In the sprint race, [[Ralph Boschung]], who missed 10 races due to a neck injury, scored his second podium of the year (and fourth overall in Formula 2 from 2017) on his return, holding off championship leader at the very end. Jehan Daruvala, who qualified second, didn't start after his engine failed, opening the door to Liam Lawson to win the sprint race. For his teammate Sargeant, it was disappointment, as his race ended in the wall, causing a safety car. [[Jack Doohan (racing driver)|Jack Doohan]] completed the podium, finishing second behind Lawson and ahead of Boschung. During the safety car period, many drivers pitted (including Drugovich), but were not able to convert their new tyres into a substantial advantage.

The polesitter led the first laps of the feature race, but could not create a gap over second-placed Doohan. The safety car returned in the feature race, once again for a crashed out Sargeant, making it two in two races where the safety car was deployed. On the restart, Drugovich could not hold off Doohan to take first and with it the race victory. He still increased the gap in the championship due to [[Theo Pourchaire|Théo Pourchaire]]'s gearbox failure on lap three. Doohan won his third race of the year, ahead of championship leader Drugovich and sprint race winner Lawson, who completed the podium. Drugovich increased his championship lead to 43 points over second-placed Pourchaire.

=== Round 12: Netherlands ===
{{Further|2022 Zandvoort Formula 2 round}}
Qualifying was once again won by Felipe Drugovich, who scored 2 more points. The sprint race was largely processional, with the biggest overtake happening right at the start when Marcus Armstrong overtook reverse grid poleman Novalak. Two drivers retired, Olli Caldwell and Tatiana Calderón. The race was won by [[Marcus Armstrong]], with [[Clement Novalak|Clément Novalak]] taking his first ever Formula 2 podium, and with Dennis Hauger completing the podium. The safety car was deployed on the final laps due to Calderón crashing out.

In the feature race, there was more drama however, the first important moment coming on the first lap where Logan Sargeant heavily crashed at Turn 7 after a bad start. The race was red-flagged and not all laps were completed due to more accidents, making the race reach its 60-minute time limit. Marino Sato nearly crashed when his wheel came off after not being properly fitted on the pitstop. On the second restart after Sato's car was evacuated, Richard Verschoor drove into the back of Jack Doohan's car, thus eliminating the Australian from the race. Novalak and Calderón had similar contact that saw their cars were badly damaged. Felipe Drugovich won his fifth race of the year, beating Verschoor and Ayumu Iwasa. Both [[Van Amersfoort Racing]] drivers [[David Beckmann]] and [[Amaury Cordeel]] came home in the points. However, Beckmann later received a penalty, stripping him of his points. Drugovich saw himself achieving the first matchball in order to decide the drivers' championship to his favor after another disappointing weekend for Pourchaire, increasing his championship lead to 69 points with two rounds to go.

=== Round 13: Italy (Monza) ===
{{Further|2022 Monza Formula 2 round}}
Jack Doohan took his third pole position of the season. In the sprint race, [[Juri Vips|Jüri Vips]] was challenged at the start, but dominated the rest of the race to win the first race on Saturday. Vips' first win of the season marked his best result since being dropped by the [[Red Bull Junior Team|Red Bull Academy]] in June. The podium was completed by [[Frederik Vesti|Vesti]] and [[Jehan Daruvala|Daruvala]]. Drugovich became champion after his main rival [[Theo Pourchaire|Théo Pourchaire]] was penalised and classified only 17th and last in the race. For the Brazilian driver the race was rather short, as he made contact at the start, sustaining suspension damage that forced him to retire. His [[MP Motorsport]] teammate [[Clement Novalak|Novalak]] also retired due to contact with [[Ralph Boschung]] on lap seven, with both cars sustaining damage. There was one full safety car deployment, when [[Olli Caldwell]] and [[Tatiana Calderon|Tatiana Calderón]] collided on lap one.

On Sunday, Calderón withdrew from the feature race due to her injuries. The race was affected by a big crash at the start: both Campos drivers, Pourchaire and the returning [[Luca Ghiotto]] all came together, while Doohan and [[Logan Sargeant]] had a separate crash, eliminating both from the race. This meant after just two laps, seven drivers had already retired from the race. On lap six, another safety car was deployed due to [[Calan Williams]]'s crash. The race was then red-flagged to repair the barriers. After the restart, Daruvala comfortably held a gap and won a race for the first time this season. Vesti and [[Enzo Fittipaldi]] completed the podium, after Iwasa was disqualified for a technical infringement. Champion Drugovich finished in sixth place between both [[Van Amersfoort Racing]] drivers Beckmann and Cordeel, another double points finish after Zandvoort, and with no penalties issued this time, allowing both to keep their places.

=== Round 14: Abu Dhabi ===
{{Further|2022 Yas Island Formula 2 round}}
Ahead of the final Formula 2 race of 2022, [[Williams Grand Prix Engineering|Williams Racing]] Team Principal and CEO [[Jost Capito]] announced that [[Carlin Motorsport|Carlin]] driver [[Logan Sargeant]] would be promoted from the [[Williams Driver Academy]] to a [[Formula One]] race seat with the team in [[2023 Formula One World Championship|2023]] if he secured enough points to qualify for an [[FIA Super Licence]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Williams boss Capito says Logan Sargeant will race for Williams in 2023 if he secures Super Licence points {{!}} Formula 1 |url=https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article.breaking-williams-boss-capito-says-logan-sargeant-will-race-for-williams-in.6JzURIiDAfCjEwuSPVBO9D.html |access-date=2022-12-21 |website=www.formula1.com |language=en}}</ref> Having already accumulated 29 Super Licence points before the Abu Dhabi race weekend, Sargeant needed to finish at least fifth in the 2022 FIA Formula 2 Drivers' Championship (or sixth, without receiving any penalty points).<ref>{{Cite web |title=F1 EXPLAINED: How does the FIA's Super Licence points system work? {{!}} Formula 1 |url=https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article.f1-explained-how-does-the-super-licence-system-work-and-what-does-sargeant.IXyLbO00195LXtCo3YIOU.html |access-date=2022-12-21 |website=www.formula1.com |language=en}}</ref>

Several changes were made to teams' driver line-ups for the Abu Dhabi race weekend. [[Trident Racing|Trident]] driver Calan Williams was replaced by [[2022 FIA Formula 3 Championship|2022]] [[FIA Formula 3 Championship|FIA Formula 3]] vice-champion [[Zane Maloney]], who would make his Formula 2 race debut.<ref>{{Cite web |last=White |first=Megan |date=2022-11-07 |title=F3 runner-up Maloney to make F2 debut in Abu Dhabi finale |url=https://www.motorsport.com/fia-f2/news/maloney-to-make-trident-f2-debut-at-abu-dhabi-season-finale/10396270/ |access-date=2022-12-21 |website=www.motorsport.com |language=en}}</ref> Additionally, [[Juan Manuel Correa]] returned to Formula 2 with [[Van Amersfoort Racing]], competing in the series for the first time since his accident in [[2019 Spa-Francorchamps Formula 2 round|2019]].

The 2022 [[FIA Formula 2 Championship#Teams'|FIA Formula 2 Teams' Championship]] had yet to be decided ahead of the final race of the season, with [[MP Motorsport]], [[ART Grand Prix]], and Carlin in contention for the title. Both MP Motorsport and ART Grand Prix were level on points, with the Dutch team ahead on countback due to having a greater number of race wins. Carlin were third in the standings, twenty-three points behind the other title contenders.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2022-11-20 |title=F2 Preview - Round 14 - Yas Island, Abu Dhabi, UAE |url=https://www.fia.com/sites/default/files/2022_fia_formula_2_championship_-_round_14_preview.pdf |access-date=2022-12-21 |website=FIA Formula 2 Championship}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=2022-09-11 |title=AMENDED F2 Team Championship - Monza 2022 |url=https://www.fia.com/sites/default/files/decision-document/2022%20Monza%20Event%20-%20AMENDED%20F2%20Team%20Championship%20-%20Monza%202022.pdf |access-date=2022-12-21 |website=FIA}}</ref>

Despite having a lap time deleted mere minutes before qualifying ended, [[Ayumu Iwasa]] managed to secure pole position in a qualifying session which was shortened due to red flags. His teammate at [[DAMS]], [[Roy Nissany]], qualified second.<ref>{{Cite web |last=White |first=Megan |date=2022-11-18 |title=F2 Abu Dhabi: Iwasa leads DAMS 1-2 in truncated qualifying |url=https://www.motorsport.com/fia-f2/news/abu-dhabi-iwasa-dams-pole-qualifying/10401659/ |access-date=2022-12-21 |website=www.motorsport.com |language=en}}</ref>

[[Richard Verschoor]] started the sprint race from pole; however, he crossed the line in second place after being passed by [[Liam Lawson]], who went on to win the race. [[Felipe Drugovich]] made up four places to finish in third after starting from seventh place on the grid. The sprint race was red-flagged midway through lap one after [[Enzo Fittipaldi]] and Jehan Daruvala collided. This allowed Logan Sargeant to take the rolling restart from sixth, despite having originally dropped to ninth at the start of the sprint race.<ref>{{Cite web |last=White |first=Megan |date=2022-11-19 |title=F2 Abu Dhabi: Lawson wins red-flagged sprint race |url=https://www.motorsport.com/fia-f2/news/f2-abu-dhabi-lawson-scores-fourth-season-win-in-red-flagged-race/10402306/ |access-date=2022-12-21 |website=www.motorsport.com |language=en}}</ref>

Ayumu Iwasa won the feature race from pole position after battling Felipe Drugovich, whose second-place finish secured MP Motorsport the 2022 Teams' Championship. Liam Lawson finished in third place, allowing Carlin to overtake ART Grand Prix to finish second in the teams' standings. A virtual safety car was deployed after Campos driver [[Ralph Boschung]] made contact with Juan Manuel Correa, causing the Swiss driver to retire from the race. [[Jack Doohan (racing driver)|Jack Doohan]] was forced to retire from the race after his front left tyre detached following a late pit stop, resulting in him dropping to sixth in the drivers' standings. [[Théo Pourchaire|Theo Pourchaire]] also retired from the race after suffering from a terminal mechanical issue.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |last=White |first=Megan |date=2022-11-20 |title=F2 Abu Dhabi: Iwasa wins finale as Sargeant secures superlicence |url=https://www.motorsport.com/fia-f2/news/iwasa-abu-dhabi-win-sargeant-superlicense/10402706/ |access-date=2022-12-21 |website=www.motorsport.com |language=en}}</ref>

Sargeant's fifth-place finish in the feature race meant he finished fourth in the championship standings, one point behind his teammate Liam Lawson. This meant that he had accrued enough Super Licence points to ensure his promotion to Formula One in 2023.<ref name=":0" /><ref>{{Cite web |title=Logan Sargeant confirmed as Williams F1 driver for 2023 |url=https://www.fiaformula2.com/Latest/3FqjaBSKbGyzivQOpTCio2/logan-sargeant-confirmed-as-williams-f1-driver-for-2023 |access-date=2022-12-21 |website=FIA_F2 - The Official F2 Website |language=en}}</ref>

=== Scoring system ===
Points were awarded to the top eight classified finishers in the Sprint race, and to the top ten classified finishers in the Feature race. The pole-sitter in the feature race also received two points, and one point was given to the driver who sets the fastest lap in both the feature and sprint races if that driver finished inside the top ten. No point was awarded if the fastest lap time was achieved by a driver who was classified outside the top ten. No extra points were awarded to the pole-sitter in the sprint race as the grid for it was set by reversing the top ten qualifiers.<ref name=Regulations>{{cite web|title=2022 FIA Formula 2 Sporting Regulations|url=https://www.fia.com/sites/default/files/2022_fia_f2_sporting_regulations_v2_11-03-2022.pdf|work=[[Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile]]|pages=3-4|date=11 March 2022|access-date=17 March 2025|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220725023026/https://www.fia.com/sites/default/files/2022_fia_f2_sporting_regulations_v2_11-03-2022.pdf|archive-date=25 July 2022|url-status=dead}}</ref>
