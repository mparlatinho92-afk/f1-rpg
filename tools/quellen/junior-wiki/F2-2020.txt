The '''2020 FIA Formula 2 Championship''' was a [[motor racing]] championship for [[Formula Two|Formula 2]] cars, sanctioned by the [[Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile]] (FIA). The championship was the fifty-fourth season of Formula 2 racing and the fourth season run under the [[FIA Formula 2 Championship]] moniker. It is an [[open-wheel racing]] category, that serves as the second tier of [[formula racing]] in the [[FIA Global Pathway]]. The category was run in support of selected rounds of the [[2020 Formula One World Championship|2020 FIA Formula One World Championship]]. As the championship was a [[spec series]], all teams and drivers competing in the championship ran the same car, the [[Dallara F2 2018]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.autosport.com/f2/news/131537/new-f2-car-revealed-to-feature-halo-device|title=New F2 car for 2018 revealed, featuring halo head protection device|last=Kalinauckas|first=Alex|website=[[autosport]].com|publisher=[[Motorsport Network]]|language=en|access-date=25 August 2019|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170901211431/https://www.autosport.com/f2/news/131537/new-f2-car-revealed-to-feature-halo-device|archive-date=1 September 2017|url-status=dead}}</ref>

[[Nyck de Vries]] was the 2019 drivers' champion, having won the title at the penultimate race of the 2019 championship in [[Sochi Autodrom|Russia]],{{efn|name=champ out|Under the series' sporting regulations, the defending drivers' champion is not permitted to continue racing in the championship.}} thus [[Mick Schumacher]] became the next driver to win the title at the final race in the Outer layout of the Bahrain International Circuit with 14 points ahead of runner-up [[Callum Ilott]]. [[DAMS]] were the reigning teams' champions, having secured their first Formula 2 title in Abu Dhabi. [[Prema Powerteam|Prema Racing]] became the next team to clinch teams' title for the first time, after the finish of the feature race in the Outer layout of the Bahrain International Circuit.

2020 was due to be the final season with the [[Dallara F2 2018]] chassis package which debuted in 2018 with a new chassis introduced for 2021. This was delayed for another three seasons due to the [[COVID-19 pandemic]].

The season would see a record 12 different race winners: with the most race victories of 4 for [[Robert Shwartzman]], 3 wins went to [[Callum Ilott]], [[Yuki Tsunoda]] and [[Felipe Drugovich]]. 2 races were won by champion [[Mick Schumacher]], [[Christian Lundgaard]] and [[Nikita Mazepin]]. Other drivers, who won a race were victorious once: victories went to [[Luca Ghiotto]] and [[Nobuharu Matsushita]], (for both of them victories were final in the series before retiring from F2), and others: [[Dan Ticktum]] and [[Jehan Daruvala]] with last winner being [[Guanyu Zhou]].

===Midseason changes===
Sean Gelael suffered a broken vertebra during the last lap of the feature race in Barcelona when he collided with Jack Aitken. As a result, Gelael was declared unfit to participate in Spa and the following three rounds, leaving his [[DAMS]] seat vacant.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Woollard |first1=Craig |title=Sean Gelael ruled out of F2 for six weeks by injury |url=https://formulascout.com/sean-gelael-ruled-out-of-f2-for-six-weeks-by-injury/66590 |access-date=19 August 2020 |work=formulascout.com |publisher=Formula Scout |date=19 August 2020}}</ref> The team hired [[Red Bull Junior Team|Red Bull Junior]] driver [[Jüri Vips]] as an interim driver.

Matsushita left the MP Motorsport team after the Mugello round, and Alesi moved across from [[HWA Team|BWT HWA Racelab]] to replace him with immediate effect. HWA's [[FIA Formula 3 Championship]] driver [[Jake Hughes]] graduated to Formula 2 to replace Alesi.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.motorsport.com/fia-f2/news/alesi-quits-hwa-f2-matsushita/4879435/?ic_source=home-page-widget&ic_medium=widget&ic_campaign=widget-1|title=Alesi leaves HWA F2 team, replaces Matsushita at MP|publisher=[[Motorsport.com]]|date=22 September 2020}}</ref>

[[2020 FIA Formula 3 Championship]] runner-up [[Théo Pourchaire]] was promoted to Formula 2 for the last two races, replacing Hughes at BWT HWA Racelab.<ref>{{Cite web|last=Wood|first=Elliot|date=2020-10-14|title=FIA F3 runner-up Pourchaire to make F2 debut in Bahrain|url=https://formulascout.com/fia-f3-runner-up-pourchaire-to-make-f2-debut-in-bahrain/69397|access-date=2020-10-14|website=Formula Scout|language=en-US}}</ref>

At the Formula One [[2020 Sakhir Grand Prix|Sakhir Grand Prix]], [[Campos Racing]] driver [[Jack Aitken]] was called up to the [[Williams Racing|Williams]] Formula One team as a replacement for [[2018 Formula 2 Championship|2018 Formula 2]] champion [[George Russell (racing driver)|George Russell]], who replaced an absent [[Lewis Hamilton]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=F2 racer Jack Aitken to make F1 debut with Williams at Sakhir Grand Prix, replacing Russell|url=https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article.f2-racer-jack-aitken-to-make-f1-debut-with-williams-at-sakhir-grand-prix.2IJ1Ws2y77irsJHcEadmI1.html|access-date=2020-12-02|website=www.formula1.com|language=en}}</ref> [[Ralph Boschung]] stood in for Aitken for the final round of the championship.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Wood |first1=Elliot |title=Ralph Boschung to make early F2 return in place of Aitken at Bahrain |url=https://formulascout.com/ralph-boschung-to-make-early-f2-return-in-place-of-aitken-at-bahrain/71956 |access-date=3 December 2020 |work=formulascout.com |publisher=Formula Scout |date=3 December 2020}}</ref>

==Calendar==
Both the original and revised calendars had twelve rounds scheduled to take place as part of the 2020 championship. Each round consists of two races: a Feature race, which is run on Saturday, over a distance of {{convert|170|km|mi|1|abbr=on}} and includes a mandatory pit stop;{{efn|name=distance certain|The Monaco and Budapest feature races are run over a reduced distance, with a length of {{convert|140|km|mi|1|abbr=on}} and {{convert|160|km|mi|1|abbr=on}} respectively.}} and a Sprint race, which is run over {{convert|120|km|mi|0|abbr=on}} and does not require drivers to make a pit stop.{{efn|name=time certain|The Feature and Sprint races are time-certain. In the event that the full race distance cannot be completed, the Feature race will end after one hour and the Sprint race after forty-five minutes.}}

===Calendar changes===
The [[Circuit Paul Ricard]] round that was run in support of the [[French Grand Prix]] was removed from the calendar. It was replaced by a round at the [[Circuit Zandvoort]], running in support of the revived [[Dutch Grand Prix]].<ref name="calendar">{{cite news|url=https://www.autosport.com/f2/news/146054/f2-f3-20-calendars-revealed-paul-ricard-exits|title=Formula 2 and Formula 3 2020 calendar revealed, Paul Ricard dropped|last=Benyon|first=Jack|website=[[autosport]].com|publisher=[[Motorsport Network]]|language=en|date=17 September 2019|access-date=17 September 2019}}</ref> The [[Bahrain International Circuit|Bahrain]] and [[Circuit de Barcelona|Barcelona]] rounds were postponed in response to the [[COVID-19 pandemic]], while the [[Circuit Zandvoort|Zandvoort]], [[Circuit de Monaco|Monaco]] and [[Baku City Circuit|Baku]] rounds were cancelled alongside the [[Dutch Grand Prix|Dutch]], [[Monaco Grand Prix|Monaco]] and [[Azerbaijan Grand Prix|Azerbaijan Grands Prix]].<ref name="BHR postponed">{{Cite web|url=http://www.fiaformula2.com/News-Room/News/2020/03_March/FIA-Formula-2-and-FIA-Formula-3-Championships-Round-1-in-Bahrain-postponed/|title=FIA Formula 2 and FIA Formula 3 Championships Round 1 in Bahrain postponed - Formula 2|website=fiaformula2.com|access-date=13 March 2020|archive-date=23 March 2024|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240323231216/http://www.fiaformula2.com/News-Room/News/2020/03_March/FIA-Formula-2-and-FIA-Formula-3-Championships-Round-1-in-Bahrain-postponed/|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref name="ZANBARMON"/><ref name="baku f2"/> A revised calendar was published in June 2020, featuring two rounds at the [[Red Bull Ring]] and two at the [[Silverstone Circuit]].<ref name="new calendar"/> On 10 July 2020, Mugello was added as the ninth round of the season. It was the first time for this circuit to be featured in the schedule of the main Formula One feeder series since the [[1997 International Formula 3000 Championship]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.fiaformula2.com/Latest/2k0dVVCDXZjSg5vIUVQee0/fia-formula-2-and-fia-formula-3-add-mugello-to-2020-season-calendar|title = FIA Formula 2 and FIA Formula 3 add Mugello to 2020 season calendar}}</ref> On 29 July 2020, Sochi was added to the calendar as the tenth round of the season.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.fiaformula2.com/Latest/7HysGz7vksoAN23hWOvG1p/sochi-added-to-the-fia-formula-2-2020-revised-calendar|title = Sochi added to the FIA Formula 2 2020 revised calendar}}</ref> The championship used the Outer Circuit layout for the season finale at Bahrain.

==Regulation changes==
===Technical changes===
The championship increased the wheel rims from {{convert|13|to|18|in|lk=on|abbr=on|order=flip}} to allow [[Formula One tyre]] partner [[Pirelli]] to gather data on how the larger tyres would work ahead of Formula One's adoption of 460&nbsp;mm wheels in 2022. In addition the Pirelli [[FIA Formula 2 Championship]] tyre sizes were also slightly altered with the front tyre width increased from {{convert|245|to|275|mm|in|1|lk=on|abbr=on}} while the rear tyre width remained same at {{convert|325|mm|in|1|lk=on|abbr=on}}. The overall tyre diameter (front and rear) sizes were increased from {{convert|660|to|705|mm|in|1|lk=on|abbr=on}} as a result of 460&nbsp;mm wheel rim introduction.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.autosport.com/f2/news/143255/f2-gets-18inch-tyres-a-year-ahead-of-f1|title=Formula 2 to run 18-inch tyres in 2020 two years ahead of F1|last=Noble|first=Jonathan|website=[[autosport]].com|date=9 May 2019 |publisher=[[Motorsport Network]]|language=en|access-date=25 August 2019}}</ref> The conventional 330&nbsp;mm wheel rims that had been used since [[2005 GP2 Series|2005]] were retired.

==Season report==
===Opening rounds===
[[Virtuosi Racing|UNI-Virtuosi]]'s [[Guanyu Zhou]] started the season opener at the [[Red Bull Ring]] from [[pole position]] alongside series debutant [[Felipe Drugovich]] in second place.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Qualifying: Zhou storms to Feature Race pole ahead of Drugovich|url=https://www.gptoday.net/en/news/f1/256287/qualifying-zhou-storms-to-feature-race-pole-ahead-of-drugovich|access-date=2020-07-03|website=GPToday.net|language=en-GB}}</ref> Zhou led the majority of the race and fought closely with teammate [[Callum Ilott]] and [[Prema Racing]]'s [[Mick Schumacher]]. A technical issue later dropped Zhou to the back and Schumacher's chance of victory was lost after a trip through the gravel at turn six, allowing Ilott to claim his first Formula 2 race victory. Drugovich started the sprint race on pole position by virtue of finishing eighth in the feature race. He maintained the lead for the whole race distance to take his debut win in Formula 2. The opening round finished with Ilott leading the championship, four points ahead of Prema's [[Robert Shwartzman]].

Formula 2 remained at the Red Bull Ring on the following weekend, where [[Carlin Motorsport|Carlin]]'s [[Yuki Tsunoda]] took pole position. The feature race began under [[safety car]] conditions due to wet weather. Tsunoda led for most of the race, but radio issues prevented him from hearing his team's call to enter the pits, causing the gap behind him to close as his tyres degraded. After eventually making a pit stop, he had lost position to Shwartzman, Ilott and Zhou. Tsunoda recovered to second place but was unable to overtake Shwartzman, who took his first Formula 2 victory and the championship lead.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Waring |first1=Bethonie |title=Shwartzman holds off Tsunoda to win wet F2 feature race at Styrian GP |url=https://formulascout.com/shwartzman-holds-off-tsunoda-to-win-wet-f2-feature-race-at-styrian-gp/64475 |access-date=11 July 2020 |work=formulascout.com |publisher=Formula Scout |date=11 July 2020}}</ref> Tsunoda and Shwartzman both retired from the sprint race, Tsunoda with a suspected clutch issue and Shwartzman after spinning on the opening lap. [[ART Grand Prix]]'s [[Christian Lundgaard]] overtook reverse-grid pole sitter [[Dan Ticktum]] to claim his maiden Formula 2 victory. Despite his retirement, Shwartzman maintained the lead of the championship by five points over Ilott.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://formulascout.com/christian-lundgaard-controls-styrian-sprint-race-for-first-f2-win/64518|title=Christian Lundgaard controls Styrian sprint race for first F2 win|website=formulascout.com|first=Josh|last=Suttill|date=12 July 2020|access-date=12 July 2020}}</ref>

Ilott claimed pole position in the wet qualifying session at the [[Hungaroring]].<ref>{{cite news |last1=Allen |first1=Peter |title=Callum Ilott claims F2 pole in wet Hungaroring qualifying |url=https://formulascout.com/callum-ilott-claims-f2-pole-in-wet-hungaroring-qualifying/64745 |access-date=18 July 2020 |work=formulascout.com |publisher=Formula Scout |date=18 July 2020}}</ref> During the feature race he traded the lead with Schumacher, but fell down the order in the closing laps as his medium-compound tyres degraded. Conversely, Shwartzman and [[Hitech Grand Prix]]'s [[Nikita Mazepin]], who started the race 11th and 16th respectively, switched from medium to soft-compound tyres later in the race. This proved to be the superior strategy and Shwartzman came through the field to take his second consecutive feature race victory, with Mazepin second. Ilott started on pole position for the sprint race. Whilst most drivers elected to make a pit stop, Hitech's [[Luca Ghiotto]] did not. This left Ghiotto in the lead with a 40-second gap behind to Ilott, which rapidly closed over the final ten laps. Ilott was ultimately unable to pass Ghiotto, who took his sixth Formula 2 victory by less than half a second. After the Hungaroring round, Shwartzman had extended his championship lead over Ilott to 18 points.

===Mid-season===
The first round at [[Silverstone Circuit|Silverstone]] saw [[MP Motorsport]]'s Felipe Drugovich take his team's first ever Formula 2 pole position, whilst championship leader Shwartzman qualified 18th.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Suttill |first1=Josh |title=Drugovich on F2 pole at Silverstone, Shwartzman at back of grid |url=https://formulascout.com/drugovich-on-f2-pole-at-silverstone-shwartzman-at-back-of-grid/65379 |access-date=31 July 2020 |work=formulascout.com |publisher=Formula Scout |date=31 July 2020}}</ref> Second-placed Ilott stalled on the [[Parade lap|formation lap]] and was forced to start from the pit lane. Schumacher took the lead at the start, but was overtaken by Mazepin soon after. Mazepin led the rest of the race to take his maiden Formula 2 race win.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Allen |first1=Peter |title=Mazepin flies to Silverstone F2 feature race win for Hitech |url=https://formulascout.com/mazepin-flies-to-silverstone-f2-feature-race-win-for-hitech/65526 |access-date=16 August 2020 |work=formulascout.com |publisher=Formula Scout |date=16 August 2020}}</ref> Dan Ticktum started the sprint race from pole position and maintained his lead. Ilott, who had been running closely behind Ticktum in the final laps, was forced into retirement after a spin, and Ticktum held off Christian Lundgaard on the final lap to achieve his first victory. Despite scoring no points, Shwartzman held his championship lead over Ilott by eight points after the fourth round.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Suttill |first1=Josh |title=Ticktum fends off fast-charging Lundgaard for first F2 win in Silverstone sprint |url=https://formulascout.com/ticktum-fends-off-fast-charging-lundgaard-for-first-f2-win-in-silverstone-sprint/65607 |access-date=16 August 2020 |work=formulascout.com |publisher=Formula Scout |date=16 August 2020}}</ref>

Ilott returned to pole position for the second round at Silverstone and led the entire feature race to take his second victory of the season.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Waring |first1=Bethonie |title=Ilott beats Lundgaard for pole at second Silverstone F2 round |url=https://formulascout.com/ilott-beats-lundgaard-for-pole-at-second-silverstone-f2-round/65911 |access-date=16 August 2020 |work=formulascout.com |publisher=Formula Scout |date=7 August 2020}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |last1=Waring |first1=Bethonie |title=Callum Ilott dominates at Silverstone to claim F2 points lead |url=https://formulascout.com/callum-ilott-dominates-at-silverstone-to-claim-f2-points-lead/66005 |access-date=16 August 2020 |work=formulascout.com |publisher=Formula Scout |date=16 August 2020}}</ref> Shwartzman finished eighth, losing his championship lead to Ilott but giving him reverse-grid pole position for the sprint race. He led for most of the race with teammate Schumacher close behind, but the two collided with two laps remaining, damaging Shwartzman's front wing and relegating him to the back. This allowed Yuki Tsunoda through to claim his first Formula 2 race win. After round five, Ilott led the championship by 19 points over Christian Lundgaard.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Allen |first1=Peter |title=Tsunoda wins after Schumacher and Shwartzman collide in Silverstone F2 sprint |url=https://formulascout.com/tsunoda-wins-after-schumacher-and-shwartzman-collide-in-silverstone-f2-sprint/66052 |access-date=16 August 2020 |work=formulascout.com |publisher=Formula Scout |date=9 August 2020}}</ref>

Ilott again took pole position at the [[Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya]].<ref>{{cite news |last1=Allen |first1=Peter |title=Ilott beats Shwartzman for another F2 pole at Barcelona |url=https://formulascout.com/ilott-beats-shwartzman-for-another-f2-pole-at-barcelona/66301 |access-date=16 August 2020 |work=formulascout.com |publisher=Formula Scout |date=16 August 2020}}</ref> He traded the lead of the race with Shwartzman and Schumacher but had reclaimed first place by lap 25, when a collision between [[Sean Gelael]] and [[Giuliano Alesi]] brought out the safety car. Whilst most drivers had made a pit stop earlier in the race after starting on soft-compound tyres, MP Motorsport's [[Nobuharu Matsushita]] had stayed out after starting from 18th place on hard tyres. He made his first stop under safety car conditions, elevating him to third place. He was then able to pass Ilott and Tsunoda to take the lead and achieve MP Motorsport's first ever Formula 2 feature race victory.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Allen |first1=Peter |title=Matsushita takes unlikely Barcelona F2 feature race win from 18th |url=https://formulascout.com/matsushita-takes-unlikely-barcelona-f2-feature-race-win-from-18th/66385 |access-date=16 August 2020 |work=formulascout.com |publisher=Formula Scout |date=15 August 2020}}</ref> A collision between Gelael and [[Jack Aitken]] on the final lap resulted in Gelael suffering a broken [[vertebra]], forcing him to miss the sprint race. Luca Ghiotto began the sprint race on pole position but was passed by Felipe Drugovich at the start, who held the position to take his second victory of the season. Ilott kept the championship lead after round six by 18 points over Shwartzman.

Tsunoda achieved his second pole position in the seventh round at [[Spa-Francorchamps]], where [[Jüri Vips]] made his Formula 2 debut replacing the injured Gelael at DAMS.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Waring |first1=Bethonie |title=Tsunoda takes Spa F2 pole as Ilott qualifies 12th |url=https://formulascout.com/tsunoda-takes-spa-f2-pole-as-ilott-qualifies-12th/67124 |access-date=30 August 2020 |work=formulascout.com |publisher=Formula Scout |date=30 August 2020}}</ref> Tsunoda was closely followed by Nikita Mazepin in the feature race until a slow pit stop handed Mazepin the lead. Mazepin held off attacks from Tsunoda to cross the line in first place, but he was issued with a time penalty for forcing Tsunoda off the track, awarding Tsunoda his second win of the season.<ref name="Spa">{{cite news |last1=Woolard |first1=Craig |title=Tsunoda takes Formula 2 Spa victory after Mazepin penalty |url=https://formulascout.com/tsunoda-takes-formula-2-spa-victory-after-mazepin-penalty/67220 |access-date=30 August 2020 |work=formulascout.com |publisher=Formula Scout |date=30 August 2020}}</ref> [[Trident Racing|Trident]]'s [[Roy Nissany]] started on pole position for the sprint race, in which championship leader Ilott was forced into retirement after a collision with Tsunoda. Nissany and Dan Ticktum traded the lead in the early laps before the two collided, sending Nissany into the barriers. This allowed Shwartzman through to claim his third win of the season, as well as the lead of the championship by 10 points over Ilott.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Woolard |first1=Craig |title=Shwartzman wins Spa F2 sprint after Ticktum and Nissany collide |url=https://formulascout.com/shwartzman-wins-spa-f2-sprint-after-ticktum-and-nissany-collide/67298 |access-date=30 August 2020 |work=formulascout.com |publisher=Formula Scout |date=30 August 2020}}</ref>

Ilott repeated his {{F2|2019}} success in qualifying at [[Autodromo Nazionale di Monza|Monza]], claiming pole position.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Shade |first1=Harry |title=Ilott beats Tsunoda to Monza F2 pole as Shwartzman struggles to 16th |url=https://formulascout.com/ilott-beats-tsunoda-to-monza-f2-pole-as-shwartzman-struggles-to-16th/67595 |access-date=6 September 2020 |work=formulascout.com |publisher=Formula Scout |date=4 September 2020}}</ref> Mick Schumacher, who started seventh, passed multiple drivers at the start to take second behind Ilott at the first corner. Ilott stalled his car during his pit stop, dropping him to the back of the field and allowing Schumacher through to take his first victory of the season. [[Charouz Racing System]] driver [[Louis Delétraz]] started on reverse-grid pole position, but was passed by Dan Ticktum before the first corner, who controlled the rest of the race to claim what would have been his second win of the season. However, Ticktum ran out of fuel on his return to the pits, and his team were not able to supply a sufficient fuel sample to the [[FIA]]. Thus, Ticktum was disqualified, and Ilott inherited the win. This allowed Ilott to retake the championship lead, six points ahead of Schumacher in second.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Woolard |first1=Craig |title=Ticktum claims second F2 win in Monza sprint race |url=https://formulascout.com/ticktum-claims-second-f2-win-in-monza-sprint-race/67719 |access-date=6 September 2020 |work=formulascout.com |publisher=Formula Scout |date=6 September 2020}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |last1=Woolard |first1=Craig |title=Ilott handed F2 win after Ticktum gets disqualified for lack of fuel |url=https://formulascout.com/ilott-handed-f2-win-after-ticktum-gets-disqualified-for-lack-of-fuel/67725 |access-date=6 September 2020 |work=formulascout.com |publisher=Formula Scout |date=5 September 2020}}</ref>

===Closing rounds===

Christian Lundgaard achieved his first pole position in qualifying at [[Mugello Circuit|Mugello]] and controlled the feature race until the closing laps. Nikita Mazepin, who started 14th, was able to take advantage of an alternative tyre strategy and accidents ahead to claim the lead with two laps to go. He led a Hitech one-two at the finish line ahead of teammate Luca Ghiotto. Shwartzman, Ilott and Tsunoda all failed to score, allowing Schumacher to claim the championship lead for the first time after finishing fifth.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Allen |first1=Peter |title=Mazepin wins Mugello F2 feature race from 14th in Hitech one-two |url=https://formulascout.com/mazepin-wins-mugello-f2-feature-race-from-14th-in-hitech-one-two/68043 |access-date=12 September 2020 |work=formulascout.com |publisher=Formula Scout |date=12 September 2020}}</ref> [[HWA Racelab]]'s [[Artem Markelov]] started on pole position in the sprint race, from which Mazepin and Ghiotto were eliminated after colliding with each other. Lundgaard took the lead of the race at the start and led the rest of the distance to take his second win of the season. Schumacher finished fourth ahead of Ilott in sixth, allowing Schumacher to extend his championship lead to eight points.

In [[Sochi Autodrom|Sochi]], Tsunoda started the feature race from pole position. He was overtaken by Schumacher with nine laps remaining, who went on to take his second victory of the season. Title challenger Ilott finished third. The sprint race began with Guanyu Zhou on pole position. On lap seven of 21, Luca Ghiotto attempted an overtake on [[Campos Racing|Campos]] driver [[Jack Aitken]] for fourth place around the outside of the long turn four. The drivers made contact and both crashed into the barriers at high-speed, after which Ghiotto's car caught fire. Both drivers escaped unharmed, but the race was [[Racing flags#Red flag|red-flagged]] and not restarted since the barriers could not be repaired in time. Zhou was awarded the race win, his first in Formula 2. Half-points were given as less than 75% of the scheduled race distance was completed. After round 10, Schumacher's championship lead over Ilott had extended to 22 points.

Round 11 at the [[Bahrain International Circuit]] began with Ilott on pole position and championship leader Schumacher qualifying 10th. Ilott and Felipe Drugovich traded the lead during the feature race. Drugovich eventually built a gap and finished ahead of Ilott to claim his third victory of the season. Robert Shwartzman started the sprint race from pole position and converted this into his fourth win of the season. Ilott failed to score after colliding with Carlin's [[Jehan Daruvala]] and receiving a drive-through penalty. Schumacher finished seventh to hold his championship lead over Ilott by 14 points.

Yuki Tsunoda took his fourth pole position for the final round on Bahrain's outer layout. Ilott qualified ninth, with Schumacher only 18th after colliding with Roy Nissany in qualifying. Tsunoda, Shwartzman and Nikita Mazepin battled for the lead during the feature race, before Tsunoda built a gap and claimed his third win of the season. Ilott and Schumacher were classified fifth and sixth respectively after Mazepin was penalised for illegal defending. Schumacher recorded the fastest lap of the race, meaning his 14-point advantage in the championship went unchanged going into the final race and guaranteeing him the title if Ilott failed to finish the sprint race in the top two. Dan Ticktum took reverse-grid pole position and was later passed by Jehan Daruvala, who claimed his first Formula 2 victory. Schumacher locked his tyres multiple times during the race and made two pit stops, dropping him to the back. Ilott had run in third place but dropped back due to tyre degradation, eventually finishing outside the points. Schumacher crossed the line 18th to become the 2020 Formula 2 champion.

Three drivers would graduate to Formula One at the end of the season. Champion Schumacher and fifth-placed Mazepin joined [[Haas F1 Team]]. Third-placed Tsunoda, who won the [[Anthoine Hubert]] Award as the highest-placed rookie in the championship, joined [[Scuderia AlphaTauri]]. Runner-up Ilott joined [[Scuderia Ferrari]] as a test driver.

===Scoring system===
Points are awarded to the top 10 classified finishers in the Feature race, and to the top 8 classified finishers in the Sprint race. The pole-sitter in the feature race also receives four points, and two points is given to the driver who sets the fastest lap inside the top ten in both the feature and sprint races. No extra points are awarded to the pole-sitter in the sprint race as the grid for the sprint race is based on the results of the feature race with the top eight drivers having their positions reversed.<ref name=Regulations>{{cite web|title=2020 FIA Formula 2 Sporting Regulations|url=https://www.fia.com/sites/default/files/2020_fia_f2_sporting_regulations_v2_04-03-20.pdf|work=[[Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile]]|pages=3-4|date=4 March 2020|access-date=17 March 2025|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201028203110/https://www.fia.com/sites/default/files/2020_fia_f2_sporting_regulations_v2_04-03-20.pdf|archive-date=28 October 2020|url-status=dead}}</ref>
