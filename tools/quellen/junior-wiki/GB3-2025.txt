The '''2025 GB3 Championship''' was a [[Motorsport#Motor racing|motor racing]] championship for [[Open-wheel car|open wheel]], [[Formula racing|formula racing cars]] held across Europe. The 2025 season was the tenth organised by the [[British Racing Drivers' Club]] in the United Kingdom, and the fifth season under the GB3 moniker after rebranding from the BRDC British Formula 3 Championship in mid-2021. The championship featured a mix of professional motor racing teams and privately funded drivers. The season was run over eight triple-header rounds. It started in April and ended in October.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |date=22 August 2024 |title=GB3 Championship reveals F1-focused 2025 calendar with Monza finale |url=https://www.gb-3.net/news/2024/aug/22/gb3-championship-reveals-f1focused-2025-calendar-with-monza-finale |access-date=22 August 2024 |website=www.gb-3.net}}</ref>

[[Rodin Motorsport]] driver [[Alex Ninovic]] won the Drivers' Championship with three races to spare, while his team  defended their Teams' Championship title a race later during the final weekend.

== Teams and drivers ==
The 2025 season saw the introduction of a new chassis and engine package. === Team changes ===

[[Velocity Racing Development|VRD Racing]] became its own entry in 2025 after entering GB3 in collaboration with [[Arden International]] in 2024, while Arden discontinued their GB3 efforts.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2024-10-01 |title=USA's Velocity Racing Development team to race in GB3 under own name |url=https://formulascout.com/usas-velocity-racing-development-team-to-race-in-gb3-under-own-name/124478 |access-date=2024-10-01 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

UAE-based team Xcel Motorsport expanded into GB3 after first entering the British junior series landscape when it joined [[F4 British Championship|British F4]] in 2024.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2024-11-14 |title=Xcel Motorsport to enter GB3 in 2025 |url=https://formulascout.com/xcel-motorsport-to-enter-gb3-in-2025/125736 |access-date=2024-11-14 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

Argenti Motorsport, who had been competing in British F4 since 2020, joined GB3 in a technical partnership with Italian junior single-seater team [[Prema Racing]] under the ''Argenti with Prema'' guise.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2024-11-22 |title=Argenti Motorsport partners with Prema for 2025 GB3 entry |url=https://formulascout.com/argenti-partners-with-prema-for-2025-gb3-entry/125923 |access-date=2024-11-22 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

[[Hitech Grand Prix]] and [[Toyota Gazoo Racing]]'s [[TGR Driver Challenge Program|TGR-DC]] junior programme formed a new collaboration in 2025, entering under the ''Hitech TGR'' guise.<ref>{{Cite web|title=About TGR |url=https://www.hitechgp.co.uk/about/#Our-Partner|date=25 December 2024 |access-date=12 March 2025|website=[[Hitech GP]]|language=en-US}}</ref>

==== Mid-season ====
Chris Dittmann Racing did not enter cars at the second round, with Divy Nandan electing to move over to JHR Developments. Fortec's Mika Abrahams meanwhile left the championship.

Hitech's Nikita Johnson left the series ahead of the event at the Hungaroring due to conflicting commitments in FIA F3, while Fortec's Stefan Bostandijev also stepped away from GB3 and Divy Nandan also missed round four.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Hungary preview: Aussies lead the way to the Hungaroring |url=https://www.gb-3.net/news/2025/jun/27/hungary-preview-aussies-lead-the-way-to-the-hungaroring- |access-date=2 July 2025 |website=[[GB3 Championship]]}}</ref>

== Season report ==

=== First half ===
The 2025 GB3 Championship began at [[Silverstone Circuit|Silverstone]] with a qualifying session dominated by [[Hillspeed]]'s [[Freddie Slater (racing driver)|Freddie Slater]], who claimed both pole positions by over fourth tenths of a second and set a new GB3 lap record in session two.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2025-04-26 |title=Slater storms to double pole in first GB3 qualifying of 2025 |url=https://formulascout.com/slater-storms-to-double-pole-in-first-gb3-qualifying-of-2025/130170 |access-date=2025-10-28 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> He converted pole position into a commanding lights-to-flag victory in race one, pulling clear of [[JHR Developments|JHR]]'s [[Noah Lisle]], who took second at the start of the race, and [[Rodin Motorsport|Rodin]]'s [[Gianmarco Pradel]], who completed the podium in third.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2025-04-26 |title=Freddie Slater and Hillspeed win first race of GB3's new era |url=https://formulascout.com/freddie-slater-and-hillspeed-win-first-race-of-gb3s-new-era/130184 |access-date=2025-10-28 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Race two brought much of the same, with Slater unchallenged again. He mastered an early safety car restart ahead of Lisle and built a nine-second gap before a second interruption saw the race finish under caution. Lisle repeated his second place, with [[Hitech Grand Prix|Hitech]] [[Toyota Gazoo Racing|TGR]]'s [[Nikita Johnson]] third.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2025-04-27 |title=Freddie Slater cruises to second GB3 win at Silverstone |url=https://formulascout.com/freddie-slater-cruises-to-second-gb3-win-at-silverstone/130209 |access-date=2025-10-28 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> The reversed-grid finale went to [[Elite Motorsport]]’s [[William Macintyre]], who led from the first lap and finished ahead of Xcel Motorsport's [[Patrick Heuzenroeder]] and JHR's [[Kai Daryanani]] after a race punctuated by multiple incidents. Slater left the first round with a 15-point championship lead over Lisle.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2025-04-27 |title=Elite's Will Macintyre wins GB3’s reversed-grid race at Silverstone |url=https://formulascout.com/elites-will-macintyre-wins-gb3s-reversed-grid-race-at-silverstone/130230 |access-date=2025-10-28 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

Heuzenroeder and Rodin's [[Alex Ninovic]] claimed their first GB3 pole positions in qualifying at [[Circuit Zandvoort|Zandvoort]], with Heuzenroeder topping the opening session and Ninovic fastest in the second.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2025-05-17 |title=Heuzenroeder and Ninovic share GB3 poles at Zandvoort |url=https://formulascout.com/heuzenroeder-and-ninovic-share-gb3-poles-at-zandvoort/130950 |access-date=2025-10-28 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Race one began with an aborted start, but when it got underway, polesitter Heuzenroeder controlled proceedings, claiming a maiden win both for him and his team, ahead of Hitech TGR's [[Deagen Fairclough]] and Pradel.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2025-05-18 |title=Rodin’s Alex Ninovic secures first GB3 win at Zandvoort |url=https://formulascout.com/rodins-alex-ninovic-secures-first-gb3-win-at-zandvoort/131090 |access-date=2025-10-28 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Ninovic also took his maiden win a race later, with Heuzenroeder second after front-row starter Fairclough had a bad start, fell to fourth behind Argenti with [[Prema Racing|Prema]]'s [[Reza Seewooruthun]] and recovered to third.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2025-05-18 |title=Rodin’s Alex Ninovic secures first GB3 win at Zandvoort |url=https://formulascout.com/rodins-alex-ninovic-secures-first-gb3-win-at-zandvoort/131090 |access-date=2025-10-28 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> [[Velocity Racing Development|VRD Racing]]'s [[Hugo Schwarze]] made it three maiden winners out of three races in the reversed-grid final by starting fourth, climbing to the lead within the first two laps and controlling two safety car restarts. Macintyre and Hitech TGR's [[Keanu Al Azhari]] completed the podium. With championship leader Slater on a part-time schedule and not present at round two, Heuzenroeder claimed the championship lead, 14 points ahead of Ninovic.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2025-05-18 |title=VRD’s Schwarze becomes GB3 winner in chaotic reversed-grid race |url=https://formulascout.com/vrds-schwarze-becomes-gb3-winner-in-chaotic-reversed-grid-race/131110 |access-date=2025-10-28 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

Qualifying at [[Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps|Spa-Francorchamps]] was disrupted by red flags. Ninovic and Hillspeed's [[Kanato Le]] claimed a pole position each as championship contenders Slater and Fairclough both failed to set times after separate incidents, consigning them to the back for all three races.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2025-05-30 |title=Ninovic and Le share poles as rivals hit trouble in GB3 Spa qualifying |url=https://formulascout.com/ninovic-and-le-share-poles-as-rivals-hit-trouble-in-gb3-spa-qualifying/131515 |access-date=2025-11-04 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Ninovic converted his race one pole into a controlled lights-to-flag victory, surviving two safety car periods to finish ahead of Schwarze and Le. Slater climbed from last to tenth after an early charge through the field.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2025-05-31 |title=Ninovic claims GB3 lead with lights-to-flag Spa win |url=https://formulascout.com/ninovic-claims-gb3-lead-with-lights-to-flag-spa-win/131532 |access-date=2025-11-04 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Le made amends in race two, recovering from a slow start that dropped him to fourth at the start to take his maiden win ahead of Johnson and Ninovic, with Al Azhari and Hillspeed's [[Hiyu Yamakoshi]] rounding out the top five.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2025-05-31 |title=Le recovers from slow start at Spa to take maiden GB3 win |url=https://formulascout.com/le-recovers-from-slow-start-at-spa-to-take-maiden-gb3-win/131558 |access-date=2025-11-04 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> The reversed-grid finale was run on a drying track, and Slater started on slick tyres to produce a remarkable charge: he started in 23rd and claimed the lead by lap six to lead Le and Seewooruthun home. Ninovic retired late on, but still ended the weekend as the new championship leader, eight points ahead of Heuzenroeder.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2025-06-01 |title=Slater storms from last to first to win GB3's damp Spa finale |url=https://formulascout.com/slater-storms-from-last-to-first-to-win-gb3s-damp-spa-finale/131614 |access-date=2025-11-04 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

Al Azhari and Ninovic were fastest in qualifying at the [[Hungaroring]], with Al Azhari securing his maiden pole position for race one before Ninovic edged him by 0.024 seconds in Q2 to claim his third of the season.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2025-07-05 |title=Ninovic and Al Azhari share GB3 poles at Hungaroring |url=https://formulascout.com/ninovic-and-al-azhari-share-gb3-poles-at-hungaroring/132775 |access-date=2025-11-04 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Pradel took his first GB3 victory in the opening race, jumping Al Azhari off the line and leading throughout despite a mid-race safety car caused by a retirement for Elite's [[Flynn Jackes]]. Macintyre finished second ahead of Al Azhari.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2025-07-05 |title=Pradel powers to maiden GB3 triumph in Hungary |url=https://formulascout.com/pradel-powers-to-maiden-gb3-triumph-in-hungary/132820 |access-date=2025-11-04 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Ninovic responded in race two by converting pole into a lights-to-flag win, surviving an early safety car triggered by a collision between Xcel's [[Jack Sherwood (racing driver)|Jack Sherwood]] and Daryanani to finish 1.8 seconds clear of Al Azhari, while Pradel took third ahead of Lisle and Macintyre.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2025-07-06 |title=Ninovic extends points lead with third GB3 win in Hungary |url=https://formulascout.com/ninovic-extends-points-lead-with-third-gb3-win-in-hungary/132879 |access-date=2025-11-04 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> The reversed-grid finale was dominated by Seewooruthun, who surged from third to the lead at turn two and took his and his team's maiden win by over 16 seconds ahead of Daryanani and Schwarze. A win and two top-ten finishes in the three races saw Ninovic bolster his championship lead to 34 points at the halfway point of the season.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2025-07-06 |title=Seewooruthun and Argenti sail to maiden win in GB3's Hungary finale |url=https://formulascout.com/seewooruthun-and-argenti-sail-to-maiden-win-in-gb3s-hungary-finale/132916 |access-date=2025-11-04 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

=== Second half ===
Ninovic topped both qualifying sessions on GB3's return to Silverstone, leading Seewooruthun and Fairclough on the respective front rows.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2025-08-02 |title=GB3 leader Ninovic takes dominant double pole at Silverstone |url=https://formulascout.com/gb3-leader-ninovic-takes-dominant-double-pole-at-silverstone/133513 |access-date=2025-11-20 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> He opened race one by converting his pole position into a comfortable victory after a second formation lap shortened the distance, extending his lead as Pradel moved past Fairclough and later won a multi-car fight for second ahead of the recovering Al Azhari.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2025-08-02 |title=Ninovic sails to his fourth GB3 success at Silverstone |url=https://formulascout.com/ninovic-sails-to-his-fourth-gb3-success-at-silverstone/133539 |access-date=2025-11-20 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Race two was interrupted early by a safety car following a retirement for Rodin's [[Abbi Pulling]], and the lead changed several times between Ninovic and Fairclough before they collided at Brooklands, eliminating both from contention. Al Azhari, having twice passed Heuzenroeder, assumed the lead and claimed his maiden win from Heuzenroeder and Hilspeed's returnee [[Michael Shin]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2025-08-03 |title=Al Azhari takes maiden GB3 win as title rivals collide |url=https://formulascout.com/al-azhari-takes-maiden-gb3-win-as-title-rivals-collide/133570 |access-date=2025-11-20 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Pradel then led every lap of the reversed-grid finale from pole, holding off Sherwood before Seewooruthun advanced to second and Lisle completed the podium. Ninovic climbed to fourth after starting eleventh to slightly extend his championship lead to 39 points.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2025-08-03 |title=Pradel wins lights-to-flag in GB3 race three at Silverstone |url=https://formulascout.com/pradel-wins-lights-to-flag-in-gb3-race-three-at-silverstone/133605 |access-date=2025-11-20 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

Qualifying for round six at [[Brands Hatch]] saw Ninovic continue his one-lap dominance as he claimed another two pole positions, both ahead of Slater.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2025-08-23 |title=Ninovic sets scorching pace to take double GB3 pole at Brands Hatch |url=https://formulascout.com/ninovic-sets-scorching-pace-to-take-double-gb3-pole-at-brands-hatch/133991 |access-date=2025-11-20 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> In race one, Ninovic held the lead from the start while Slater recovered from a poor launch to reclaim second with a move around Fairclough and Heuzenroeder at Druids. Ninovic extended his margin to four seconds before a safety car was triggered by Sherwood’s off at Stirling’s, then controlled the three-lap restart to win ahead of Slater and Fairclough.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2025-08-23 |title=Ninovic extends GB3 lead with commanding win at Brands Hatch |url=https://formulascout.com/ninovic-extends-gb3-lead-with-commanding-win-at-brands-hatch/134010 |access-date=2025-11-20 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Race two began after a long delay; Ninovic again kept Slater at bay before an opening-lap collision eliminated Al Azhari. After the restart on lap five, Ninovic gradually pulled clear to win by two seconds, with Slater and Fairclough repeating their podium positions. The reversed-grid finale went to VRD's [[Enzo Tarnvanichkul]], who led Jack Sherwood before a safety car finish secured his maiden win. Pulling took third as non-scores for Pradel, Fairclough and Heuzenroeder coupled with Ninovic's two wins saw his championship lead grow sharply to 71 points.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2025-08-24 |title=Red Bull’s Tarnvanichkul takes maiden GB3 win at Brands Hatch |url=https://formulascout.com/red-bulls-tarnvanichkul-takes-maiden-gb3-win-at-brands-hatch/134086 |access-date=2025-11-20 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

The penultimate round at [[Donington Park]] saw Ninovic and Seewooruthun share pole positions and front rows in qualifying.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2025-10-04 |title=Ninovic and Seewooruthun share GB3 poles at Donington Park |url=https://formulascout.com/ninovic-and-seewooruthun-share-gb3-poles-at-donington-park/134997 |access-date=2025-11-20 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Ninovic started race one leading into Redgate and extended his margin before a safety car when [[Fortec Motorsport|Fortec]]'s debutant [[Liam McNeilly]] spun into the gravel trap. Ninovic rebuilt his advantage after the restart while Lisle and Fairclough exchanged third. Lisle eventually prevailed, and a later incident for Al Azhari brought a second safety car that ended the race under neutralisation.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2025-10-04 |title=Ninovic closes in on GB3 crown with seventh win of 2025 |url=https://formulascout.com/ninovic-closes-in-on-gb3-crown-with-seventh-win-of-2025/135028 |access-date=2025-11-20 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Race two began with Ninovic jumping the start, earning a ten-second penalty, but he pulled clear either side of a brief safety car and stretched his margin sufficiently to retain victory once the penalty was applied, with Seewooruthun second and Fairclough third.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2025-10-05 |title=Ninovic overcomes 10s penalty to score eighth GB3 victory |url=https://formulascout.com/ninovic-overcomes-10s-penalty-to-score-eighth-gb3-victory/135060 |access-date=2025-11-20 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Hillspeed's [[Lucas Fluxá]] led throughout the reversed-grid finale as an opening-lap collision caused a lengthy caution. Fluxa stayed ahead to beat JHR's [[Bart Harrison]] and McNeilly, while Ninovic rose to seventh to seal the championship title by moving 113 points clear of Heuzenroeder.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2025-10-05 |title=Ninovic seals GB3 crown as Fluxa wins race three at Donington Park |url=https://formulascout.com/ninovic-seals-gb3-crown-as-fluxa-wins-race-three-at-donington-park/135094 |access-date=2025-11-20 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

GB3 debuted at [[Monza Circuit|Monza]] for its season finale, and champion Ninovic claimed another two pole positions.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2025-10-18 |title=Ninovic storms to another double pole at GB3's Monza finale |url=https://formulascout.com/ninovic-storms-to-another-double-pole-at-gb3s-monza-finale/135369 |access-date=2025-12-04 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> In race one, he retained the lead at the restart after an opening-lap red flag triggered by multiple incidents. A brief safety car followed a spin for Elite's [[Bianca Bustamante]], and Ninovic headed a six-car train thereafter before edging clear on the final lap to defeat Heuzenroeder and Pradel.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2025-10-18 |title=Ninovic wins in Italy in GB3's inaugural Monza race |url=https://formulascout.com/ninovic-wins-in-italy-in-gb3s-inaugural-monza-race/135380 |access-date=2025-12-04 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Race two began with after contact that forced Ninovic, Lisle and Fairclough to miss the first chicane and reshuffled the order. Further collisions eliminated eight drivers as Fairclough was able to claim the lead from Ninovic throughout successive restarts. He stayed ahead to take victory in front of Ninovic and Pradel.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2025-10-19 |title=Fairclough takes maiden GB3 victory in wild race two at Monza |url=https://formulascout.com/fairclough-takes-maiden-gb3-victory-in-wild-race-two-at-monza/135401 |access-date=2025-12-04 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Hillspeed's debutant [[Maxim Rehm]] prevailed in the reversed-grid finale after passing Tarnvanichkul early on and resisting Fairclough and Pradel in a long slipstream train, sealing his first victory. Heuzenroeder recovered to sixth to secure second in the standings, while Ninovic rose from 19th to seventh to conclude the season on 524 points.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2025-10-19 |title=Rehm wins on third GB3 start at Monza as season draws to a close |url=https://formulascout.com/rehm-wins-on-third-gb3-start-at-monza-as-season-draws-to-a-close/135429 |access-date=2025-12-04 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

Across the season, Ninovic established clear superiority both in one-lap and race pace, taking nine wins, ten pole positions and two additional podiums to secure the title by 148 points, the largest margin in several years. Thirteen different drivers won races, among them a dominant double win for Slater in the opening Silverstone round, while further maiden victories for Le, Al Azhari, Tarnvanichkul, Fluxá and Rehm underlined the depth of the field. Frequent reversed-grid unpredictability and changing weather at Spa produced several standout recoveries, and incidents at Zandvoort, Silverstone and Monza contributed to a high number of safety-car-affected races. Despite this variability, Ninovic’s run of front-row starts and controlled race-management proved decisive, ensuring that no full-season rival - Heuzenroeder, Pradel or Fairclough among them - could mount a sustained challenge as the championship was settled with a round to spare.
