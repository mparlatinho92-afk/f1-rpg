The '''2026 Eurocup-3 season''' is the fourth season of the Eurocup-3 series. Eurocup-3 is a multi-event motor racing championship for single-seater open wheel formula racing cars held across Europe, created in 2023 as an alternative to the FIA-sanctioned [[Formula Regional European Championship]] and the [[Euroformula Open Championship|Euroformula Open championship]].<ref name="EC3Launch">{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |title=New Eurocup-3 series launched to rival Euroformula and FREC |url=https://formulascout.com/new-eurocup-3-series-launched-to-rival-euroformula-and-frec/100901 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-GB |date=17 November 2022 |access-date=30 July 2025}}</ref>

2026 also saw the second edition of the Eurocup-3 Spanish Winter Championship, held across the [[Iberian Peninsula]] in February and March.<ref name=":1">{{Cite web |last=elena |title=Spanish Winter Championship confirms its 2026 calendar |url=https://spanishwinterchampionship.com/newsarticle/spanish-winter-championship-confirms-its-2026-calendar/ |access-date=2025-11-21 |website=spanish winter championship |language=en-US}}</ref>

[[Álex Palou|Palou Motorsport]] driver [[James Egozi]] dominated the championship, winning eight races before clinching the Drivers' Championship title at [[Circuito de Jerez|Jerez]] with two race weekends to spare.

== Main series ==

=== Teams and drivers ===
The 2026 season saw the championship enter its second generation of technical regulations. This saw the championship change both its chassis and engine supplier. After previously using a slightly revised [[Tatuus F3 T-318]] chassis, 2026 saw the debut of the [[Dallara 326]] chassis, an evolution of the Dallara 324 currently used by the [[Euroformula Open Championship|Euroformula Open]] and [[Super Formula Lights]]. Like in these two series, the car is powered by a [[Toyota]] three-cylinder 1.6-litre turbo engine, with Eurocup-3 discontinuing its collaboration with [[Alfa Romeo]].<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |last1=Tirado |first1=Seb |last2=Muschlien |first2=Finjo |title=Eurocup-3 in 2026: A Dallara car, Madring debut and new teams revealed |url=https://feederseries.net/2025/07/30/eurocup-3-in-2026-a-dallara-car-madring-debut-and-new-teams-revealed/ |website=Feeder Series |language=en |date=30 July 2025 |access-date=30 July 2025}}</ref>

=== Race calendar ===
The provisional calendar was announced on 30 July 2025.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Eurocup-3 on Instagram: 'A new era begins.' |url=https://www.instagram.com/eurocup_3/p/DMu1njxNfnA/ |website=[[Instagram]] |date=30 July 2025 |access-date=30 July 2025}}</ref> That first draft included the new [[Spanish Grand Prix]] venue [[Madring]], even though the circuit denied that any agreement regarding the Eurocup-3 round was achieved.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Chambenoit |first=Mathieu |date=31 July 2025 |title=Madring denies the presence of Eurocup-3 on the 2026 calendar |url=https://www.autohebdof1.com/actualites/monoplace/le-madring-dement-la-presence-de-leurocup-3-au-calendrier-2026.html |access-date=31 July 2025 |website=Autohebdo}}</ref> An updated version of the calendar was released on 4 December 2025, where the planned Madring event was not included, with one venue still to be announced.<ref>{{Cite web |date=4 December 2025 |title=Eurocup-3 on Instagram: 'Introducing the 2026 Eurocup-3 calendar. 🗓️' |url=https://www.instagram.com/eurocup_3/p/DR1zPWYjMqs/ |access-date=4 December 2025 |website=[[Instagram]]}}</ref> The sixth round was later confirmed to be held at [[Circuito de Jerez]] which will be held two weeks after the round at [[Silverstone Circuit]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Eurocup-3 updates its 2026 calendar |url=https://eurocup3.org/eurocup-3-updates-its-2026-calendar/ |website=[[Eurocup-3]] |language=en-US |date=20 May 2026 |access-date=20 May 2026}}</ref> For the first time ever, the series is set to visit the United Kingdom and Hungary, with its fifth round taking place at [[Silverstone Circuit]] and its seventh round at the [[Hungaroring]]. The series will also debut at [[Imola Circuit]], while [[TT Circuit Assen]], [[Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps]] and the [[Red Bull Ring]] all left the schedule. Three of the rounds will be held using the three-round format that adds a sprint race to the weekend.

=== Season report ===

==== First half ====
The 2026 Eurocup-3 season began its main season at the [[Circuit Paul Ricard]] with [[Hitech Grand Prix|Hitech]]'s [[Keanu Al Azhari]] and [[MP Motorsport|MP]]'s [[Gianmarco Pradel]] sharing pole positions in qualifying. Al Azhari stalled from pole position in the opening race, handing the lead to MP's [[René Lammers]] as [[Campos Racing|Griffin Core]]'s [[Alex Powell]] overtook [[Palou Motorsport|Palou]]'s [[James Egozi]] for second. Egozi quickly retook second and closed up to Lammers, before he claimed the lead with five minutes to go to take victory. Reversed-grid pole position went to Griffin Core's [[Enzo Tarnvanichkul]], but [[Drivex]] driver [[Christopher El Feghali]] overtook him on the first lap. He maintained his lead to secure his first career single-seater victory, with Tarnvanichkul second as Egozi rose up the order to third. Race three began with Egozi jumping polesitter Pradel and MP's [[Ean Eyckmans]] to take the lead. Pradel then retired with a mechanical issue before Al Azhari and Egozi spent several laps battling for the lead. Al Azhari got in front in the end to claim victory, with Egozi taking second to end the weekend with a 13-point championship lead. Behind the pair, Powell, Lammers and Eyckmans battled over third place, with Powell taking it on the road before a post-race penalty handed the place to Eyckmans.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Awcock |first=Jacob |date=2026-05-05 |title=Egozi the early Eurocup-3 leader as one of three Paul Ricard winners |url=https://formulascout.com/egozi-the-early-eurocup-3-leader-as-one-of-three-paul-ricard-winners/139200 |access-date=2026-05-14 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=FormulaRapida.net |date=2026-05-05 |title=Egozi Leads Eurocup-3 After Thrilling Opener at Paul Ricard |url=https://formularapida.net/en/egozi-leads-eurocup-3-after-thrilling-opener-at-paul-ricard/ |access-date=2026-05-14 |website=FormulaRapida.net |language=en-US}}</ref>

Round two at [[Algarve International Circuit|Portimão]] began with El Feghali taking pole position for the first race. He and Al Azhari had slow starts, allowing Lammers to take the lead. Egozi slotted into second, but attacked Lammers right away and took the lead at the end of the opening lap. El Feghali dropped to fourth, before hitting Powell while trying to move back into third. Powell was forced to retire, and El Feghali was handed a post-race penalty that handed third to Palou's [[Heitor Dall'Agnol]]. Egozi won the race before continuing his advantage into Sunday by taking pole position for race two. He held the lead at the start ahead of Eyckmans and controlled proceedings from then on. Three safety car phases punctured the race, but Egozi remained faultless to take his second victory of the weekend. Dall'Agnol took second ahead of Al Azhari, who was now 44 points behind leader Egozi in the standings.<ref>{{Cite web |last= |first= |date=2026-06-08 |title=Feeder Series weekend review, results and standings: 04–07 June 2026 |url=https://feederseries.net/2026/06/08/feeder-series-weekend-review-results-and-standings-04-07-june-2026/ |access-date=2026-06-23 |website=Feeder Series |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Chambenoit |first=Mathieu |date=2026-06-06 |title=Portimão - Race 1: Egozi wins, Pérard scores points |url=https://www.autohebdof1.com/news/car/eurocup/portimao-race-1-egozi-wins-perard-scores-points.html |access-date=2026-06-23 |website=AutoHebdo |language=en}}</ref>

Eurocup-3's debut at [[Imola Circuit]] began with Egozi claiming pole position in both qualifying sessions. The first race saw Egozi initially hold on to his lead throughout two safety car periods, before a mechanical issue forced him into retirement on lap eleven. That handed the lead to Dall'Agnol, who won on the road, but was handed a post-race penalty for an incident on the opening lap. Eyckmans inherited his maiden victory ahead of Powell, with Dall'Agnol dropping to third. The sprint race began with Tarnvanichkul stalling, before he was rear-ended and a five-car crash ensued, causing a red flag. Heuzenroeder led the restart from reversed-grid pole position and managed the rest of the race to take his maiden win. Mistakes for Tecnicar's [[Kacper Sztuka]] and Al Azhari dropped them out of podium contention, which, coupled with a post-race penalty for TC Racing's [[Juan Cota]], saw Hitech's [[Gino Trappa]] and Pradel take second and third. Egozi was back in front for race three and took an unchallenged lights-to-flag victory. Behind him, teammate [[Rafaël Pérard]] spent the race fending off Sztuka to take his maiden podium. Al Azhari in fourth dropped to third in the standings, behind Eyckmans, who now trailed Egozi by 56 points.<ref>{{Cite web |last= |first= |date=2026-07-06 |title=Feeder Series weekend review, results and standings: 03–05 July 2026 |url=https://feederseries.net/2026/07/06/feeder-series-weekend-review-results-and-standings-03-05-july-2026/ |access-date=2026-07-24 |website=Feeder Series |language=en}}</ref>

The first half of the season ended at [[Monza Circuit]] began with Pérard following up his first podium with a maiden pole position for race one. Slow starts for Powell and Pradel from second and third saw Egozi climb from seventh to fourth as Pérard held the lead. Egozi continued his rise up the order with moves on Heuzenroeder and Al Azhari, before passing Pérard on lap ten. He fought back, but Egozi was able to hold him behind as Lammers claimed the final podium spot from El Feghali. The latter took pole position for race two and defended from Egozi, who locked up and fell to sixth. Dall'Agnol then attacked for the lead before making contact with Pérard and then with El Feghali, taking both out of the race. He then lost out to a recovering Egozi, who took his sixth victory of 2026 to grow his lead to 93 points as a post-race penalty promoted Lammers to second and Sztuka to third.<ref>{{Cite web |last= |first= |date=2026-08-03 |title=Feeder Series weekend review, results and standings: 30 July–2 August 2026 |url=https://feederseries.net/2026/08/03/feeder-series-weekend-review-results-and-standings-30-july-2-august-2026/ |access-date=2026-09-08 |website=Feeder Series |language=en}}</ref>

==== Second half ====
The series debuted at [[Silverstone Circuit]] for its fifth round of the season, and Al Azhari took pole position in both qualifying sessions. Eyckmans started race one in second, but dropped behind Lammers at the start, who attacked Al Azhari until the safety car was deployed. On the restart, Al Azhari was able to pull away and took victory, while Egozi took the final podium spot off Eyckmans. Wet conditions for the second race meant Al Azhari and second-placed Pradel both had slow starts, allowing Egozi to move from fourth to first in the opening lap. He gapped the field as a spin cost Pradel third place, handing it to Eyckmans. With three laps to go, an issue with Al Azhari's front wing saw him drop down the order. Eyckmans took second and Dall’Agnol third, with Al Azhari finishing seventh, allowing Egozi to again extend his championship lead to 107 points over Eyckmans.<ref>{{Cite web |last= |first= |date=2026-09-14 |title=Feeder Series weekend review, results and standings: 11–13 September 2026 |url=https://feederseries.net/2026/09/14/feeder-series-weekend-review-results-and-standings-11-13-september-2026/ |access-date=2026-09-24 |website=Feeder Series |language=en}}</ref>

== Winter series ==
{{Motorsport season|series=Eurocup-3|title=[[Eurocup-3]] Spanish Winter Championship|year=2026|previous=2025|footer=<small>Feeder series:<br>[[2026 F4 Spanish Championship#Winter series|Eurocup-4 Spanish Winter Championship]]</small>|subheader=<small>Drivers' Champion: </small>[[Keanu Al Azhari]]<br><small>Rookie Champion: </small>[[René Lammers]]<br><small>Teams' Champion: </small>[[Campos Racing]]|previouslink=2025 Eurocup-3 season#Winter series|nextlink=2027 Eurocup-3 season#Winter series}}

The second running of the Eurocup-3 Spanish Winter Championship was held over three weekends and comprised nine races. [[Hitech Grand Prix|Hitech]] driver [[Keanu Al Azhari]] won the Drivers' Championship title with a race to spare. [[MP Motorsport]]'s [[René Lammers]] came third overall to claim the Rookie Championship, while [[Campos Racing]] won the Teams' Championship.

=== Teams and drivers ===
Teams utilized the same [[Dallara 326]] chassis powered by a [[Toyota]] three-cylinder 1.6-litre turbo engine and running [[Hankook]] tires used in the main series.

=== Season report ===
The opening weekend of the 2026 winter season took place at the [[Algarve International Circuit]], and [[MP Motorsport]]'s [[Ean Eyckmans]] took pole position in the first qualifying session. The first Eurocup-3 race using the [[Dallara 326]] began with multiple cars stalling off the line, among them polesitter Eyckmans and [[Campos Racing]]'s P3 starter [[Alex Powell]]. That saw Campos's [[Thomas Strauven]] take the lead, before [[Álex Palou|Palou Motorsport]]'s [[James Egozi]] overtook him later in the lap. Multiple safety car phases followed before [[Hitech Grand Prix|Hitech]]'s [[Keanu Al Azhari]] and MP's [[René Lammers]] both moved past Strauven to complete the podium behind Egozi. [[Thibaut Courtois|TC Racing]]'s [[Luca Viișoreanu]] had reversed-grid pole position for race two, but Hitech's [[Gino Trappa]] and Egozi moved past him straight away. Egozi claimed the lead later on, before contact sent Trappa down the order, allowing Al Azhari and Powell to claim podium spots. Qualifying for the third race saw Al Azhari take pole position. He initially lost out to Eyckmans at the start, but shadowed him before retaking the lead on lap two. The top two remained static afterwards while Lammers secured third. Egozi finished outside the points after stalling at the start, so Al Azhari claimed the championship lead.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Scout |first=Formula |date=2026-02-22 |title=New Eurocup-3 era begins with winter series wins by Egozi and Al Azhari |url=https://formulascout.com/new-eurocup-3-era-begins-with-winter-series-wins-by-egozi-and-al-azhari/138154 |access-date=2026-02-26 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

Round two was held at [[Circuito del Jarama]], where MP's [[Gianmarco Pradel]] narrowly beat Al Azhari and Eyckmans to pole position for the opening race.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2026-02-28 |title=Pradel pips Al Azhari and Eyckmans to Eurocup-3 pole |url=https://formulascout.com/pradel-pips-al-azhari-and-eyckmans-to-eurocup-3-pole/138262 |access-date=2026-03-04 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Eyckmans stalled at the start, allowing Lammers into third place as Pradel locked up and came under attack from Al Azhari. Two safety car phases punctured the race, and the Emirati tried claiming the lead on each restart before finally getting past Pradel on lap 15 and winning the race. The reversed grid for race two saw the [[Drivex]] of [[Christopher El Feghali]] start in first place, but a slow start saw Hitech's [[Stefan Bostandjiev]] take the lead right away before El Feghali hit two cars entering the first turn, causing a red flag. Bostandjiev held the lead at the restart ahead of Palou's [[Heitor Dall'Agnol]] and Powell, and was handed victory when the race finished under safety car conditions. Al Azhari claimed pole position for race three, but an off into turn two saw him drop to the back as Lammers took the lead. Powell overtook him mid-race and finished first, but post-race penalties saw him and Lammers dropped down the order. Egozi inherited the win ahead of teammate [[Rafaël Pérard]] and Campos's [[Enzo Tarnvanichkul]], with Al Azhari recovering to fifth to retain a 33-point standings lead.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2026-03-02 |title=Al Azhari, Bostandjiev and Egozi share Eurocup-3 winter series wins |url=https://formulascout.com/al-azhari-bostandjiev-and-egozi-share-eurocup-3-winter-series-wins/138299 |access-date=2026-03-04 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

The winter series’ final weekend at [[MotorLand Aragón]] began with a third pole position for Al Azhari. [[Double R Racing]]’s [[Bart Harrison]] started alongside him, but Egozi quickly got past him on the opening lap before taking the lead on lap two. Al Azhari fought back, with the pair swapping first place throughout multiple safety car interruptions and restarts. Egozi took victory in the end to prolong the championship fight as Lammers beat Powell to third place.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2026-03-14 |title=Egozi beats Al Azhari to Eurocup-3 victory to tighten their title fight |url=https://formulascout.com/egozi-beats-al-azhari-to-eurocup-3-victory-to-tighten-their-title-fight/138773 |access-date=2026-03-25 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Pole position for the sprint race went to Pradel ahead of El Feghali, with Lammers quickly climbing to third at the start. He and Campos’s [[Patrick Heuzenroeder]] attacked El Feghali for second before contact between the latter two saw both drop out of contention. Lammers took second, with Al Azhari clinching the title in third after Egozi also retired following contact with Harrison. Tarnvanichkul took pole position for the final race. El Feghali started second and the top two remained static until the latter began falling down the order. Al Azhari started 16th and spent the race rising up the order, finishing third behind Pradel after on-track podium finishers Lammers and Powell incurred penalties. Egozi failed to start the final race and so Al Azhari ended up with a 52-point lead.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2026-03-16 |title=Keanu Al Azhari wins Eurocup-3's winter series with a race to spare |url=https://formulascout.com/keanu-al-azhari-wins-eurocup-3s-winter-series-with-a-race-to-spare/138860 |access-date=2026-03-25 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>
