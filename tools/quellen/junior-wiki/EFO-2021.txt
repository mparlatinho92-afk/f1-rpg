The '''2021 Euroformula Open Championship''' was a multi-event motor racing championship for single-seater open wheel formula racing cars that was held across Europe. The championship featured drivers competing in [[Euroformula Open Championship]] specification Dallara 320 chassis cars. It was the eighth Euroformula Open Championship season.

== Teams and drivers ==
All teams utilized the [[Dallara 320]] chassis.

== Race calendar ==
A provisional seven-round calendar was announced on 26 September 2020, with the option to add another round at [[Hockenheimring]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=GT Sport announces provisional 2021 race calendar|url=http://www.euroformulaopen.net/en/news/4052/gt-sport-announces-provisional-2021-race-calendar|access-date=2 November 2020|website=www.euroformulaopen.net|language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|last=Wood|first=Elliot|date=26 September 2020|title=Pau Grand Prix set to continue with Euroformula in 2021|url=https://formulascout.com/pau-grand-prix-set-to-continue-with-euroformula-in-2021/68709|access-date=2 November 2020|website=Formula Scout|language=en-GB}}</ref> A plan to resurrect the [[Pau Grand Prix]] after it was cancelled in 2020 due to the [[COVID-19 pandemic]] did not come to fruition.<ref>{{Cite web|date=5 November 2020|title=Pau Grand Prix organiser calls off next year's race|url=https://formulascout.com/pau-grand-prix-organiser-calls-off-next-years-race/70550|access-date=5 November 2020|website=Formula Scout|language=en-GB}}</ref> The calendar was then further concretized on 3 December 2020, adding an event in [[Autodromo Internazionale Enzo e Dino Ferrari|Imola]] and leaving a ninth event to be announced.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Euroformula Open welcomes Imola in its 2021 calendar and confirms Barcelona Winter Test|url=http://www.euroformulaopen.net/en/news/4154/euroformula-open-welcomes-imola-in-its-2021-calendar-and-confirms-barcelona-winter-test|access-date=3 December 2020|website=www.euroformulaopen.net|language=en}}</ref> On 9 March 2021, the Portimão round shifted one week later, and it would be the official support round of the [[2021 Portuguese Grand Prix]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Euroformula Open confirmed as support series to the Portuguese GP on 1-2 May|url=https://www.euroformulaopen.net/en/news/4205/euroformula-open-confirmed-as-support-series-to-the-portuguese-gp-on-1-2-may|access-date=9 March 2021|website=www.euroformulaopen.net|language=en}}</ref> On 27 April 2021, it was confirmed that the series would be held over eight rounds.<ref name=":1" />

In early 2021, it was announced that for the 2021 season the series would switch to a three-race format per round, with only one qualifying session and the other two race grids determined by a top-six reversed grid and the fastest laps from the first two races respectively.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web|last=Wood|first=Elliot|date=8 February 2021|title=Euroformula set for three-race weekend format and Macau|url=https://formulascout.com/euroformula-set-for-three-race-weekend-format-and-macau/74954|access-date=8 February 2021|website=Formula Scout|language=en-GB}}</ref>
