'''Ricardo Escotto Lechuga''' (born 14 December 2004) is a Mexican [[Auto racing|racing driver]] who last competed in [[FIA Formula 3 Championship|FIA Formula 3]] for [[AIX Racing]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=24 August 2023 |title=Driver: Ricardo Escotto |url=https://www.driverdb.com/drivers/ricardo-escotto}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=AIX Racing confirms Ricardo Escotto as replacement for Brad Benavides in Barcelona |url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/ |access-date=2026-06-09 |website=FIAFormula3® - The Official F3® Website |language=en}}</ref>

== Career ==

=== Formula 4 UAE Championship ===
Following a three-year hiatus from racing, Escotto signed with [[Cram Motorsport|Cram]] – [[Hitech Grand Prix|Hitech GP]] to compete in the [[2022 Formula 4 UAE Championship]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Escotto con Cram nella Formula 4 UAE - F4 NEL MONDO |url=https://www.italiaracing.net/Escotto-con-Cram-nella-Formula-4-UAE/245038/87 |access-date=2025-05-04 |website=www.italiaracing.net |language=it-IT}}</ref> He had a difficult season with no points and finishing 25th in the standings.

=== F4 Spanish Championship ===
For the 2022 season, Escotto Joined [[Cram Motorsport|Cram]]-[[Pinnacle Motorsport (Filipino auto racing team)|Pinnacle]] to compete full-time in the [[2022 F4 Spanish Championship]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=staff |date=2022-04-25 |title=Cram Motorsport con Ricardo Escotto nel Formula 4 Spanish Championship |url=https://www.crammotorsport.com/cram-motorsport-con-ricardo-escotto-nel-formula-4-spanish-championship/ |access-date=2025-05-04 |language=it-IT}}</ref> He had a best result of ninth twice at [[MotorLand Aragón|Aragón]] and finished 22nd in the championship.

=== Italian F4 Championship ===
Alongside his campaign in the F4 Spanish Championship, Escotto competed in select rounds of the [[2022 Italian F4 Championship]] with [[Cram Motorsport]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=AKM conferma Clerot e Akhmedkhodjaev per Imola - F4 ITALIA |url=https://www.italiaracing.net/AKM-conferma-Clerot-e-Akhmedkhodjaev-per-Imola/245801/79 |access-date=2025-05-04 |website=www.italiaracing.net |language=it-IT}}</ref>

=== USF Pro 2000 Championship ===

==== 2023 ====
For 2023, Escotto made the switch to America, competing in the [[2023 USF Pro 2000 Championship]] with [[Jay Howard Driver Development]]. He would get his first win in the championship at [[Indianapolis Motor Speedway|Indianapolis]], finishing ahead of his teammate [[Reece Ushijima]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2023-05-13 |title=Ricardo Escotto becomes USF Pro 2000 race-winner in bizzare fashion |url=https://formulascout.com/ricardo-escotto-becomes-usf-pro-2000-race-winner-in-bizzare-fashion/106867 |access-date=2025-05-04 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Escotto wouldn't finish on the podium again in the remainder of the season, and finished thirteenth in the standings.

==== 2024 ====
Escotto returned for the [[2024 USF Pro 2000 Championship|2024 season]], switching teams to [[BN Racing]].<ref name=":1">{{Cite web |last=Escotto |first=Ricardo |date=March 2, 2024 |title=Next week we race 💙💙💙 @gpstpete |url=https://www.instagram.com/p/C4BP6JnrSEy/ |access-date=March 2, 2024 |website=Instagram}}</ref> He would have a more successful season, finishing on the podium twice at [[Road America]] and [[Exhibition Place|Toronto]] respectively. Escotto finished ninth in the championship.

=== Indy NXT ===

==== 2024 ====
On May 31, 2024, it was announced that Escotto would make his debut in the [[2024 Indy NXT|Indy NXT]] series for [[Juncos Hollinger Racing]], where he would drive in five races for the team.<ref name=":2">{{Cite web |title=Juncos Hollinger Adds Escotto to Lineup for Five Races |url=https://www.indycar.com/News/2024/05/05-31-JHR-Escotto |access-date=2024-05-31 |website=IndyCar.com}}</ref> He would have his highest result of thirteenth at [[Laguna Seca]] and [[Milwaukee Mile|Milwaukee]].

==== 2025 ====
For 2025, Escotto moved up to the series full-time for the 2025 season. He signed with [[Andretti Global|Andretti]] [[Cape Motorsports|Cape Indy NXT]], becoming an [[Andretti Global]] affiliated driver and with the aim of driving for the team for the 2026 season.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |last=gabbyhamill |date=2024-10-24 |title=Ricardo Escotto to Join Andretti Cape INDY NXT in 2025 |url=https://andrettiglobal.com/news/2024/10/ricardo-escotto-to-join-andretti-cape-indy-nxt-in-2025/ |access-date=2025-05-04 |website=Andretti Global |language=en-US}}</ref> However, on July 25, Andretti Cape announced that it had parted ways with Escotto ahead of the Laguna Seca double-header.<ref>{{Cite web |date=July 25, 2025 |title=Ricardo Escotto, Andretti Cape INDY NXT Part Ways Ahead of Laguna Seca |url=https://www.indynxt.com/news/2025/07/07-25-ricardo-escotto |access-date=July 26, 2025 |website=[[Indy NXT]] |language=en}}</ref>

==== 2026 ====
On January 14, 2026, it was announced that Escotto will return to Indy NXT in [[2026 Indy NXT|2026]] with [[Juncos Hollinger Racing]].<ref>{{Cite web|last=Barnes|first=Joey|url=https://www.motorsport.com/indylights/news/escotto-confirmed-by-juncos-hollinger-racing-for-2026-indy-nxt-campaign/10789790/|date=14 January 2026|title=Escotto confirmed by Juncos Hollinger Racing for 2026 Indy NXT campaign|access-date=14 January 2026|website=[[Motorsport.com]]}}</ref>

=== FIA Formula 3 ===
In June 2026, Escotto made a cameo appearance in [[2026 FIA Formula 3 Championship|FIA Formula 3]] with [[AIX Racing]] at the [[2026 Barcelona Formula 3 round|Barcelona round]], as a stand-in replacement for an injured [[Brad Benavides]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=AIX Racing confirms Ricardo Escotto as replacement for Brad Benavides in Barcelona|date=9 June 2026|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/7KvKTc7PLzHZx4Nto5ba27/aix-racing-confirms-ricardo-escotto-as-replacement-for-brad-benavides-in-barcelona|access-date=10 June 2026|website=FIA_Formula 3® - The Official F3® Website|language=en}}</ref> He reprised his role as replacement driver for the [[2026 Silverstone Formula 3 round|Silverstone]] and [[2026 Spa-Francorchamps Formula 3 round|Spa-Francorchamps rounds]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Ricardo Escotto returns with AIX Racing for Silverstone|date=29 June 2026|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/5SJSxZBDURn8pXByaDa6Z2/ricardo-escotto-returns-with-aix-racing-for-silverstone|access-date=30 June 2026|website=FIA_Formula 3® - The Official F3® Website|language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|title=AIX Racing confirms Ricardo Escotto for Spa-Francorchamps Round|date=10 July 2026|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/en/latest/article/aix-racing-confirms-ricardo-escotto-for-spa-francorchamps-round.2nG3icUmPQZRkQkxPm7f3U|access-date=25 July 2026|website=FIA_Formula 3® - The Official F3® Website|language=en}}</ref> He was replaced by [[Salim Hanna]] for the [[2026 Budapest Formula 3 round|Hungary round]] but was announced to return to the team for the final two rounds of the season in [[2026 Monza Formula 3 round|Monza]] and [[2026 Madrid Formula 3 round|Madrid]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=AIX Racing confirms Ricardo Escotto for final races of 2026|date=22 August 2026|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/en/latest/article/aix-racing-confirms-ricardo-escotto-for-final-races-of-2026.2dL1ua6KvJeVcbMGH0xfkv|access-date=24 August 2026|website=FIA_Formula 3® - The Official F3® Website|language=en}}</ref> However, he was unable to partake in the latter round after being involved in a road traffic accident.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Escotto and Xie withdrawn from Madrid round|date=11 September 2026|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/en/latest/article/escotto-and-xie-withdrawn-from-madrid-round.4LN74LoKWJ6BHYxB0jmuVy|access-date=11 September 2026|website=FIA_Formula 3® - The Official F3® Website|language=en}}</ref>
