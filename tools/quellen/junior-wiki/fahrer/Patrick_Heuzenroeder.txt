'''Patrick Heuzenroeder''' (born 17 November 2005) is an Australian racing driver<ref name=":3">{{Cite web |title=Driver: Patrick Heuzenroeder {{!}} Driver Database |url=https://www.driverdb.com/drivers/patrick-heuzenroeder |access-date=2024-07-10 |website=www.driverdb.com}}</ref> who competes in [[Eurocup-3]] for [[Campos Racing]].

Heuzenroeder is the [[2025 GB3 Championship|2025 GB3]] vice-champion for Xcel Motorsport.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Heuzenroeder locks in GB3 drive for 2024 |url=https://motorsport.org.au/media/news/detail/2024/01/23/heuzenroeder-locks-in-gb3-drive-for-2024 |access-date=2024-07-10 |website=Motorsport Australia |date=23 January 2024 |language=en}}</ref>

== Career ==

=== Karting (2018–2022) ===
Heuzenroeder first expressed an interest in motorsport when he visited Sydney's Eastern Creek go-kart circuit at the age of 13. He would then kart for the next three years, including an appearance in the Australian Kart Championship in 2022.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |title=Pat Heuzenroeder {{!}} Heuzenroeder Motorsport |url=https://patheuzenroeder.com/ |access-date=2024-07-10 |website=patheuzenroeder.com}}</ref><ref name=":1">{{Cite web |title=Championships {{!}} Driver Database |url=https://www.driverdb.com/championships/australian-kart-championship-tag-125/2022 |access-date=2024-07-10 |website=www.driverdb.com}}</ref>

=== Formula Ford (2022) ===
Heuzenroeder competed in seven races of the 2022 Formula Ford Australian Championship as he moved up to car racing. He also competed in the New South Wales championship, where he finished third after being in contention for the championship but missing the last round.<ref name=":2">{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2022-11-07 |title=FFord racer Patrick Heuzenroeder graduates to British F4 with Argenti |url=https://formulascout.com/fford-racer-patrick-heuzenroeder-graduates-to-british-f4-with-argenti/100796 |access-date=2024-07-10 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> After some impressive performances in Formula Ford, Heuzenroeder joined the Ferrari Driver Academy Asia/Pacific trials, where he narrowly missed out on a place in the final.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Championships {{!}} Driver Database |url=https://www.driverdb.com/championships/formula-ford-australia/2022 |access-date=2024-07-10 |website=www.driverdb.com}}</ref><ref name=":0" />

=== Formula 4 (2023) ===
In late 2022, Heuzenroeder moved to the UK to compete in the [[2023 F4 British Championship]] with Phinsys by Argenti.<ref name=":2" /> He contested one round of the [[2023 Formula Winter Series|Formula Winter Series]] with the team prior to the full season in [[F4 British Championship|British F4]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2023-02-04 |title=Spain's new Formula Winter Series attracts its first drivers |url=https://formulascout.com/spains-new-formula-winter-series-attracts-its-first-drivers/103405 |access-date=2024-07-10 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Heuzenroeder made his debut in the championship at [[Donington Park]], scoring points in race three.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Waring |first=Bethonie |date=2023-04-23 |title=Noah Lisle takes dominant first British F4 win at Donington Park |url=https://formulascout.com/noah-lisle-takes-dominant-first-british-f4-win-at-donington-park/106112 |access-date=2024-07-10 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> He secured his only podium at the reverse-grid race at [[Snetterton Circuit|Snetterton]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Waring |first=Bethonie |date=2023-05-21 |title=Virtuosi's Aqil Alibhai wins reversed-grid British F4 race at Snetterton |url=https://formulascout.com/virtuosis-aqil-alibhai-wins-reversed-grid-british-f4-race-at-snetterton/107146 |access-date=2024-07-10 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Heuzenroeder ended the season in 19th in the standings, with 37 points to his name.<ref>{{Cite web |date=10 July 2024 |title=BF4 Points 2023 |url=https://www.tsl-timing.com/file/?f=TOCA/2023/232403ptsbf4.pdf}}</ref>

=== Formula Regional (2024–) ===
==== 2024 ====
[[File:GB3 2024 Mogyoród Nr. 10 Heuzenroeder.jpg|thumb|left|Heuzenroeder driving at the [[Hungaroring]] in 2024]]
In 2024, it was announced that Heuzenroeder would join the grid for the [[2024 GB3 Championship]], joining reigning champions [[JHR Developments]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2024-01-23 |title=Patrick Heuzenroeder joins JHR Developments in GB3 |url=https://formulascout.com/patrick-heuzenroeder-joins-jhr-developments-in-gb3/115159 |access-date=2024-07-10 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> He managed to finish 12th in the drivers' standings, with a best result of third at the reverse grid race in round 5 at [[Circuit Zandvoort]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Standings |url=https://www.gb-3.net/standings |access-date=2024-07-10 |website=www.gb-3.net}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2024-07-14 |title=Nikita Johnson wins GB3's reversed-grid race at Zandvoort |url=https://formulascout.com/nikita-johnson-wins-gb3s-reversed-grid-race-at-zandvoort/121467 |access-date=2024-12-19 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

==== 2025 ====
During pre-season, Heuzenroeder competed in the [[2025 Formula Regional Oceania Championship|Formula Regional Oceania Championship]] with mtec Motorsport.<ref>{{Cite web|last=Allen|first=Peter|date=7 December 2024|title=First drivers for Formula Regional Oceania 2025 revealed|url=https://formulascout.com/first-drivers-for-formula-regional-oceania-2025-revealed/126296|access-date=17 January 2026|website=Formula Scout|language=en-GB}}</ref>

Heuzenroeder remained in [[2025 GB3 Championship|GB3]] for 2025, switching to Xcel Motorsport.<ref>{{Cite web|last=Wood|first=Ida|date=3 February 2025|title=Patrick Heuzenroeder moves to Xcel Motorsport in GB3|url=https://formulascout.com/patrick-heuzenroeder-moves-to-xcel-motorsport-in-gb3/128050|access-date=17 January 2026|website=Formula Scout|language=en-GB}}</ref>

==== 2026 ====
At the start of the year, Heuzenroeder competed in the [[2026 Eurocup-3 season#Winter series|Eurocup-3 winter series]] with [[Campos Racing]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Eurocup-3 Spanish Winter Championship: 2026 season guide|date=18 February 2026|url=https://feederseries.net/2026/02/18/eurocup-3-spanish-winter-championship-2026-season-guide/|access-date=20 February 2026|website=Feeder Series|language=en}}</ref>

For his main campaign, Heuzenroeder moved to [[2026 Eurocup-3 season|Eurocup-3]] with Campos.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Patrick Heuzenroeder signs with GriffinCore Campos Racing to compete in Eurocup-3|date=15 January 2026|url=https://www.camposracing.com/en/noticias/eurocup3/patrick-heuzenroeder-signs-with-griffincore-campos-racing-to-compete-in-eurocup-3|access-date=17 January 2026|website=[[Campos Racing]]|language=en}}</ref>

=== FIA Formula 3 (2026) ===
At the start of 2026, Heuzenroeder deputised for an injured [[Ernesto Rivera (racing driver)|Ernesto Rivera]] at [[Campos Racing]] during [[Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya|Barcelona]] pre-season testing.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Rivera out of 2026 F3 pre-season tests in Barcelona; Heuzenroeder steps in with Campos Racing|date=9 February 2026|url=https://www.pitdebrief.com/post/rivera-out-of-2026-f3-pre-season-tests-in-barcelona-heuzenroeder-steps-in-with-campos/|access-date=9 February 2026|website=pitdebrief.com|language=en}}</ref> He would continue to replace Rivera for the season-opening [[2026 Melbourne Formula 3 round|Australia round]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Patrick Heuzenroeder to deputise for Ernesto Rivera in Melbourne|date=28 February 2026|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/sJxwfW1FCsdaakMYKyGwk/patrick-heuzenroeder-to-deputise-for-ernesto-rivera-in-melbourne|access-date=1 March 2026|website=FIA_Formula 3® - The Official F3® Website|language=en}}</ref>

== Personal life ==
Heuzenroeder is mentored by [[List of 24 Hours of Le Mans winners|Le Mans]] winner and former [[Formula One]] driver [[David Brabham]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://feederseries.net/2024/04/23/behind-patrick-heuzenroeders-progression-to-gb3-with-aussie-legend-david-brabham-alongside/|title=Behind Patrick Heuzenroeder's progression to GB3 with Aussie legend David Brabham alongside|website=Feeder Series|last=Brabner|first=George|date=23 April 2024|access-date=3 February 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://autoaction.com.au/2024/09/19/how-brabham-is-helping-heuzenroader|title=How Brabham is helping Heuzenroader|last=Miles|first=Thomas|website=[[Auto Action]]|date=19 September 2024|access-date=3 February 2025}}</ref>
