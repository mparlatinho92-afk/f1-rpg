'''Francesco Simonazzi''' (born 8 March 2004) is an Italian racing driver who competes in the [[2025 European Le Mans Series]] for [[Duqueine Team]]. He previously competed in the [[2023 Euroformula Open Championship|Euroformula Open Championship]] with BVM Racing from [[2021 Euroformula Open Championship|2021]] to [[2024 Euroformula Open Championship|2024]], finishing runner-up in the latter season.

== Junior racing career ==

=== Lower formulae ===
==== 2019 ====
In 2019, Simonazzi made his car racing debut, competing in the [[2019 Italian F4 Championship|Italian F4 Championship]] with [[Cram Motorsport]].<ref>{{cite web |last=Caruccio |first=Antonio |date=22 March 2019 |title=Simonazzi chiude il poker Cram (Simonazzi closes Cram poker) |url=https://www.italiaracing.net/Simonazzi-chiude-il-poker-Cram--/237174/79 |access-date=25 March 2019 |work=ITALIARACING.net}}</ref> He experienced a challenging rookie year, scoring points on just one occasion, with an eighth place coming at [[Imola Circuit|Imola]]. Simonazzi missed the final two rounds of the season, which meant that he ended up 22nd in the standings.

==== 2020 ====
Simonazzi returned to [[2020 Italian F4 Championship|Italian F4]] for 2020, contesting the first round for DRZ Benelli.<ref>{{cite web |date=28 November 2019 |title=Accordo 2020 tra DRZ Benelli e Simonazzi (2020 agreement between DRZ Benelli and Simonazzi) |url=https://www.italiaracing.net/Accordo-2020-tra--DRZ-Benelli-e-Simonazzi/240015/79 |access-date=19 December 2019 |work=Italiaracing.net}}</ref> Having scored two points in a pair of appearances for [[Jenzer Motorsport]],<ref name="Imola">{{cite web |title=Imola Entry List |url=http://www.acisport.it/public/2020/pdf/F4/2020_3615Imola//Elenco_iscritti_imola_30082020_entry_list_if4c.pdf |access-date=29 August 2020 |work=ACI Sport}}</ref> which also included a one-off start in [[2020 F4 Spanish Championship|Spanish F4]],<ref name="Jenzer joins grid">{{cite tweet|number=1293613072234446849|user=F4Spain|title=NOTICIA: Jenzer Motorsport estará en el Circuito Paul Ricard para participar en la 2ª cita del campeonato de F4 Spanish Championship|date=12 August 2020}}</ref> where he took the first podium of his career, Simonazzi drove for BVM Racing in the remaining half of the Italian F4 campaign. More points finishes followed, as the Italian finished 18th in the championship with a best result of sixth at the season finale at [[Vallelunga Circuit|Vallelunga]].

==== 2021 ====
[[File:F4 Italy 2021 Spielberg Nr. 84 Simonazzi.jpg|thumb|left|Simonazzi racing att the [[Red Bull Ring]] during the [[2021 Italian F4 Championship]]]]
The following year, Simonazzi once again raced in the [[2021 Italian F4 Championship|Italian F4 Championship]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Elliot |date=2021-01-03 |title=R-ace GP signs Australian karter Marcos Flack for Italian F4 |url=https://formulascout.com/r-ace-gp-signs-australian-karter-marcos-flack-for-italian-f4/73587 |access-date=2021-01-03 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> He remained with BVM and scored 28 points, having competed in merely four of the seven rounds, which left him 14th at season's end.

Simonazzi also made an appearance in the F2000 Italian Formula Trophy during 2021, scoring his first two victories in car racing.{{citation needed|date=December 2025}}

=== Euroformula Open ===
==== 2021 ====
In 2021, Simonazzi spearheaded BVM's return to the [[2021 Euroformula Open Championship|Euroformula Open Championship]], racing in the final two rounds at [[Monza Circuit|Monza]] and [[Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya|Barcelona]].<ref name="bvm">{{cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=17 September 2021 |title=BVM Racing returns to Euroformula after four-year break |url=https://formulascout.com/bvm-racing-returns-to-euroformula-after-four-year-break/83866 |accessdate=17 September 2021 |website=Formula Scout}}</ref> With the former event yielding a triple of points finishes, Simonazzi was classified 18th in the standings.

==== 2022 ====
[[File:Euroformula Open 2022 Spielberg Nr. 84 (2).jpg|thumb|Simonazzi racing in the [[2022 Euroformula Open Championship|2022 Euroformula Open]] at the [[Red Bull Ring]].]]
Simonazzi returned to the series for the [[2022 Euroformula Open Championship|2022 season]], being the sole driver for BVM Racing once more.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Mishra |first=Oorjit |date=2022-04-27 |title=Your season guide to the 2022 Euroformula Open Championship |url=https://feederseries.net/2022/04/27/your-season-guide-to-the-2022-euroformula-open-championship/ |access-date=2022-12-21 |website=F1 Feeder Series |language=en}}</ref> Despite low grid numbers, he struggled in the opening rounds, having to wait until the middle of the season for his first podium, which he scored at the [[Hungaroring]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Mansell puts on defensive masterclass in Race 3 to beat an impressive Simonazzi |url=http://www.euroformulaopen.net/en/news/4709/mansell-puts-on-defensive-masterclass-in-race-3-to-beat-an-impressive-simonazzi |access-date=2022-12-21 |website=www.euroformulaopen.net |language=en}}</ref> The biggest success of his season would follow just two rounds later, as, after finishing second in Race 2, Simonazzi scored his first win in the category at the [[Red Bull Ring]], inheriting victory after original race winner [[Vladislav Lomko]] was awarded a five-second penalty for colliding with [[Frederick Lubin]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Lomko (CryptoTower) makes it 3 in action-packed Race 2 at Red Bull Ring |url=http://www.euroformulaopen.net/en/news/4751/lomko-cryptotower-makes-it-3-in-action-packed-race-2-at-red-bull-ring |access-date=2022-12-21 |website=www.euroformulaopen.net |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Simonazzi (BVM) inherits well-deserved first win in Race 3 |url=http://www.euroformulaopen.net/en/news/4754/simonazzi-bvm-inherits-well-deserved-first-win-in-race-3 |access-date=2022-12-21 |website=www.euroformulaopen.net |language=en}}</ref> A less successful weekend in [[Monza Circuit|Monza]] followed, which included Simonazzi causing a collision with [[Christian Mansell]], which sent the latters car flipping upside down,<ref>{{Cite web |title=Mason takes Race 2 at Monza, Mansell unscathed after flip-over |url=http://www.euroformulaopen.net/en/news/4775/mason-takes-race-2-at-monza-mansell-unscathed-after-flip-over |access-date=2022-12-21 |website=www.euroformulaopen.net |language=en}}</ref> before Simonazzi capped off his season by taking second place in Race 1 at [[Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya|Barcelona]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Goethe (Motopark) crowned 2022 EFO Champion with dominant win in Race 1 |url=http://www.euroformulaopen.net/en/news/4790/goethe-motopark-crowned-2022-efo-champion-with-dominant-win-in-race-1 |access-date=2022-12-21 |website=www.euroformulaopen.net |language=en}}</ref> This result meant that Simonazzi finished sixth in the standings, being the second-lowest full-time competitor in the standings, only ahead of [[Alex García (racing driver, born 2003)|Alex García]].

==== 2023 ====
Simonazzi returned to the [[2023 Euroformula Open Championship|Euroformula Open]] in 2023, remaining with BVM Racing once more.<ref>{{Cite web|last=Wood|first=Ida|date=7 February 2023|title=Simonazzi confirmed as BVM Racing's first Euroformula driver for 2023|url=https://formulascout.com/simonazzi-confirmed-as-bvm-racings-first-euroformula-driver-for-2023/103485|access-date=8 February 2023|website=Formula Scout|language=en-GB}}</ref>

==== 2024 ====
[[File:Euroformula Open 2024 Spielberg Nr. 84 Simonazzi (2).jpg|thumb|left|Simonazzi driving at the [[Red Bull Ring]] during the [[2024 Euroformula Open Championship|2024 Euroformula Open]]]]
Simonazzi was retained by BVM Racing for a third season of [[2024 Euroformula Open Championship|Euroformula Open]] in 2024.<ref>{{Cite web|last=Wood|first=Ida|date=23 April 2024|title=Bergmeister, Fluxa and Simonazzi confirmed on 2024 Euroformula grid|url=https://formulascout.com/bergmeister-fluxa-and-simonazzi-confirmed-on-2024-euroformula-grid/118045|access-date=26 April 2024|website=Formula Scout|language=en-GB}}</ref>

==== 2025 ====
Simonazzi opted not to continue in [[2025 Euroformula Open Championship|Euroformula Open]] in 2025, but remained in a developmental role for BVM Racing.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Why the star of EFO's 'David and Goliath' battle is stepping back for 2025|date=23 April 2025|url=https://feederseries.net/2025/04/23/why-the-star-of-efos-david-and-goliath-battle-is-stepping-back-for-2025/|access-date=23 April 2025|website=Feeder Series|language=en}}</ref>

=== FIA Formula 3 Championship ===
Simonazzi was announced to race in the [[2023 FIA Formula 3 Championship]] with [[Rodin Carlin]] for the final two rounds, replacing [[Max Esterson]] who returned to the [[2023 GB3 Championship|GB3 Championship]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Rodin Carlin confirms Francesco Simonazzi for remainder of the 2023 F3 season|date=26 July 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/26F4pPGevxch2GXp75NS3u/rodin-carlin-confirms-francesco-simonazzi-for-remainder-of-the-2023-f3|access-date=27 July 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3® - The Official F3® Website|language=en}}</ref>

== Endurance racing career ==
=== 2025: ELMS debut ===
Simonazzi switched to the [[2025 European Le Mans Series|European Le Mans Series]] for 2025, joining French outfit [[Duqueine Team]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Record 44 Car Grid for 2025 European Le Mans Series Revealed|date=3 December 2024|url=https://www.dailysportscar.com/2024/12/03/record-44-car-grid-for-2025-european-le-mans-series-revealed.html|access-date=20 February 2025|website=Daily Sportscar|language=en}}</ref>
