'''Joshua Mason Akinwunmi''' (born 19 March 2002) is a [[British people|British]] racing driver who currently races in the [[2026 McLaren Trophy Europe]]. He is a race winner in [[Formula Regional Oceania Championship|Formula Regional Oceania]], as well as both the [[Euroformula Open Championship|Euroformula Open]] and [[BRDC British Formula 3 Championship|BRDC British F3]] Championships. He also completed a partial season of [[FIA Formula 2 Championship|FIA Formula 2]] with [[PHM Racing]] in 2023, and a partial season of [[Indy NXT]] in 2024.

== Career ==

=== Karting ===
Mason began competing in karts at the relatively late age of fourteen, taking part in club racing before moving into car racing.<ref>{{Cite web |title=ABOUT JOSH |url=https://www.joshmasonracing.com/about-josh |access-date=2022-10-20 |website=Josh Mason Racing |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Josh Mason: This year's going to be better |url=http://www.euroformulaopen.net/en/news/4589/josh-mason-this-year-s-going-to-be-better |access-date=2022-10-20 |website=www.euroformulaopen.net |language=en}}</ref>

=== BRDC British F3 ===
==== 2018 ====
In 2018, Mason graduated to car racing, driving for Lanan Racing in the [[2018 BRDC British Formula 3 Championship|BRDC British Formula 3 Championship]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=2018-10-14 |title=GB3 Driver - Joshua Mason #21 |url=https://www.gb-3.net/drivers/2018-drivers/joshua-mason/ |access-date=2021-09-28 |website=GB3 Championship |language=en |archive-date=21 October 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211021031443/https://www.gb-3.net/drivers/2018-drivers/joshua-mason/ |url-status=dead }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Racecar |title=Lanan Racing confirms Joshua Mason as first signing for 2018 |url=https://www.racecar.com/news/87442/motorsport/lanan-racing-confirms-joshua-mason-as-first-signing-for-2018-brdc-f3/ |access-date=2022-10-20 |website=www.racecar.com |language=en}}</ref> Having made his debut at [[Rockingham Motor Speedway|Rockingham]], the Brit would return to the series for the second half of the campaign, managing to win the reverse-grid race in the final round at [[Silverstone Circuit|Silverstone]] after it had been abandoned due to heavy rain following two laps behind the Safety car.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Chokhani |first=Darshan |date=2018-10-14 |title=British F3: Mason wins Race 2 as rain curtails Silverstone weekend |url=https://formularapida.net/british-f3-mason-wins-race-2-as-rain-curtails-silverstone-weekend/ |access-date=2022-10-20 |website=FormulaRapida.net |language=en-US}}</ref>

==== 2019 ====
Mason stayed at Lanan Racing for the [[2019 BRDC British Formula 3 Championship|2019 season]], competing as the team's sole full-time entrant.<ref>{{cite web |date=7 December 2018 |title=Josh Mason confirms BRDC British F3 return with Lanan Racing |url=http://www.britishf3.com/british-f3-news/josh-mason-confirms-brdc-british-f3-return-with-lanan-racing/ |accessdate=7 December 2018 |archive-date=9 December 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20181209124304/http://www.britishf3.com/british-f3-news/josh-mason-confirms-brdc-british-f3-return-with-lanan-racing/ |url-status=dead }}</ref> Despite this, Mason would manage to score three podiums in reverse-grid races, including a victory at [[Donington Park]], where he overtook poleman [[Pavan Ravishankar]] at the Safety car restart.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Hine |first=Nathan |date=2019-06-24 |title=Mason monsters to race two victory at Donington Park as Hoggard and Simmons crash |url=https://www.thecheckeredflag.co.uk/2019/06/mason-monsters-to-race-two-victory-at-donington-park-as-hoggard-and-simmons-crash/ |access-date=2022-10-20 |website=The Checkered Flag |language=en-GB}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Mega Mason takes F3 race two victory at Donington Park |url=https://news.msv.com/17301.aspx |access-date=2022-10-20 |website=news.msv.com |archive-date=20 October 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20221020083607/https://news.msv.com/17301.aspx |url-status=dead }}</ref> Mason ended up twelfth in the standings in a season that, given his lack of overall experience, he described as "having gone well".<ref>{{Cite web |title=2019 REVIEW |url=https://www.joshmasonracing.com/2019-review |access-date=2022-10-20 |website=Josh Mason Racing |language=en}}</ref>

==== 2020 ====
Once again, Mason remained with the same team in [[2020 BRDC British Formula 3 Championship|2020]], this time racing alongside Piers Prior and [[Bart Horsten]]. Having been unable to prepare adequately for the season due to living with a high-risk individual at the height of the [[COVID-19 pandemic]],<ref>{{Cite web |title=2020 REVIEW |url=https://www.joshmasonracing.com/general-8 |access-date=2022-10-20 |website=Josh Mason Racing |language=en}}</ref> Mason only scored a sole podium at [[Donington Park|Donington]] and finished 16th in the championship, behind both of his teammates.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Lanan Racing {{!}} News |url=http://www.lananracing.com/news/2020_BritishF3_Donington_Race2Sept.html |access-date=2022-10-20 |website=www.lananracing.com |archive-date=4 December 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20221204095051/http://lananracing.com/news/2020_BritishF3_Donington_Race2Sept.html |url-status=dead }}</ref>

=== Euroformula Open ===
==== 2021 ====
[[File:Euroformula Open 2021 Spielberg Nr. 21 Mason.jpg|thumb|Mason racing in the [[2021 Euroformula Open Championship|2021 Euroformula Open]] at the [[Red Bull Ring]].]]
For 2021, Mason moved to the [[2021 Euroformula Open Championship|Euroformula Open Championship]], driving for [[Double R Racing]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Britain's Josh Mason joins Double R Racing line-up in Euroformula |url=http://www.euroformulaopen.net/en/news/4253/britains-josh-mason-joins-double-r-racing-line-up-in-euroformula |access-date=2022-10-20 |website=www.euroformulaopen.net |language=en}}</ref> His best result came at a wet [[Hungaroring]], finishing fourth, as he ended up ninth in the standings, significantly ahead of rookie teammate [[Zdeněk Chovanec]].

the season fifth in the championship standings, narrowly missing out on fourth to [[Frederick Lubin]].

==== 2023 ====
Mason returned to the [[Euroformula Open Championship|Euroformula Open]] in [[2023 Euroformula Open Championship|2023]] with [[Motopark Academy|CryptoTower Racing]] for a cameo appearance at [[Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps]].<ref>{{Cite web|last=Wood|first=Ida|date=23 May 2023|title=Mason returns to Euroformula as FR2.0 champion Xie debuts at Spa|url=https://formulascout.com/mason-returns-to-euroformula-as-fr2-0-champion-xie-debuts-at-spa/107192|access-date=28 July 2023|website=Formula Scout|language=en-GB}}</ref> He returned at the [[Hungaroring]], winning the last race.<ref>{{Cite web|last=Wood|first=Ida|date=13 June 2023|title=Berta and Mason to race for CryptoTower in Euroformula at Hungaroring|url=https://formulascout.com/berta-and-mason-to-race-for-cryptotower-in-euroformula-at-hungaroring/108037|access-date=28 July 2023|website=Formula Scout|language=en-GB}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|last=Wood|first=Ida|date=18 June 2023|title=Mason survives last-lap impact with Noda for fourth Euroformula win|url=https://formulascout.com/mason-survives-last-lap-impact-with-noda-for-fourth-euroformula-win/108233|access-date=28 July 2023|website=Formula Scout|language=en-GB}}</ref>

=== Formula Regional ===
At the start of 2023, Mason joined the [[2023 Formula Regional Oceania Championship]], driving for Kiwi Motorsport.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Mason signs up with Kiwi Motorsport for 2023 New Zealand campaign |url=https://www.toyota.co.nz/toyota-racing/castrol-toyota-fr-oceania/news/2022/december/mason-signs-up-with-kiwi-motorsport-for-2023-castrol-trs-campaign/ |access-date=2025-03-09 |website=Toyota NZ |language=en-nz}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|last=Wood|first=Ida|date=29 December 2022|title=FFord graduate Sceats and Euroformula's Mason join FRegional Oceania|url=https://formulascout.com/fford-graduate-seats-and-euroformulas-mason-join-fregional-oceania/102363|access-date=29 December 2022|website=Formula Scout|language=en-GB}}</ref> He achieved one win in [[Manfeild: Circuit Chris Amon|Manfeild]], which saw him end eighth in the standings.<ref>{{Cite web|last=Wood|first=Ida|date=29 January 2023|title=Mason and Penrose win following Manfeild drama in FRegional Oceania|url=https://formulascout.com/mason-and-penrose-win-following-manfeild-drama-in-fregional-oceania/103214|access-date=28 July 2023|website=Formula Scout|language=en-GB}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|title=Castrol Toyota Formula Regional Oceania Championship 2023|url=https://www.driverdb.com/championships/toyota-racing-series-new-zealand/2023|access-date=28 July 2023|website=DriverDB|language=en}}</ref>

=== FIA Formula 2 Championship ===
Mason was confirmed by [[PHM Racing]] by [[Charouz Racing System|Charouz]] to compete in the [[2023 Formula 2 Championship]] at [[2023 Spa-Francorchamps Formula 2 round|Spa-Francorchamps]], replacing [[Brad Benavides]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=PHM Racing by Charouz confirms Joshua Mason for Spa-Francorchamps|date=27 July 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula2.com/Latest/4t4MGJzJhbjYa96u3DlLyE/phm-racing-by-charouz-confirms-joshua-mason-for-spa-francorchamps|access-date=28 July 2023|website=FIA_F2® - The Official F2® Website|language=en}}</ref> He was retained for the [[2023 Zandvoort Formula 2 round|Zandvoort round]].<ref>{{Cite web|last=Wood|first=Ida|date=24 August 2023|title=Josh Mason gets another F2 outing with PHM by Charouz at Zandvoort|url=https://formulascout.com/josh-mason-gets-another-f2-outing-with-phm-by-charouz-at-zandvoort/110490|access-date=25 August 2023|website=Formula Scout|language=en-GB}}</ref> After demonstrating decent pace throughout the first two rounds, Mason was confirmed to remain with the team for the final two rounds.<ref>{{Cite web|last=Wood|first=Ida|date=28 August 2023|title=Josh Mason to keep PHM Racing by Charouz seat for rest of F2 season|url=https://formulascout.com/josh-mason-to-keep-phm-racing-by-charouz-seat-for-rest-of-f2-season/110632|access-date=29 August 2023|website=Formula Scout|language=en-GB}}</ref> He secured a best finish of twelfth in the [[2023 Monza Formula 2 round|Monza feature race]] and placed 23rd in the overall standings.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Formula 2 2023 Driver Standings|url=https://www.fiaformula2.com/Standings/Driver?seasonId=180|access-date=28 February 2024|website=FIA_F2® - The Official F2® Website|language=en}}</ref> He was set to remain with team for the [[2024 Formula 2 Championship|2024]], but plans fell through due to funding issues.<ref>{{Cite web|last=Wood|first=Ida|date=27 February 2024|title=Josh Mason finds seat in Indy Nxt after F2 plan falls through|url=https://formulascout.com/josh-mason-finds-seat-in-indy-nxt-after-f2-plan-falls-through/116117|access-date=28 February 2024|website=Formula Scout|language=en-GB}}</ref>

=== Indy NXT ===
In 2024, Mason secured a deal to race in the [[2024 Indy NXT|Indy NXT]] with [[Abel Motorsports]].<ref>{{Cite web|last=Barnes|first=Josh|url=https://www.motorsport.com/indylights/news/josh-mason-joins-abel-motorsports-for-2024-indy-nxt-campaign/10580474/|date=28 February 2024|title=Josh Mason joins Abel Motorsports for 2024 Indy NXT campaign|access-date=28 February 2024|website=[[Motorsport.com]]}}</ref> However, he was released after two races.

==Personal life==
Mason is of [[Nigerian]] descent through his father, and both [[Grenadian people|Grenadian]] and [[Trinidadians and Tobagonians|Trinidadian/Tobagonian]] descent through his mother. Mason grew up between [[Sussex]] and the [[Cayman Islands]], and played both [[Association football|football]] and [[rugby union]] in his youth before chest surgery prevented him from furthering either career.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://blog.remax.ky/re-max-cayman-islands-partners-with-race-car-driver-josh-mason-in-euroformula-open-championship/|title=RE/MAX Cayman Islands Partners with Race Car Driver Josh Mason in Euroformula Open Championship|website=[[RE/MAX|remax.ky]]|date=9 June 2021|access-date=30 September 2021}}</ref>
