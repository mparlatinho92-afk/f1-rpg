'''Francisco Javier Sagrera Pont''' (born 12 January 2004) is a Spanish [[racing driver]] who last competed in the [[Formula Regional European Championship]] for Akcel GP.

Sagrera was the vice-champion of the [[2024 Eurocup-3 season|2024 Eurocup-3 series]], with [[MP Motorsport]]. Previously, he raced in the [[F4 Spanish Championship]] and the [[GB3 Championship]]. He previously competed in the [[2025 FIA Formula 3 Championship]] for [[AIX Racing]].

== Career ==

=== Karting ===
In 2017, Sagrera took part in his first national and continental karting competitions, with second place in the junior category of the [[Spanish Karting Championship|Spanish Championship]] being his most notable achievement that year. He would progress to the senior class the following year, finishing third in the same series, only two points behind the champion. Sagrera finished his karting career at the end of 2019.

=== Lower formulae ===
[[File:Eurocup-3 2023 Spa Nr. 3 (1).jpg|thumb|left|Sagrera during the Eurocup-3 race in Spa 2023]]
Sagrera made his single-seater debut with MOL Racing in the final round of the [[2019 F4 Spanish Championship|F4 Spanish Championship]] at the [[Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya]].<ref name="Barcelona">{{cite web|date=5 November 2019|title=Drivers record registration for Barcelona!|url=http://f4spain.es/en/2019/11/05/drivers-record-registration-barcelona-f4-spain/|accessdate=6 November 2019|archive-date=26 March 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220326082539/http://f4spain.es/en/2019/11/05/drivers-record-registration-barcelona-f4-spain/|url-status=dead}}</ref> Despite being a rookie, he managed to finish the third race in the top ten, although he did not receive points as he was classed as a guest driver.<ref>{{Cite web|last=Wood|first=Ida|date=2019-11-10|title=Franco Colapinto crowned Spanish F4 champion at Barcelona|url=https://formulascout.com/franco-colapinto-spanish-f4-champion/56464|access-date=2021-09-15|website=Formula Scout|language=en-US}}</ref>

In 2020, Sagrera returned to the [[2020 F4 Spanish Championship|Spanish Championship]], this time racing the first four rounds of the season for MOL.<ref name="Navarra">{{cite tweet|user=@F1FeederSeries1|number=1280910549237317633|title=The provisional entry list for the 2020 Spanish F4 season is out! it kicks off at Navarra on July 18. At least fifteen drivers will take part, three drivers still have to confirm they can make it|date=8 July 2020}}</ref> However, Sagrera was only able to score eight points over the course of the twelve races, and ended up 21st in the standings.

=== GB3 Championship ===
Sagrera stepped up to the [[2021 GB3 Championship|GB3 Championship]] in 2021, partnering [[2020 Ginetta Junior Championship|Ginetta Junior Champion]] [[Tom Lebbon]] and Mexican [[José Garfias]] at [[Elite Motorsport]].<ref>{{Cite web|last=Allen|first=Peter|date=2020-11-09|title=Elite Motorsport to replace Lanan Racing on BRDC British F3 grid|url=https://formulascout.com/elite-motorsport-to-replace-lanan-racing-on-brdc-british-f3-grid/70783|access-date=2020-11-09|website=Formula Scout|language=en-US}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|last=Wood|first=Ida|date=2021-02-01|title=Elite Motorsport announces Javier Sagrera as first British F3 signing|url=https://formulascout.com/elite-motorsport-announces-javier-sagrera-as-first-british-f3-signing/74750|access-date=2021-09-15|website=Formula Scout|language=en-US}}</ref> He started his season strongly, scoring a podium in just his third race in the series.<ref>{{Cite web|last=Whitfield|first=Steve|date=2021-05-23|title=Mansell ends British F3 opener with reversed grid Brands Hatch win|url=https://formulascout.com/mansell-ends-british-f3-opener-with-reversed-grid-brands-hatch-win/79193|access-date=2021-09-15|website=Formula Scout|language=en-US}}</ref> Sagrera wouldn't manage to hit those same heights again that season, although he only missed out on a top ten finish in five of the following sixteen races he finished.

=== Eurocup-3 ===

==== 2023 ====
Sagrera moved over to the [[Eurocup-3]] series in [[2023 Eurocup-3 season|2023]], signing with [[Álex Palou|Palou Motorsport]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2023-04-26 |title=Palou Motorsport announces Pingasov and Sagrera for Eurocup-3 |url=https://formulascout.com/palou-motorsport-announces-pingasov-and-sagrera-for-eurocup-3/106190 |access-date=2025-02-25 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> He would get three podiums throughout the season, however, he did not contest the last two rounds of the season. Sagrera finished eighth in the standings.

==== 2024 ====
[[File:Eurocup-3 2024 Spielberg Nr. 3 Sagrera.jpg|thumb|right|Sagrera racing at the [[Red Bull Ring]] during the [[2024 Eurocup-3 season]].]]

In 2024, Sagrera signed with [[MP Motorsport]] to compete full-time in the [[2024 Eurocup-3 season|2024 Eurocup-3]] season. He would get his maiden win in the first race of the season at [[Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps]]. Throughout the first half of the season, Sagrera would get consistent results with occasional podium finishes which allowed him to challenge championship leader [[Christian Ho]]. In the fifth round of the season at [[Circuit Zandvoort|Zandvoort]], Sagrera qualified on pole and won both races.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Series |first=Feeder |date=2024-07-18 |title=Eurocup-3 Zandvoort: Sagrera delivers at MP’s home, Michael Shin’s accident, and more |url=https://feederseries.net/2024/07/18/eurocup-3-zandvoort-sagrera-delivers-at-mps-home-michael-shins-accident-and-more/ |access-date=2025-02-25 |website=Feeder Series |language=en}}</ref> The next race at [[MotorLand Aragón|Aragón]] saw him retire after lap one following a collision with teammate [[Owen Tangavelou]]. However, Sagrera's fortunes would turn in race two as he would win with a comfortable margin to teammate [[Bruno del Pino]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Series |first=Feeder |date=2024-08-03 |title=Eurocup-3 Aragón: Sagrera maintains healthy championship lead despite teammate clash |url=https://feederseries.net/2024/08/03/eurocup-3-aragon-sagrera-maintains-healthy-championship-lead-despite-teammate-clash/ |access-date=2025-02-25 |website=Feeder Series |language=en}}</ref> Title rival Christian Ho finished on the podium in both races, however, Ho later received a ten-second time penalty in race two after the race stewards determined that Ho had overtaken Tangavelou outside of the track on lap 1. This dropped Ho to ninth in race two, and resulted in Sagrera becoming the new championship leader following his three wins in the previous four races. While Sagrera would finish in second in both races at [[Circuito de Jerez|Jerez]], Ho had a slim chance at the championship following a win in race one and fifth in race two.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Campos Racing |url=https://www.camposracing.com/en/noticias/eurocup3/christian-ho-scores-his-fourth-win-in-jerez-shin-present-on-the-podium-twice |access-date=2025-02-25 |website=www.camposracing.com}}</ref> Going into the final round of the season at [[Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya|Barcelona]], Sagrera had a 26 point lead to Ho in the championship.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Series |first=Feeder |date=2024-11-08 |title=Eurocup-3 season finale: The drivers in contention for the title |url=https://feederseries.net/2024/11/08/eurocup-3-season-finale-the-drivers-in-contention-for-the-title/ |access-date=2025-02-25 |website=Feeder Series |language=en}}</ref> Despite Ho's slim chances in the title fight, he would capitalize by finishing in second in race one and winning race two. Sagrera would only manage to finish sixth in both races, however, this would be enough to crown Sagrera as champion, winning it by just two points.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2024-11-10 |title=Sagrera provisionally crowned Eurocup-3 champion as Ho wins finale |url=https://formulascout.com/sagrera-provisionally-crowned-eurocup-3-champion-as-ho-wins-finale/125601 |access-date=2025-02-25 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Although, Ho's team [[Campos Racing]] had lodged a protest for the race one results, claiming that race one winner [[Emerson Fittipaldi Jr.]] had illegally overtaken Ho outside of the track which gave Fittipaldi an unfair advantage. This put the true winner of the championship in doubt for months since the [[Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile|FIA]] had still not reached a conclusion.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Scout |first=Formula |date=2024-11-11 |title=The off-track drama that is keeping the Eurocup-3 crown in limbo |url=https://formulascout.com/the-off-track-drama-that-is-keeping-the-eurocup-3-crown-in-limbo/125651 |access-date=2025-02-25 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>  Three months later, following a review of results, Ho was awarded the win for the first Barcelona race after the FIA determined that Fittipaldi did indeed overtake off-track.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-02-24 |title=FIA confirms Christian Ho as 2024 Eurocup-3 champion after appeal |url=https://formulascout.com/fia-confirms-christian-ho-as-2024-eurocup-3-champion-after-appeal/128557 |access-date=2025-02-25 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> This meant Ho gained seven points and was crowned champion, while Sagrera would ultimately finish second in the championship, five points behind Ho.

=== FIA Formula 3 ===
Sagrera signed with [[AIX Racing]] to compete in the [[2025 FIA Formula 3 Championship]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Scout |first=Formula |date=2024-11-30 |title=Sagrera and Nael secure steps up to F3 for 2025 |url=https://formulascout.com/sagrera-and-nael-secure-steps-up-to-f3-for-2025/126106 |access-date=2024-11-30 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Following 2 races, Sagrera was replaced by British Driver [[James Hedley]] for round three from [[2025 Imola Formula 3 round|Imola]] and onwards.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Scout |first=Formula |date=10 May 2025 |title=James Hedley replaces Javier Sagrera in AIX F3 drive |url=https://formulascout.com/james-hedley-replaces-javier-sagrera-in-aix-f3-drive/130736 |access-date=10 May 2025 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

=== Formula Regional European Championship ===
[[File:FRECA 2025 Mogyoród Nr. 44 Sagrera.jpg|thumb|left|Sagrera driving at the [[Hungaroring]] during the [[2025 Formula Regional European Championship]]]]
==== 2023 ====
Sagrera joined [[MP Motorsport]] for the final two rounds of the [[2023 Formula Regional European Championship]], scoring a best finish of 15th.<ref>{{Cite web|last=Wood|first=Ida|date=12 October 2023|title=Tramnitz tops FREC test at Zandvoort and announces Red Bull backing|url=https://formulascout.com/tramnitz-tops-frec-test-at-zandvoort-and-announces-red-bull-backing/112015|access-date=4 July 2025|website=Formula Scout|language=en-GB}}</ref>

==== 2025 ====
After losing his [[2025 FIA Formula 3 Championship|Formula 3]] drive, Sagrera signed with AKCEL GP to compete in the [[2025 Formula Regional European Championship]].<ref>{{Cite web|last=Wood|first=Ida|date=3 July 2023|title=Returning faces among late additions to FREC’s Hungaroring grid|url=https://formulascout.com/returning-faces-among-late-additions-to-frecs-hungaroring-grid/132651|access-date=4 July 2025|website=Formula Scout|language=en-GB}}</ref>
