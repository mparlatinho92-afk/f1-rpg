The '''2026 GB3 Championship''' is a [[Motorsport#Motor racing|motor racing]] championship for [[Open-wheel car|open wheel]], [[Formula racing|formula racing cars]] held across Europe. The 2026 season is the eleventh organised by the [[British Racing Drivers' Club]] in the United Kingdom, and the sixth season under the GB3 moniker after rebranding from the BRDC British Formula 3 Championship in mid-2021. The championship features a mix of professional motor racing teams and privately funded drivers. The season is scheduled to be run over eight triple-header rounds, starting in April and ending in November.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |date=25 September 2025 |title=GB3 Championship reveals provisional 2026 calendar with Red Bull Ring and Barcelona additions |url=https://www.gb-3.net/news/2025/sept/25/gb3-championship-reveals-provisional-2026-calendar-with-red-bull-ring-and-barcelona-additions- |access-date=26 September 2025 |website=[[GB3 Championship]]}}</ref>

=== Team changes ===
[[Arden International|Arden Motorsport]] returned to GB3 after a one-year absence, while Nitrous Competitions ADM Racing, a cooperation between [[British Superbike Championship]] team Nitrous Competitions and [[GB4 Championship|GB4]] team ADM Racing, entered GB3, initially running a single car.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=27 August 2025 |title=ADM joins forces with Nitrous Competitions for 2026 GB3 entry |url=https://formulascout.com/adm-joins-forces-with-nitrous-competitions-for-2026-gb3-entry/134098 |access-date=26 September 2025 |website=Formula Scout |language=en}}</ref><ref name=":1">{{Cite web |date=2025-11-10 |title=Leon Wilson to spearhead Arden Motorsport’s GB3 return in 2026 |url=https://www.gb-3.net/news/2025/nov/10/leon-wilson-to-spearhead-arden-motorsports-gb3-return-in-2026 |access-date=2025-11-10 |website=[[GB3 Championship]]}}</ref>

[[Chris Dittmann Racing]], who had entered every season since 2013, and Argenti with [[Prema Racing|Prema]], who had joined the series in 2025, both left the championship before the beginning of the season. [[JHR Developments]], despite being allocated car numbers, did not enter any rounds.

=== Mid-season ===
[[Hitech Grand Prix|Hitech]] announced ahead of round three that [[2026 F4 Spanish Championship|Spanish F4]] driver [[Jacob Micallef]] would make his [[GB3 Championship]] debut by joining the team on selected rounds, driving the No. 4 car in alternation with [[Jin Nakamura]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=2026-06-24|title=Jacob Micallef joins Hitech for selected GB3 Championship rounds|url=https://www.hitechgp.co.uk/jacob-micallef-joins-hitech-for-selected-gb3-championship-rounds/|access-date=2026-06-24|website=[[Hitech Grand Prix]]}}</ref>

The [[Fortec Motorsport|Fortec Motorsports]] and Nitrous Competitions ADM Racing teams, and their drivers of [[Alex Kattoulas|Alexandros Kattoulas]], Jack Taylor and Jason Pribyl respectively, did not enter the rounds at the [[Hungaroring]] nor the [[Red Bull Ring]].<ref name=":2">{{Cite web |date=2026-07-02 |title=Feeder Series on X: 'NEWS {{!}} 🇭🇺 Here is the entry list for this weekend's GB3 round at Budapest!' |url=https://x.com/feeder_series/status/2072775439416705199 |access-date=2026-07-03 |website=X}}</ref>

Before the [[Silverstone Circuit|Silverstone]] 2nd round, [[Lewis Gilbert (racing driver)|Lewis Gilbert]] left the series to join [[West Surrey Racing|WSR]] in the [[British Touring Car Championship]] which left his seat at Arden vacant.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2026-07-13 |title=BTCC newcomer Lewis Gilbert to complete 2026 season with WSR {{!}} WSR - West Surrey Racing |url=https://www.wsr-racing.com/btcc-newcomer-lewis-gilbert-to-complete-2026-season-with-wsr/ |access-date=2026-07-13 |language=en-GB}}</ref> [[Elite Motorsport]] meanwhile fielded [[Edu Robinson]] on his GB3 debut in the car previously driven by [[Kirill Kutskov]].<ref>{{Cite Instagram |postid=DbasT4xDTek |user=elitemotorsport_ |title=@edu.robinson joins us in the @gb3championship this weekend. |author=[[Elite Motorsport]] |date=2026-07-30}}</ref> [[Fortec Motorsport]] and Nitrous Competitions ADM Racing rejoined the grid, albeit with Kattoulas still absent and with Nitrous Competitions ADM Racing and Jason Pribyl only staying on for one round.

[[Kanato Le]] replaced [[Jacob Micallef]] in the No. 4 Hitech at [[Brands Hatch]], returning to GB3 after driving for Hillspeed in three rounds in 2025.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2026-09-23 |title=GB3 race-winner Kanato Le set for series return with Hitech at Brands Hatch |url=https://www.gb-3.net/news/2026/sept/23/gb3-racewinner-kanato-le-set-for-series-return-with-hitech-this-weekend |access-date=2026-09-23 |website=[[GB3 Championship]]}}</ref> Jack Taylor and [[Fortec Motorsport]] missed the round, while [[Edu Robinson]] returned for another outing at [[Elite Motorsport]]. Brands Hatch also saw the debut of former FREC outfit AKCEL GP, who fielded a single car for series returnee Aditya Kulkarni.<ref name=":3">{{Cite web |url=https://www.gb-3.net/news/2026/sept/24/akcelgp-to-make-gb3-championship-debut-at-brands-hatch-with-aditya-kulkarni |access-date=2026-09-24 |website=[[GB3 Championship]]|title=AKCEL GP to make GB3 Championship debut at Brands Hatch with Aditya Kulkarni|date=2026-09-24}}</ref>

== Season report ==

=== First half ===
[[Silverstone Circuit|Silverstone]] hosted the opening round of the 2026 GB3 Championship, which began with a double pole position for [[Velocity Racing Development|VRD Racing]]'s [[Nikita Bedrin]] in qualifying.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2026-04-25 |title=Bedrin takes double-pole on debut at GB3’s Silverstone season-opener |url=https://formulascout.com/bedrin-takes-double-pole-on-debut-at-gb3s-silverstone-season-opener/139070 |access-date=2026-05-07 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> He dropped behind the [[Rodin Motorsport|Rodin]] pair of [[Martin Molnár]] and [[Maxim Rehm]] after a slow start to the opening race, but quickly regained second place, closed up to Molnár and retook the lead on lap five. He led throughout a late-race safety car to secure victory.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2026-04-25 |title=Bedrin comes from behind to win GB3 season-opener at Silverstone |url=https://formulascout.com/bedrin-comes-from-behind-to-win-gb3-season-opener-at-silverstone/139089 |access-date=2026-05-07 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Bedrin's start to the second race was suboptimal again as he dropped behind [[Hitech GP|Hitech]]'s [[Deagen Fairclough]], but he moved back in front a few corners later. Fairclough kept close to Bedrin before retiring with a car issue. That left Bedrin to fend off Rehm before winning again, with Xcel's [[Lucas Fluxá]] taking third.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2026-04-26 |title=Bedrin wins again as his GB3 rivals hit trouble at Silverstone |url=https://formulascout.com/bedrin-wins-again-as-his-gb3-rivals-hit-trouble-at-silverstone/139123 |access-date=2026-05-07 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> [[Elite Motorsport|Elite]]'s [[Flynn Jackes]] had reversed-grid pole position and led throughout the race, before a last-lap battle saw Hitech's [[Jin Nakamura]] snatch victory at the final corner. Elite's [[Kyuho Lee]] took second in the same battle, before a penalty dropped him off the podium. Fluxá inherited third as Bedrin assumed the top of the points standings.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2026-04-26 |title=Nakamura snatches last-gasp win in GB3’s reversed-grid race at Silverstone |url=https://formulascout.com/nakamura-snatches-last-gasp-win-in-gb3s-reversed-grid-race-at-silverstone/139142 |access-date=2026-05-07 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

In qualifying for round two at [[Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps|Spa]], [[Rodin Motorsport|Rodin]]'s [[Abbi Pulling]] became the first woman to take a pole position in GB3, sharing the honors with Bedrin.<ref>{{Cite web |last= |first= |date=2026-05-29|url=https://www.gb-3.net/news/2026/may/29/abbi-poleing-pulling-makes-history-as-first-female-to-take-gb3-pole |access-date=2026-05-30 |website=[[GB3 Championship]]  |language=en-US|title=Abbi POLEing! Pulling makes history as first woman to take GB3 pole}}</ref> The first race of the weekend was postponed by multiple hours due to rain, which saw the reversed-grid race get cancelled. When the race got underway, Pulling held the lead ahead of Bedrin and Rehm before a mid-race safety car brought them back onto her tail. On the restart, Rehm attacked and passed Bedrin for second, allowing Pulling to escape up front. Bedrin reclaimed second one lap later as Rehm dropped down the order and Molnár claimed third. Bedrin set off after Pulling, but she held on, taking the first win for a female driver in GB3 history.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Mee|first=Lydia |date=2026-05-30|url=https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/2024-f1-academy-champion-abbi-162346165.html |access-date=2026-05-30 |website=Yahoo  |language=en-US|title=2024 F1 Academy champion Abbi Pulling makes history with first GB3 Championship race win}}</ref> The second race saw a clean start for Bedrin up front, who gapped the field as Pulling attacked Rehm for second. The latter fended her off, which saw Pulling drop behind Fairclough and Molnár on the Kemmel straight. Molnár then took third and the positions remained static afterwards, allowing Bedrin to win and extend his lead to 27 points over Rehm.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2026-05-31 |title=Bedrin dominates GB3 race two at Spa |url=https://formulascout.com/gb3/139456 |access-date=2026-06-22 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

Home hero Molnár and Bedrin shared pole positions in qualifying at the [[Hungaroring]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Molnar joins Pole Position Cup battle as Bedrin smashes lap record in Hungary |url=https://www.gb-3.net/news/2026/jul/7/molnar-joins-pole-position-cup-battle-as-bedrin-smashes-lap-record-in-hungary |access-date=2026-07-10 |website=[[GB3 Championship]]}}</ref> A better start from the front row for Fairclough saw him take the lead of the first race, with Molnár dropping to second ahead of Bedrin. He held the points leader off multiple times, but lost out to both Bedrin and Rehm after a safety car restart as Fairclough was free to gap the field and take victory.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2026-07-04 |title=Fairclough flies to second GB3 win at Hungaroring opener |url=https://formulascout.com/fairclough-flies-to-second-gb3-win-at-hungaroring-opener/139818 |access-date=2026-07-10 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Fairclough was once again the fastest starter in race two as he took the lead again, but Bedrin reclaimed the position directly after. Fairclough's car then stopped and he retired, handing a bigger lead to Bedrin as Molnár and Rehm disputed second place. Rehm then also retired with an issue, handing P3 to Fluxá.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2026-07-05 |title=Bedrin wins GB3 race two at Hungaroring as rivals hit trouble |url=https://formulascout.com/bedrin-wins-gb3-race-two-at-hungaroring-as-rivals-hit-trouble/139861 |access-date=2026-07-10 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Jackes started the reversed-grid race from pole position and led from lights to flag to secure his maiden GB3 win. [[Hillspeed]]'s front row starter Dante Vinci initially held second, before Fluxá and Xcel's [[Rowan Campbell-Pilling]] passed him and he dropped down. Bedrin came fifth, with his championship lead now at 68 points following Rehm's race 2 non-score.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2026-07-05 |title=Jackes takes maiden GB3 win in Hungaroring reversed-grid race |url=https://formulascout.com/jackes-takes-maiden-gb3-win-in-hungaroring-reversed-grid-race/139882 |access-date=2026-07-10 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

The first half of the season concluded with GB3's debut at the [[Red Bull Ring]], where pole positions were once again shared between Molnár and Bedrin. The Hungarian lost the lead of the first race to Rehm into turn one, before Bedrin also moved past him. A safety car then followed, and Bedrin attacked Rehm for the lead. The pair battled for the rest of the race, before Bedrin took the lead with two laps to go. Rehm finished second, before penalties promoted Fluxá to second and Molnár to third.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2026-07-11 |title=Bedrin battles to GB3 victory in Red Bull Ring race one thriller |url=https://www.msv.com/news/2026/jul/11/race-one-report |access-date=2026-07-29 |website=www.msv.com}}</ref> Race two was trouble-free for Bedrin as he was able to convert his pole position into victory. Fluxá and Campbell-Pilling passed Molnár on lap one and spent the race fighting over second, with Fluxá coming out ahead and Rehm inheriting third after penalties for Campbell-Pilling. Race three brought pole position for VRD's Rodrigo González, who lost the lead to his brother Patricio before running off track and dropping to 13th. Patricio González led Bedrin and [[Arden International|Arden]]'s Leon Wilson to victory as Bedrin grew his championship lead to 106 points over Fluxá.<ref>{{Cite web |last= |first= |date=2026-07-13 |title=Feeder Series weekend review, results and standings: 09–12 July 2026 |url=https://feederseries.net/2026/07/13/feeder-series-weekend-review-results-and-standings-09-12-july-2026/ |access-date=2026-07-29 |website=Feeder Series |language=en}}</ref>

=== Second half ===
GB3 returned to Silverstone for round five of the season, and both pole positions went to Fairclough.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2026-08-01 |title=Fairclough flies to double GB3 pole at Silverstone |url=https://formulascout.com/fairclough-flies-to-double-gb3-pole-at-silverstone/139965 |access-date=2026-09-01 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> The first race saw him hold Molnár behind before he lost his lead after a restart on lap four. However, Fairclough remained close and was able to retake the lead one lap later. Campbell-Piling then moved into second, before Molnár got back past him, set after Fairclough but was unable to make another attack for the lead.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2026-08-01 |title=Fairclough prevails in Molnar dice to win GB3 opener at Silverstone |url=https://formulascout.com/fairclough-prevails-in-molnar-dice-to-win-gb3-opener-at-silverstone/139970 |access-date=2026-09-01 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> The contenders for the win in race two were the same, with Fairclough leading Campbell-Piling ahead of Molnár this time. An unsuccessful challenge for second saw Molnár then drop behind Fluxá before the gaps at the front stabilized and Fairclough took another win.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2026-08-02 |title=Fairclough wins again in GB3 race two at Silverstone |url=https://formulascout.com/fairclough-wins-again-in-gb3-race-two-at-silverstone/139979 |access-date=2026-09-01 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Reverse grid pole position went to [[Elite Motorsport|Elite]]'s debutant [[Edu Robinson]], who spent the race fighting with Rodrigo González and Hitech's [[Jacob Micallef]], with the latter taking his maiden victory after overtaking González on the final lap. Championship leader Bedrin did not feature on the podium all weekend, allowing Molnár to slightly reduce his lead to 103 points.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2026-08-02 |title=Micallef prevails in last-lap battle to grab maiden GB3 win at Silverstone |url=https://formulascout.com/micallef-prevails-in-last-lap-battle-to-grab-maiden-gb3-win-at-silverstone/139982 |access-date=2026-09-01 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

Bedrin returned to the front in qualifying one at [[Donington Park]] as Fairclough took another pole position in qualifying two. The latter started the first race in second and moved past polesitter Bedrin on the first lap as Pulling took third off Molnár. Bedrin kept close to the leader and launched multiple attacks in the latter half of the race, but was unable to deny Fairclough the win. The second race saw Bedrin hit the wall on the opening lap after pressure from Wilson and Molnár. Fairclough pulled away from the pair after the ensuing safety car, managing one more interruption on his way to another victory. Reversed-grid pole position went to Lee, but Campbell-Pilling took the lead after starting third. Both cars ran off track when Lee tried retaking the lead, allowing Pulling into the lead. Campbell-Pilling reclaimed first place one lap later, before Hillspeed's Peter Bouzinelos also moved past Pulling. Campbell-Pilling fended off multiple attacks from the Australian to win. Bedrin had another points-free race, his lead over Molnár further reduced to 81 points.<ref>{{Cite web |last= |first= |date=2026-09-07 |title=Feeder Series weekend review, results and standings: 3–6 September 2026 (Europe) |url=https://feederseries.net/2026/09/07/feeder-series-weekend-review-results-and-standings-3-6-september-2026-europe/ |access-date=2026-09-23 |website=Feeder Series |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Whitfield |first=Steve |date=2026-09-08 |title=Fairclough flies to more GB3 wins as Bedrin has tough weekend at Donington Park |url=https://www.autosport.com/national/news/fairclough-flies-to-more-gb3-wins-as-bedrin-has-tough-weekend-at-donington-park/10853691/ |access-date=2026-09-23 |website=Autosport}}</ref>

== Championship standings ==
; Scoring system

Points are awarded to the top 20 classified finishers in races one and two, with the third race awarding points to only the top 15. Race three, which has its grid formed by reversing the top twelve from the qualifying order, awards extra points, up until a maximum of twelve, for positions gained from the drivers' respective starting positions.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2024-03-25 |title=GB3 announces reversed-grid change and superlicence points for 2024 |url=https://formulascout.com/gb3-announces-reversed-grid-change-and-superlicence-points-for-2024/117240 |access-date=2024-03-25 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>
