The '''2019 BRDC British Formula 3 Championship''' was a [[Motorsport#Motor racing|motor racing]] championship for [[Open-wheel car|open wheel]], [[Formula racing|formula racing cars]] held across [[England]], with one round in [[Belgium]]. The 2019 season was the fourth organised by the [[British Racing Drivers' Club]] in the United Kingdom. The championship featured a mix of professional motor racing teams and privately funded drivers, and also featured the 2-litre 230-bhp Tatuus-Cosworth single seat race car in the main series. The season commenced at [[Oulton Park]] on 20 April and ended on 15 September at [[Donington Park]], after eight triple header events for a total of twenty-four races.<ref name="Calendar">{{cite news|title=2019 BRDC British F3 Championship calendar revealed|url=http://www.britishf3.com/british-f3-news/2019-brdc-british-f3-championship-calendar-revealed//|date=8 November 2018|publisher=BRDC British F3 Championship|accessdate=8 November 2018|archive-date=9 November 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20181109111907/http://www.britishf3.com/british-f3-news/2019-brdc-british-f3-championship-calendar-revealed//|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==Championship standings==
;Scoring system

Points were awarded to the top 20 classified finishers in races one and three, with the second race awarding points to only the top 15. Race two, which reversed the order of the race one finishers, providing they set a lap time within 103% of the fastest driver, will be awarded extra points for positions gained from drivers' respective starting positions.
