The '''2023 FIA Formula 3 Championship''' was a [[motor racing]] championship for [[Formula Three|Formula 3]] cars sanctioned by the [[Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile]] (FIA). The championship was the fourteenth season of Formula 3 racing and the fifth season run under the [[FIA Formula 3 Championship]] moniker. It was an [[open-wheel racing]] category serving as the third tier of [[formula racing]] in the [[FIA Global Pathway]]. The category was run in support of selected rounds of the [[2023 Formula One World Championship]]. As the championship was a [[spec series]], all teams and drivers competing in the championship ran the same car, the [[Dallara F3 2019]].<ref name="the car"/>

After all 18 races were completed in this season (the round in [[Emilia Romagna Grand Prix|Imola]] being cancelled due to the [[2023 Emilia-Romagna floods]]). Ten different drivers won races: [[Zak O'Sullivan]] took four victories, while [[Pepe Martí]] won three races this season. [[Franco Colapinto]], [[Gabriele Minì]] and eventual Drivers' Champion [[Gabriel Bortoleto]] each won two races. [[Paul Aron]] had one victory to his name in the season, as did [[Oliver Goethe]] and [[Caio Collet]]. [[Taylor Barnard]] took his first Formula 3 win in [[2023 Spa-Francorchamps Formula 3 round|Belgium]], with [[Jonny Edgar]] following suit in the last race of the year at [[2023 Monza Formula 3 round|Monza]].

[[Prema Racing]] entered the championship as the reigning Teams' Champions, having secured their title at the final race of the 2022 season in Monza. They also secured the Teams' Championship title in 2023.

Gabriel Bortoleto, driving for [[Trident Motorsport|Trident]], claimed the Drivers' Championship after qualifying at the final round at Monza.

=== Team changes ===
German [[Formula 4]] team [[PHM Racing]] took over the entry and assets of [[Charouz Racing System]] at the end of the 2022 season, and ran in cooperation with the Czech squad under the ''PHM Racing by Charouz'' moniker.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://formulascout.com/phm-racing-to-enter-f2-and-f3-in-2023-with-charouz-deal/101336|title=PHM Racing to enter F2 and F3 in 2023 with Charouz deal|website=Formula Scout|last=Wood|first=Ida|date=28 November 2022|access-date=28 November 2022}}</ref>

[[Carlin Motorsport|Carlin]] competed under new ownership in 2023. The New Zealand-based car manufacturer Rodin Cars became majority shareholder of the team. With that, the team's name changed to Rodin Carlin.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Leading race team Carlin to become Rodin Carlin |url=https://www.carlin.co.uk/news/leading-race-team-carlin-to-become-rodin-carlin |access-date=2023-01-25 |website=www.carlin.co.uk |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230207103110/https://www.carlin.co.uk/news/leading-race-team-carlin-to-become-rodin-carlin |archive-date=7 February 2023 |url-status=dead}}</ref>

[[Hitech Grand Prix|Hitech]] partnered with hardware company [[Pulse-Eight]] during the off-season, changing the official name of the team to Hitech Pulse-Eight.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Hitech Pulse-Eight |url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Teams/244/Hitech-Pulse-Eight |access-date=2023-02-06 |website=FIAFormula3 - The Official F3 Website |language=en}}</ref>

[[Alex García (racing driver, born 2003)|Alex García]] made his Formula 3 debut after a seventh place in the [[2022 Euroformula Open Championship]] with [[Motopark Academy|Motopark]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Jenzer Motorsport signs Alejandro Garcia for 2023 campaign |url=https://www.camposracing.com/es/noticias/fia-f2/campos-racing-completa-con-kush-maini-su-dupla-de-pilotos-de-fia-f2-en-2023 |access-date=27 January 2023 |website=FIAFormula3 - The Official F3 Website |language=en}}</ref> Alongside him was PHM-backed [[Nikita Bedrin]], who stepped up to the category after two years in Formula 4, with a high point of fourth place in the 2022 [[2022 ADAC Formula 4 Championship|ADAC]] and [[2022 Formula 4 UAE Championship|UAE F4]] Championships.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Jenzer Motorsport add rookie Nikita Bedrin to 2023 line-up |url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/4kenI97pVkkfGUSSQ3QV4V/jenzer-motorsport-add-rookie-nikita-bedrin-to-2023-line-up |access-date=30 January 2023 |website=FIAFormula3 - The Official F3 Website |language=en}}</ref> The last seat was filled by another PHM-supported F4 graduate in [[Taylor Barnard]], last years' ADAC F4 vice-champion.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Barnard completes Jenzer Motorsport's 2023 roster |url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/2qzhyQC1Y9fGtG3ejKAFtL/barnard-completes-jenzer-motorsports-2023-roster |access-date=31 January 2023 |website=FIAFormula3 - The Official F3 Website |language=en}}</ref>

New entrant [[PHM Racing]] by [[Charouz Racing System|Charouz]] hired [[Sophia Flörsch]] for their first season in the championship, a move partially funded by FIA Formula 3 promoter Formula Motorsport.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gascoigne |first=Roger |date=9 February 2023 |title=Sophia Floersch returns to single-seaters with PHM in FIA F3 |url=https://formulascout.com/sophia-floersch-returns-to-single-seaters-with-phm-in-fia-f3/103525 |access-date=9 February 2023 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-GB}}</ref><ref>{{cite news|url=https://formulascout.com/formula-3-promoter-helping-to-fund-sophia-floerschs-seat/104107|title=Formula 3 promoter helping to fund Sophia Floersch's seat|publisher=Formula Scout|date=26 February 2023}}</ref> Flörsch previously drove for Campos Racing in [[2020 FIA Formula 3 Championship|2020]] and had competed in endurance and sportscar racing since then. She replaced [[Francesco Pizzi]], who left FIA F3 to join TJ Speed in [[2023 USF Pro 2000 Championship|USF Pro 2000]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=5 January 2023 |title=Francesco Pizzi leaves FIA F3 for USF Pro 2000 move with TJ Speed |url=https://formulascout.com/francesco-pizzi-leaves-fia-f3-for-usf-pro-2000-move-with-tj-speed/102581 |access-date=9 February 2023 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-GB}}</ref> Their second seat, which was shared by a handful of drivers in 2022, was filled by [[Roberto Faria]], who competed in [[GB3 Championship|GB3]] for the last three years, coming fifth in the latter two.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gascoigne |first=Roger |date=10 February 2023 |title=Roberto Faria steps up to FIA F3 with PHM Racing by Charouz |url=https://formulascout.com/roberto-faria-steps-up-to-fia-f3-with-phm-racing-by-charouz/103563 |access-date=10 February 2023 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-GB}}</ref> The final seat was taken by [[Piotr Wiśnicki]], who graduated to FIA F3 after a season in the Formula Regional European Championship, to replace [[László Tóth (racing driver)|László Tóth]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gascoigne |first=Roger |date=10 February 2023 |title=Rookie Wisnicki completes PHM Racing by Charouz's FIA F3 line-up |url=https://formulascout.com/rookie-piotr-wisnicki-completes-phm-racing-by-charouzs-fia-f3-line-up/103565 |access-date=10 February 2023 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-GB}}</ref>

=== Calendar changes ===
* The FIA Formula 3 Championship made its debut in Australia, supporting the [[Australian Grand Prix]] at the [[Albert Park Circuit]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=FIA Formula 3 to race in Melbourne from 2023 |url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/1Haf6sS0UfTNOddnqLDVxR/fia-formula-3-to-race-in-melbourne-from-2023 |access-date=13 September 2022 |website=FIAFormula3 - The Official F3 Website |language=en}}</ref>
* Formula 3 returned to the [[Circuit de Monaco]] for the first time since [[2012 GP3 Series|2012]], known at the time as the [[GP3 Series]].<ref name="Monaco">{{Cite web |title=Monaco GP organiser confirms return of F3 to support bill for 2023 |url=https://formulascout.com/monaco-gp-organiser-confirms-return-of-f3-to-support-bill-for-2023/99389 |access-date=5 October 2022 |website=Formula Scout |date=4 October 2022 |language=en}}</ref>
* The round at [[Circuit Zandvoort]], in support of the [[Dutch Grand Prix]], was removed from the calendar.<ref name=":0" />
* The championship was originally scheduled to feature ten rounds, but the round at [[Imola Circuit|Imola]], in support of the [[2023 Emilia Romagna Grand Prix|Emilia Romagna Grand Prix]], was cancelled along with the [[2023 Formula One World Championship|Formula 1]] and [[2023 Formula 2 Championship|Formula 2]] rounds as a result of [[2023 Emilia-Romagna floods|mass flooding]] which affected the region.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Update on the 2023 Emilia Romagna Grand Prix at Imola {{!}} Formula 1 |url=https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article.breaking-update-on-the-emilia-romagna-grand-prix-at-imola.14xk8GLh2Lr8wBGdJqhxnO.html |access-date=2023-05-17 |website=www.formula1.com |language=en}}</ref>
* The [[Macau Grand Prix]] returned to the [[Formula Three]] category for the first time since 2019, serving as a non-championship round of the [[FIA Formula 3 Championship]].

== Regulation changes ==

=== Technical regulations ===
Formula 3 ran with 55% sustainable fuel in 2023, supplied by [[Saudi Aramco|Aramco]], who replaced [[Elf Aquitaine]] as an official fuel partner and supplier.<ref>{{cite web |last= |first= |date=2 September 2022 |title=Formula 2 and Formula 3 partner with Aramco to pioneer low-carbon fuels from 2023 |url=https://www.aramco.com/en/news-media/news/2022/formula-2-and-formula-3-partner-with-aramco-to-pioneer-low-carbon-fuels-from-2023 |access-date=2 September 2022 |work=aramco.com |publisher=[[Aramco]]}}</ref> In a bid to decrease the championships' carbon footprint, an incremental gain in usage is planned until the 2027 season, where usage of 100% sustainable fuel is planned.<ref>{{Cite web |title=F2, F3 to run with 55% sustainable fuels from 2023 season |url=https://www.motorsport.com/fia-f2/news/f2-f3-to-run-with-55-sustainable-fuels-from-2023-season/10362301/ |access-date=9 September 2022 |website=[[Motorsport.com]] |date=2 September 2022 |language=en}}</ref>

== Season report ==
=== Round 1: Bahrain ===
{{Further|2023 Sakhir Formula 3 round}}
[[Gabriele Minì]] claimed feature race pole position with the fastest qualifying time at the [[Bahrain International Circuit]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=QUALIFYING: Minì rockets to debut pole position in Sakhir|date=3 March 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/6DWWJwO5h2fnzwbBsSOTSU/qualifying-mini-rockets-to-debut-pole-position-in-sakhir|access-date=10 September 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3 - The Official F3 Website|language=en}}</ref> [[Franco Colapinto]] qualified twelfth to start the reverse-grid sprint race from first place. Contact between [[Gabriel Bortoleto]] and [[Rafael Villagómez]] on lap two sent Villagómez into the barriers and brought out the [[safety car]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Bortoleto penalised following Sakhir Sprint Race|date=4 March 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/2hVp5KymBsRFoUMv9kfejW/bortoleto-penalised-following-sakhir-sprint-race|access-date=10 September 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3 - The Official F3 Website|language=en}}</ref> As racing resumed, [[Pepe Martí]] improved to second place and later passed race leader Colapinto to take his first victory in FIA Formula 3.<ref>{{Cite web|title=SPRINT RACE: Martí prevails over Colapinto to claim maiden F3 win|date=4 March 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/yKI4owPdocUkvHT9XVZxD/sprint-race-marti-prevails-over-colapinto-to-claim-maiden-f3-win|access-date=10 September 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3 - The Official F3 Website|language=en}}</ref>

On the opening lap of the feature race, pole-sitter Minì lost positions to Bortoleto and [[Grégoire Saucy]]. He reclaimed second place from Saucy shortly before the safety car was deployed to recover [[Tommy Smith (racing driver)|Tommy Smith]]'s car, which had stalled after contact with [[Mari Boya]]. When the race restarted, Minì was issued a five-second time penalty for his car being outside the lines at the race start.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.fia.com/sites/default/files/decision-document/2023%20Sakhir%20Event%20-%20OFFENCE%20-%20Car%2015%20-%20%20front%20tyres%20outside%20of%20the%20line%20during%20start%20procedure.pdf|title=Document 100|website=fia.com|date=5 March 2023}}</ref> He soon passed Bortoleto for the lead, and by the penultimate lap was far enough ahead of fourth-placed [[Dino Beganovic]] to stay on the podium despite his penalty, however a final-lap safety car to recover [[Alex García (racing driver, born 2003)|Alex García]]'s retired Jenzer bunched the cars together and dropped Minì to eighth at the finish line. Bortoleto was awarded the victory, followed by [[Oliver Goethe]] and Beganovic, all claiming their first podiums in the category.<ref>{{Cite web|title=2023 Round 1 Feature Race: Thoughts from the top 3|date=5 March 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/3t98xSSZHH4XOs56xXlutu/2023-round-1-feature-race-thoughts-from-the-top-3|access-date=10 September 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3 - The Official F3 Website|language=en}}</ref> At the end of Round 1, Bortoleto led the Drivers' Championship by three points over Goethe.<ref>{{Cite web|title=FEATURE RACE: Bortoleto leads Trident 1-2 after late heartbreak for Minì|date=5 March 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/11Atq9KUGkvpELWQDrz7tJ/bortoleto-leads-trident-1-2-after-late-heartbreak-for-mini|access-date=10 September 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3 - The Official F3 Website|language=en}}</ref>

=== Round 2: Australia ===
{{Further|2023 Melbourne Formula 3 round}}
Championship leader Bortoleto qualified fastest at the [[Albert Park Circuit]] and [[Sebastián Montoya]] started the sprint race on pole position.<ref>{{Cite web|title=QUALIFYING: Bortoleto on pole in Red Flag-affected session|date=31 March 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/1piS2qVzKtqMRV7xsqPlJj/qualifying-bortoleto-on-pole-in-red-flag-affected-session|access-date=10 September 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3 - The Official F3 Website|language=en}}</ref> The safety car was deployed on the opening lap when second-placed starter Goethe received a puncture and got stuck in the gravel. [[Luke Browning]] challenged Montoya for the lead at the restart but lost positions after being launched over a kerb; Montoya was then passed by Franco Colapinto. Two more safety car periods followed to recover the crashed cars of [[Ido Cohen]] and Tommy Smith in separate incidents. One more lap of racing took place in which Montoya dropped to third place behind [[Zak O'Sullivan]], however the race ended under a fourth safety car period when Alex García stopped in the gravel. Colapinto crossed the line first, however all three MP Motorsport cars were later disqualified for breaches of the technical regulations, handing O'Sullivan his first victory in FIA Formula 3.<ref>{{Cite web|title=SPRINT RACE: Colapinto takes measured win amidst the drama in Melbourne|date=1 April 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/23fuODK7d80gW4WbzPDVHN/sprint-race-colapinto-takes-measured-win-amidst-the-drama-in-melbourne|access-date=10 September 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3 - The Official F3 Website|language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|title=Colapinto loses Sprint Race win after technical infringement|date=1 April 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/4xoddh02wBmEis11XurZfp/colapinto-loses-sprint-race-win-after-technical-infringement|access-date=10 September 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3 - The Official F3 Website|language=en}}</ref>

Pole-sitter Bortoleto held the lead on the first lap of the feature race ahead of Grégoire Saucy and Gabriele Minì. Colapinto and Mari Boya were involved in separate accidents on lap two and the safety car was deployed, under which [[Kaylen Frederick]] also crashed. A second safety car period began on lap twelve to recover Ido Cohen's car after a collision with Rafael Villagómez; the top three maintained their positions through both restarts and to the end of the race, with Bortoleto taking a second consecutive feature race victory and Minì his first FIA Formula 3 podium.<ref>{{Cite web|title=FEATURE RACE: Bortoleto keeps perfect Feature Race record with Melbourne win|date=2 April 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/6h05YQelFEjSfDjgzSVlN0/feature-race-bortoleto-keeps-perfect-feature-race-record-with-melbourne-win|access-date=10 September 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3 - The Official F3 Website|language=en}}</ref> Pepe Martí, who started 30th, came through the field to finish seventh.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Martí thrilled to have pushed it to the limit with Feature Race points|date=3 April 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/29xdBc6MGIbD6a2OU7rvoo/marti-thrilled-to-have-pushed-it-to-the-limit-with-feature-race-points|access-date=10 September 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3 - The Official F3 Website|language=en}}</ref> Bortoleto's victory extended his lead in the Drivers' Championship to 20 points over Saucy.

=== Round 3: Monaco ===
{{Further|2023 Monte Carlo Formula 3 round}}
Monaco became Round 3 after [[2023 Emilia-Romagna floods|flooding in the Emilia-Romagna region]] of [[Italy]] forced the cancellation of the scheduled round at the [[Imola Circuit]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Update on Imola race weekend|date=17 May 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/6DTqY3in9te6dOLUBmOYPg/update-on-imola-race-weekend-f3|access-date=10 September 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3 - The Official F3 Website|language=en}}</ref> Gabriele Minì took his second feature race pole position in qualifying at the [[Circuit de Monaco]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=QUALIFYING: Minì takes commanding Monte Carlo pole position|date=26 May 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/4cjsGKzld4FJMvymj2w4zK/qualifying-mini-takes-commanding-monte-carlo-pole-position|access-date=10 September 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3 - The Official F3 Website|language=en}}</ref> Pepe Martí started the sprint race on reverse-grid pole. The safety car was deployed on the first lap of the sprint race when [[Jonny Edgar]] hit the wall at turn one and other cars received punctures. Martí held his lead at the restart and took his second victory of the year, followed by [[Leonardo Fornaroli]] with his first FIA Formula 3 podium. [[Taylor Barnard]] had started third but dropped to fifth by the end; Grégoire Saucy completed the podium.<ref>{{Cite web|title=SPRINT RACE: Martí drives off into the distance for Monte Carlo victory|date=27 May 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/4xANwAdwKgztkwq07sLR0S/sprint-race-marti-drives-off-into-the-distance-for-monte-carlo-victory|access-date=10 September 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3 - The Official F3 Website|language=en}}</ref>

Minì, Beganovic and [[Paul Aron]] held their top three positions through the start of the feature race and at the safety car restart after a crash by Ido Cohen. A close battle for fifth place between Sebastián Montoya and [[Caio Collet]] ended in contact and dropped both drivers to the back. Minì, Beganovic and Aron made up the podium, with Minì claiming his first win in the category, moving him to second place in the Drivers' Championship. Points leader Bortoleto was classified fifth in the feature race and now held a 17-point lead in the standings.<ref>{{Cite web|title=FEATURE RACE: Minì breezes to maiden victory in Monte Carlo masterclass|date=28 May 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/46kqYTP4YnOilcIlkvjVwD/feature-race-mini-breezes-to-maiden-victory-in-monte-carlo-masterclass|access-date=10 September 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3 - The Official F3 Website|language=en}}</ref>

=== Round 4: Spain ===
{{Further|2023 Barcelona Formula 3 round}}
Pepe Martí set the fastest time in qualifying at the [[Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya]] and Zak O'Sullivan started the sprint race from pole position.<ref>{{Cite web|title=QUALIFYING: Martí brings it home for first F3 pole|date=2 June 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/579W0ZJAPOpZKGFA6bjK58/qualifying-marti-brings-it-home-for-first-f3-pole|access-date=10 September 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3 - The Official F3 Website|language=en}}</ref> O'Sullivan led for the entire race distance despite challenges from Luke Browning and two safety car restarts, one for a collision involving Ido Cohen and Rafael Villagómez and the second after separate incidents in which [[Christian Mansell]] and Dino Beganovic were beached in the gravel.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Three drivers penalised following Barcelona Sprint Race|date=3 June 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/2AoHD2rd2RrpgEV8NlRdkj/three-drivers-penalised-following-barcelona-sprint-race|access-date=10 September 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3 - The Official F3 Website|language=en}}</ref> O'Sullivan's win was his second of the season.<ref>{{Cite web|title=SPRINT RACE: O'Sullivan holds off Browning for Barcelona victory|date=3 June 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/6mAzKOpKrzBPmWLtUdAS3h/sprint-race-osullivan-holds-off-browning-for-barcelona-victory|access-date=10 September 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3 - The Official F3 Website|language=en}}</ref>

On the opening lap of the feature race, pole-sitter Martí held off attacks from second-placed Taylor Barnard, and a collision between Browning and Leonardo Fornaroli resulted in Browning's retirement. Martí led for the rest of the race to claim his third victory of the year. His win lifted him to second place in the Drivers' Championship, 24 points adrift of championship leader Bortoleto, who finished fourth in both races.<ref>{{Cite web|title=FEATURE RACE: Martí and Campos untouchable on home soil|date=4 June 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/6EKBjtvi8u82sSddNLJPcG/feature-race-marti-and-campos-untouchable-on-home-soil|access-date=10 September 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3 - The Official F3 Website|language=en}}</ref>

=== Round 5: Austria ===
{{Further|2023 Spielberg Formula 3 round}}
Grégoire Saucy claimed feature race pole at the [[Red Bull Ring]] and Martí started first in the sprint race, which took place in wet conditions.<ref>{{Cite web|title=QUALIFYING: Saucy claims first F3 pole after Aron loses lap for track limits|date=30 June 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/3ThFFrZhpnyJQsRN7vUdF5/qualifying-saucy-claims-first-f3-pole-after-aron-loses-lap-for-track-limits|access-date=10 September 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3 - The Official F3 Website|language=en}}</ref> Martí led the majority of the sprint race whilst Paul Aron, who had started eighth, made his way up the order to second place. Racing was paused during a safety car period to recover the collided cars of Rafael Villagómez and [[Oliver Gray]], and Aron began to pressure Martí for the lead when racing resumed.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Oliver Gray and Nikita Bedrin handed post-Sprint Race penalties|date=1 July 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/1yY4969vsDP1BCrcopQXXa/oliver-gray-and-nikita-bedrin-handed-post-sprint-race-penalties|access-date=10 September 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3 - The Official F3 Website|language=en}}</ref> Aron took first place with three laps remaining to claim his first victory in the series, whilst Martí dropped to sixth place in the closing laps.<ref>{{Cite web|title=SPRINT RACE: Aron takes maiden F3 win with comeback fight|date=1 July 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/4N5AOfAp5lPOPrNV1qz9hB/sprint-race-aron-takes-maiden-f3-win-with-comeback-fight|access-date=10 September 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3 - The Official F3 Website|language=en}}</ref>

Contact between teammates Gabriele Minì and Luke Browning brought out the safety car on the first lap of the feature race. Soon after racing resumed, Dino Beganovic overtook pole-sitter Saucy for first place. Beganovic, Saucy, Aron and championship leader Bortoleto fought closely for the lead in the following laps, but contact between Saucy and Aron gave both drivers damage and dropped them down the order. Beganovic complained of tyre wear and began to fall back; the lead was taken by Bortoleto and then O'Sullivan, who had started sixth, soon after. The final podium position was contested by Franco Colapinto, Sebastián Montoya and Caio Collet in a close final-lap battle. Contact between the former two demoted Montoya out of contention and allowed Collet through to third place.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Floersch disqualified and Montoya and Yeany penalised after Spielberg Feature Race|date=2 July 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/2G7eXcVzpaZjPQMD7ll2WC/floersch-disqualified-from-spielberg-feature-race|access-date=10 September 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3 - The Official F3 Website|language=en}}</ref> O'Sullivan crossed the finish line to achieve his third win of the year, followed by Bortoleto, whose championship advantage now stood at 36 points over Martí.<ref>{{Cite web|title=FEATURE RACE: O'Sullivan triumphs in Spielberg classic|date=2 July 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/1nIkYe56dDvd8zk7XRHWl5/feature-race-osullivan-triumphs-in-spielberg-classic|access-date=10 September 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3 - The Official F3 Website|language=en}}</ref>

=== Round 6: United Kingdom ===
{{Further|2023 Silverstone Formula 3 round}}
Leonardo Fornaroli set the fastest qualifying time at [[Silverstone Circuit]] and Sebastián Montoya started the sprint race from first place.<ref>{{Cite web|title=QUALIFYING: Fornaroli takes maiden pole in Trident 1-2 at Silverstone|date=7 July 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/2sppu2VCRyrEsOmwfOOeIs/qualifying-fornaroli-takes-maiden-pole-in-trident-1-2-at-silverstone|access-date=10 September 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3 - The Official F3 Website|language=en}}</ref> Franco Colapinto, who started fourth, improved to second place on the opening lap but dropped behind Taylor Barnard shortly after. Rain began to fall on lap 8 of 18 and the safety car was deployed due to the conditions; some drivers opted to switch to wet-weather tyres but the top three stayed out on slicks. As racing resumed, Barnard made contact with Montoya, allowing Colapinto to pass both of them for the lead.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Several drivers penalised after Silverstone Sprint Race|date=8 July 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/6713PX1xvq2RA1RywrWE6O/barnard-penalised-after-silverstone-sprint-race|access-date=10 September 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3 - The Official F3 Website|language=en}}</ref> The wet tyre runners had an early advantage but dropped back in the closing laps as the track dried. Colapinto claimed his first win of the year and Christian Mansell his first FIA Formula 3 podium.<ref>{{Cite web|title=SPRINT RACE: Colapinto takes victory after Silverstone downpour|date=8 July 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/3jgR4Eg4IRfBdpWX73aZwM/sprint-race-colapinto-takes-victory-after-silverstone-downpour|access-date=10 September 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3 - The Official F3 Website|language=en}}</ref>

Fornaroli built a lead on the first lap of the feature race before racing was paused to recover the car of Luke Browning after a collision with [[Hugh Barter]]. Oliver Goethe, who started second, passed Fornaroli for the lead with seven laps remaining. Rain started to fall shortly after and a crash involving [[Roberto Faria]] and Tommy Smith prompted a second safety car. Smith was the only driver to switch to wet tyres, and rose from the back of the field to 11th place in the two remaining racing laps, but was later demoted with a time penalty. The order of the top three remained unchanged, and Goethe took his first win in the category. At the end of the round, Bortoleto's lead in the championship remained at 36 points ahead of Martí.<ref>{{Cite web|title=FEATURE RACE: Goethe heads home Trident 1-2 after late race rain|date=9 July 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/6QqiAL2WtS6cfmGPwWMCOy/feature-race-goethe-heads-home-trident-1-2-after-late-race-rain|access-date=10 September 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3 - The Official F3 Website|language=en}}</ref>

=== Round 7: Hungary ===
{{Further|2023 Budapest Formula 3 round}}
Feature race pole position at the [[Hungaroring]] was claimed by [[Zak O'Sullivan]], his first of the season.<ref>{{Cite web|title=QUALIFYING: O'Sullivan fires to pole in PREMA Racing front row lockout|date=21 July 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/49yFmDgEcKz6qzcJEZuAfU/qualifying-osullivan-fires-to-pole-in-prema-racing-front-row-lockout|access-date=10 September 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3 - The Official F3 Website|language=en}}</ref> [[Gabriele Minì]] qualified 12th to start the sprint race on pole position. Minì was overtaken by [[Nikita Bedrin]] at the first corner, but later reclaimed the lead on lap eight. The top two built a gap to Bortoleto in third, but their advantage was nullified in the closing laps when the safety car was deployed to recover Sebastián Montoya's broken-down car. Bortoleto passed Bedrin for second place at the final corner of the last lap. Minì took his second win of the season and Bedrin claimed [[Jenzer Motorsport]]'s first podium of the year.<ref>{{Cite web|title=SPRINT RACE: Minì steers clear of trouble to clinch victory in Budapest|date=22 July 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/6dI5QHEEvnMxZuLGzdsml9/sprint-race-mini-steers-clear-of-trouble-to-clinch-victory-in-budapest|access-date=10 September 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3 - The Official F3 Website|language=en}}</ref>

The feature race was shortened due to concerns over excessive tyre wear.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://formulascout.com/f3s-hungaroring-feature-race-to-be-shortened-due-to-tyre-wear-concern/109478|title=F3's Hungaroring feature race to be shortened due to tyre wear concern|website=Formula Scout|first=Ida|last=Wood|date=22 July 2023|access-date=28 July 2023}}</ref> O'Sullivan led the entire race distance and set the [[fastest lap]] on his way to his fourth victory of the season. He was followed by teammate Dino Beganovic and fellow [[Williams Driver Academy|Williams junior]] Franco Colapinto. O'Sullivan's victory promoted him to second place in the standings, one point ahead of Pepe Martí and 43 points behind championship leader Bortoleto.<ref>{{Cite web|title=FEATURE RACE: O'Sullivan flawless in Budapest to ignite title challenge|date=23 July 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/27qAIErol6ZgQlzrRUhT9O/feature-race-osullivan-flawless-in-budapest-to-ignite-title-challenge|access-date=10 September 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3 - The Official F3 Website|language=en}}</ref>

=== Round 8: Belgium ===
{{Further|2023 Spa-Francorchamps Formula 3 round}}
Martí took pole position at the [[Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps]] whilst Bortoleto qualified 15th.<ref>{{Cite web|title=QUALIFYING: Martí steals pole late on to keep title hopes intact|date=28 July 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/6hBMbNvkqFD239ftcpzjck/qualifying-marti-steals-pole-late-on-to-keep-title-hopes-intact|access-date=10 September 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3 - The Official F3 Website|language=en}}</ref> Hugh Barter started first in the sprint race, but dropped positions on the opening lap. The lead passed to Taylor Barnard who was soon overtaken by Caio Collet, who had started fourth. The 12-lap race was interrupted by three safety car periods; firstly when Rafael Villagómez collided with Sebastián Montoya, secondly when Martí hit the car of Ido Cohen whilst rejoining the track, and thirdly when Bortoleto came to a halt on the Kemmel straight. The race ended behind the safety car. Collet's victory was his first of the season.<ref>{{Cite web|title=SPRINT RACE: Collet wins as Championship contenders hit trouble|date=29 July 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/2xlahgMM7tapukRm6aPF8G/sprint-race-collet-wins-as-championship-contenders-hit-trouble|access-date=10 September 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3 - The Official F3 Website|language=en}}</ref>

The feature race began in damp conditions and the drivers were split on tyre choice, most starting on slicks and some on wets. The wet tyre runners had an immediate advantage and Paul Aron, who started 11th, was in the lead before the end of the first lap. Oliver Goethe crashed on lap 5 of 15 and brought out the safety car, prompting some of the wet tyre runners, including Aron, to switch to slick tyres. The lead passed to Barnard as racing resumed. Aron had emerged from the pits ahead of the original slick runners, but they were unable to catch up to the wet tyre runners before the end of the race. Barnard was challenged by Christian Mansell for the lead in the closing laps, but Barnard held the position to claim his first victory in the series. Bortoleto's championship lead now stood at 38 points ahead of Aron with 39 points possible in the final round.<ref>{{Cite web|title=FEATURE RACE: Barnard takes maiden F3 win with Bortoleto two points short of title|date=30 July 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/6vfDiYRNu6WYexB6XQo8uD/feature-race-barnard-takes-maiden-f3-win-with-bortoleto-two-points-short-of|access-date=10 September 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3 - The Official F3 Website|language=en}}</ref>

=== Round 9: Italy ===
{{Further|2023 Monza Formula 3 round}}
Oliver Goethe set the fastest qualifying time at [[Monza Circuit]] prior to crashing and causing a red flag, securing his pole position.<ref>{{Cite web|title=QUALIFYING: Goethe claims maiden pole as Bortoleto seals Championship title|date=1 September 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/xdZIHv7Rgup5zKNS3u2UV/qualifying-goethe-claims-maiden-pole-as-bortoleto-seals-championship-title|access-date=10 September 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3 - The Official F3 Website|language=en}}</ref> As neither of Bortoleto's remaining championship rivals, Aron and Martí, claimed the two points on offer for pole position, Bortoleto secured the title after qualifying and became Trident's first FIA Formula 3 champion.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Thoughts of a Champion - Gabriel Bortoleto|date=1 September 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/519z98Vfivkhg0OVEdM08E/thoughts-of-a-champion-gabriel-bortoleto|access-date=10 September 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3 - The Official F3 Website|language=en}}</ref> Franco Colapinto started on sprint race pole after five drivers were disqualified from the results due to [[parc fermé]] regulation breaches.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Five drivers disqualified and one penalised following Monza Qualifying|date=1 September 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/1H24McQOkWf5rDX1u5JTwv/five-drivers-disqualified-and-one-penalised-following-monza-qualifying|access-date=10 September 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3 - The Official F3 Website|language=en}}</ref> Mari Boya, who started second, passed Colapinto for the lead off the start line. Contact between Aron and Caio Collet gave Aron a puncture, which caused a first-corner collision that eliminated Aron, Martí and Jonny Edgar from the race. On lap 10, Colapinto regained the lead and third-placed Grégoire Saucy received a puncture after contact with Zak O'Sullivan, promoting Bortoleto to the podium places. The lead was traded between Colapinto and Boya over the next few laps, until Colapinto ultimately built a gap and Boya was passed by Bortoleto for second place. Colapinto's win was his second of the season and Boya claimed his first podium in the series.<ref>{{Cite web|title=SPRINT RACE: Colapinto clinches record-equalling victory in frenetic Monza showdown|date=2 September 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/5zzJ8GjFgDjyTPwJNtKPJp/sprint-race-colapinto-clinches-record-equalling-victory-in-frenetic-monza|access-date=10 September 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3 - The Official F3 Website|language=en}}</ref> Goethe, Luke Browning and Gabriele Minì finished in the points positions despite all starting outside the top 20, but fifth-placed Browning was later disqualified for a technical infringement.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Luke Browning disqualified from Monza Sprint Race, Leonardo Fornaroli and Woohyun Shin penalised|date=2 September 2023|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/3zqQ5a690Mjh417LlIrs6t/luke-browning-disqualified-from-monza-sprint-race-woohyun-shin-assigned-grid|access-date=10 September 2023|website=FIA_Formula 3 - The Official F3 Website|language=en}}</ref>

A throttle issue forced feature race pole-sitter Goethe out of the race on the formation lap, allowing Caio Collet to start from the front. The race was interrupted by four safety car periods as Grégoire Saucy, Sebastián Montoya, and championship runner-up contenders Colapinto and Martí were all forced into the gravel in separate incidents. Jonny Edgar gained second place from Aron on the opening lap and later passed Collet for the lead on lap six. The two drivers swapped the lead over the next few laps before Collet ultimately fell behind Zak O'Sullivan and Taylor Barnard to finish fourth. Edgar's race win was his first podium finish in three years of FIA Formula 3. O'Sullivan's podium lifted him to second place in the Drivers' Championship. Champion Bortoleto finished fifth and claimed the fastest lap, extending his lead to 45 points. Despite Trident driver Bortoleto's championship victory, Prema Racing clinched the Teams' Championship at the final race.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.motorsport.com/fia-f3/news/f3-monza-edgar-scores-first-win-in-finale-prema-clinches-teams-title/10515411/|title=F3 Monza: Edgar scores first win in finale, Prema clinches teams' title|website=motorsport.com|first=Megan|last=White|date=3 September 2023|access-date=10 September 2023}}</ref>

=== Scoring system ===
Points were awarded to the top ten classified finishers in both races. The pole-sitter in the feature race also received two points, and one point was given to the driver who set the fastest lap in both the feature and sprint races, provided that driver finished inside the top ten. If the driver who set the fastest lap was classified outside the top ten, the point was given to the driver who set the fastest lap of those inside the top ten. No extra points where awarded to the pole-sitter in the sprint race as the grid for it was set by reversing the top twelve qualifiers.<ref name=Regulations>{{cite web|title=2023 FIA Formula 3 Sporting Regulations|url=https://www.fia.com/sites/default/files/2023_fia_f3_sporting_regulations_v4_18-07-23.pdf|work=[[Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile]]|pages=3-4|date=18 July 2023|access-date=17 March 2025|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240407084456/https://www.fia.com/sites/default/files/2023_fia_f3_sporting_regulations_v4_18-07-23.pdf|archive-date=7 April 2024}}</ref>
