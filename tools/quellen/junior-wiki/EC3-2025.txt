The '''2025 Eurocup-3 season''' was the third season of the Eurocup-3 series. Eurocup-3 is a multi-event motor racing championship for single-seater open wheel [[formula racing]] cars held across Europe, created in 2023 as an alternative to the FIA-sanctioned [[Formula Regional European Championship]] and the [[Euroformula Open Championship|Euroformula Open championship]].<ref name="EC3Launch">{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2022-11-17 |title=New Eurocup-3 series launched to rival Euroformula and FREC |url=https://formulascout.com/new-eurocup-3-series-launched-to-rival-euroformula-and-frec/100901 |access-date=2022-11-17 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-GB}}</ref>

Following an inaugural non-championship round in March 2024, the Eurocup-3 and [[F4 Spanish Championship|Spanish F4]] organisers announced the creation of a new Spanish Winter Championship for each series, held across the [[Iberian Peninsula]] in the early months of 2025.<ref name="SWC">{{Cite web |last=Arroyo |first=Juan |last2=Muschlien |first2=Finjo |date=30 September 2024 |title=Exclusive: Two new winter single-seater series coming to Europe in 2025 |url=https://feederseries.net/2024/09/30/exclusive-two-new-winter-single-seater-series-coming-to-europe-in-2025/ |access-date=19 January 2025 |website=Feeder Series |language=en}}</ref>

[[MP Motorsport]]'s [[Mattia Colnaghi]] won the Drivers' Championship with two races to spare, and in doing so also secured the Rookies' Championship title and helped his team defend the Teams' Championship title at the same race.

== Main series ==
=== Teams and drivers ===
Teams utilized the same [[Tatuus F3 T-318]] chassis used in the Formula Regional European Championship, but fitted with a different body kit, and new intercoolers and a battery kits, as well as being 25&nbsp;kg lighter. The car used a 270&nbsp;hp [[Alfa Romeo]]-Autotecnica engine and [[Hankook]] tires.<ref name="TheCar">{{Cite web |title=The Car |url=https://eurocup3.org/about/the-car |access-date=2023-11-20 |website=eurocup3.org |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20241207112909/https://eurocup3.org/about/the-car |archive-date=7 December 2024 |url-status=dead}}</ref>

=== Race calendar ===
<div class="overflowbugx" style="overflow-x:auto;">
The calendar for the 2025 season was announced on 18 November 2024. The race tally was originally scheduled to remain the same, at 16 races across eight tracks.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Eurocup-3 announces calendar for 2025 |url=https://eurocup3.org/new/eurocup-3-announces-calendar-for-2025 |access-date=2024-11-18 |website=eurocup3.org}}</ref> With a separate winter championship also taking place in 2025, the one-off non-championship round was not repeated. [[Monza Circuit]] returned to the calendar after a one-year absence, while the series also debuted at [[TT Circuit Assen]]. [[Circuit Zandvoort]] and [[MotorLand Aragón]] left the schedule.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2024-11-19 |title=Eurocup-3 to race at Assen in 2025 |url=https://formulascout.com/eurocup-3-to-race-at-assen-in-2025/125838 |access-date=2024-11-19 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Ahead of the second round of the season, it was announced that the rounds at [[Algarve International Circuit]] and [[Circuit Paul Ricard]] would feature a sprint race, meaning they would feature the same weekend format used during the winter champioship.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-06-06 |title=Feeder Series weekend preview and schedule: 06–08 June |url=https://feederseries.net/2025/06/06/feeder-series-weekend-preview-and-schedule-06-08-june/ |access-date=2025-06-06 |website=Feeder Series |language=en}}</ref> This brought up the total amount of races to 18.

=== Season report ===

==== First half ====
Eurocup-3's main season began in May at the [[Red Bull Ring]] with [[MP Motorsport]]'s [[Mattia Colnaghi]] claiming pole position for the opening race.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gascoigne |first=Roger |date=2025-05-17 |title=MP's Mattia Colnaghi on pole for Eurocup-3's season-opening race |url=https://formulascout.com/mps-mattia-colnaghi-on-pole-for-eurocup-3s-season-opening-race/130984 |access-date=2025-12-18 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Griffin Core's [[Kacper Sztuka]] started alongside him, but found no way past the Italian despite pressuring him through the final stages of the race. GRS driver [[Yani Stevenheydens]] initially ran third, but [[Campos Racing]]'s [[Ernesto Rivera (racing driver)|Ernesto Rivera]] passed him on lap three to claim the final podium spot.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gascoigne |first=Roger |date=2025-05-17 |title=Mattia Colnaghi starts Eurocup-3 campaign with victory at Red Bull Ring |url=https://formulascout.com/mattia-colnaghi-starts-eurocup-3-campaign-with-victory-at-red-bull-ring/131020 |access-date=2025-12-18 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Race two pole position went to Campos and [[Jules Caranta]],<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gascoigne |first=Roger |date=2025-05-18 |title=Jules Caranta takes pole for Eurocup-3's second Red Bull Ring race |url=https://formulascout.com/jules-caranta-takes-pole-for-eurocup-3s-second-red-bull-ring-race/131073 |access-date=2025-12-18 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> but a slow start allowed Colnaghi to get alongside him through the opening turns, with both him and his teammate [[Maciej Gładysz]] getting past Caranta on the opening lap. The latter then quickly built a gap to take the race win by five seconds as Colnaghi had a similar gap to third-placed Caranta, enjoying a quiet race to leave Spielberg in the lead of the championship.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gascoigne |first=Roger |date=2025-05-18 |title=Maciej Gladysz dominates second Eurocup-3 race at Red Bull Ring |url=https://formulascout.com/maciej-gladysz-dominates-second-eurocup-3-race-at-red-bull-ring/131102 |access-date=2025-12-18 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

Round two at the [[Algarve International Circuit]] featured three races for the first time in the series' main season history. Sztuka was fastest in the opening qualifying session, leading a Campos top-five lockout,<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-06-07 |title=Sztuka takes pole in Campos top-five lockout of Eurocup-3 qualifying |url=https://formulascout.com/sztuka-takes-pole-in-campos-top-five-lockout-of-eurocup-3-qualifying/131738 |access-date=2025-12-18 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> but lost his lead after a first-lap [[safety car]]. Leaving it very late for the restart, his teammate [[Jesse Carrasquedo Jr.]] and Rivera both got past him before the first green flag corner. Sztuka then dropped further down, before a second safety car late in the race allowed him to retake third place. [[Álex Palou|Palou Motorsport]]'s [[James Egozi]] started the sprint race from pole position, leading KCL by MP's [[Alexander Abkhazava]] and MP's [[Valerio Rinicella]] to take a lights-to-flag victory, untroubled by a mid-race safety car restart.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-06-07 |title=Carrasquedo wins then Egozi takes reversed-grid honours in Eurocup-3 |url=https://formulascout.com/carrasquedo-wins-then-egozi-takes-reversed-grid-honours-in-eurocup-3/131768 |access-date=2025-12-18 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> The top four spots in the second qualifying session once again all belonged to Campos-run cars, with Carrasquedo Jr. fastest.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-06-08 |title=Carrasquedo grabs pole as Campos controls Eurocup-3 qualifying again |url=https://formulascout.com/carrasquedo-grabs-pole-as-campos-controls-eurocup-3-qualifying-again/131818 |access-date=2025-12-18 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> A five-place grid penalty for the Mexican saw Rivera start the race in first place instead, and he quickly was able to pull away as Colnaghi, Caranta and Sztuka fought over second behind him all race. Colnaghi won that battle on track, but a post-race penalty demoted him to seventh, giving second to Caranta, promoting Sztuka onto the podium and handing Rivera the championship lead.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-06-08 |title=Red Bull junior Rivera wins Eurocup-3's third Algarve race |url=https://formulascout.com/red-bull-junior-rivera-wins-eurocup-3s-third-algarve-race/131843 |access-date=2025-12-18 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

[[Circuit Paul Ricard]] hosted round three, also featuring a sprint race. Rivera led Colnaghi in the opening qualifying session,<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-06-21 |title=Rivera rises to the fore in Eurocup-3 Q1 at Paul Ricard |url=https://formulascout.com/rivera-rises-to-the-fore-in-eurocup-3-q1-at-paul-ricard/132132 |access-date=2025-12-21 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> but a slow start for the polesitter saw Colnaghi move in front right away as Rivera had to fend off Sztuka behind him. He remained right behind the leader all throughout the race, before attacking with two laps to go. That allowed Sztuka to join the fight. The pair collided, with Rinicella benefitting to take third, before Rivera ran off track at the final corner and fell to fourth behind both of them. [[Saintéloc Racing]]’s [[Garrett Berry]] had pole position for the sprint race. Egozi attacked Griffin Core's [[Francisco Macedo (racing driver)|Francisco Macedo]] for second, before he lost third to MP's [[Emerson Fittipaldi Jr.]] and the race was interrupted by a lengthy safety car. Only eight minutes were left when it was withdrawn, and the podium order remained static until the finish.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Allen |first=Peter |date=2025-06-21 |title=Colnaghi claims Eurocup-3 points lead at Paul Ricard with victory |url=https://formulascout.com/colnaghi-claims-points-lead-with-paul-ricard-victory-berry-wins-sprint/132149 |access-date=2025-12-21 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Sztuka inherited pole position for the final race after Carrasquedo Jr. had his fastest qualifying laps deleted.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-06-22 |title=Carrasquedo on pole in red flag-disrupted Eurocup-3 qualifying |url=https://formulascout.com/carrasquedo-on-pole-in-red-flag-disrupted-eurocup-3-qualifying/132227 |access-date=2025-12-21 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Rinicella beat him to turn one to take the lead as Sztuka fended off Rivera and Colnaghi. Sztuka kept close to the lead to retake it on the final lap, winning the race ahead of Rinicella and Rivera to take a six-point championship lead over Colnaghi in the process.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-06-22 |title=Sztuka plays patient game to win Eurocup-3's third Paul Ricard race |url=https://formulascout.com/sztuka-plays-patient-game-to-win-eurocup-3s-third-paul-ricard-race/132246 |access-date=2025-12-21 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

Round four at [[Monza Circuit]] brought an end to the season's first half. Rivera claimed pole position for the first race ahead of Rinicella and Gładysz.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-07-05 |title=Rivera denies Rinicella pole for Eurocup-3 race one at Monza by 0.015s |url=https://formulascout.com/rivera-denies-rinicella-pole-for-eurocup-3-race-one-at-monza-by-0-015s/132755 |access-date=2025-12-26 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> The first race began with multiple incidents and crashes, among them Rinicella and Gładysz, who both went off. That saw MP's [[Andrés Cárdenas]] move into second ahead of Rinicella, before the safety car then came out. Restarts on laps four and seven were both short-lived, and Rivera pulled away to win at the final restart, taking back the championship lead.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-07-05 |title=Rivera back into Eurocup-3 points lead by winning chaotic Monza race |url=https://formulascout.com/rivera-back-into-eurocup-3-points-lead-by-winning-chaotic-monza-race/132813 |access-date=2025-12-26 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Caranta took pole position for race two,<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-07-06 |title=Caranta quickest at Monza in red-flagged Eurocup-3 qualifying |url=https://formulascout.com/caranta-quickest-at-monza-in-red-flagged-eurocup-3-qualifying/132877 |access-date=2025-12-26 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> but had dropped to third behind Colnaghi and Rinicella by the first turn. He fought back into second, but fell further down the order as the race went on. Up front, Colnaghi defended from Rinicella to take victory and close up to four points behind Rivera, while Campos driver [[Enzo Tarnvanichkul]] completed the podium.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-07-06 |title=Colnaghi holds off Eurocup-3 team-mate Rinicella in Italian 1-2 at Monza |url=https://formulascout.com/eurocup-3-race-2/132906 |access-date=2025-12-26 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

==== Second half ====
Eurocup-3's debut at [[TT Circuit Assen]] opened up the season's second half. Championship chasers Colnaghi and Rinicella took the top two spots in the first qualifying session. Allay Racing's [[Michael Belov]] started third, and the top three all attacked each other in the opening part of the first race, but no positions changed all race. Colnaghi won the race on track, but a ten-second penalty for a starting procedure infringement saw him drop to third. Rinicella inherited the win and took the championship lead as Rivera did not score points.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-08-09 |title=Colnaghi reclaims Eurocup-3 lead with Assen win as Rivera non-scores |url=https://formulascout.com/colnaghi-reclaims-eurocup-3-lead-with-assen-win-as-rivera-non-scores/133722 |access-date=2025-12-28 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Colnaghi bounced back by taking pole position again for the second race. Belov started second, but mechanical problems saw him retire immediately at the start. That promoted Abkhazava to second as Rinicella moved to third, and the top three remained static afterwards to hand Colnaghi a twelve-point championship lead over Rinicella.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-08-10 |title=Colnaghi to the front again in Eurocup-3 with Assen pole and victory |url=https://formulascout.com/colnaghi-to-the-front-again-in-eurocup-3-with-assen-pole-and-victory/133766 |access-date=2025-12-28 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

Round six at [[Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps]] was up next, where Carrasquedo Jr. and Fittipaldi Jr. shared pole positions for the two races.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-09-05 |title=Carrasquedo and Fittipaldi share Eurocup-3 poles at Spa-Francorchamps |url=https://formulascout.com/carrasquedo-and-fittipaldi-share-eurocup-3-poles-at-spa-francorchamps/134320 |access-date=2025-12-28 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Carrasquedo Jr. held his lead at the start of race one as Colnaghi moved from fourth to second and Sztuka slotted into third. The Pole soon attacked Colnaghi for second, but could not get past and dropped to fourth behind Tarnvanichkul. Heavy rain then began to fall, with the race soon placed under safety car conditions, before the race was ended early after nine laps.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-09-05 |title=Carrasquedo converts pole into victory in shortened Eurocup-3 race one |url=https://formulascout.com/carrasquedo-converts-pole-into-victory-in-shortened-eurocup-3-race-one/134328 |access-date=2025-12-28 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Fittipaldi Jr. failed to convert his race two pole position. He stalled at the start and fell to the back, allowing Colnaghi to lead Rivera, Sztuka and Carrasquedo Jr., will all of them leading spells of the race. In the end, Rivera won ahead of Colnaghi and Carrasquedo Jr., with both Sztuka and Rinicella retiring to hand Colnaghi a sizable 48-point lead in the championship.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-09-06 |title=Rivera victorious at Spa as Colnaghi puts one hand on Eurocup-3 title |url=https://formulascout.com/rivera-victorious-at-spa-as-colnaghi-puts-one-hand-on-eurocup-3-title/134348 |access-date=2025-12-28 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

The penultimate round of 2025 was held at [[Circuito de Jerez]]. Sztuka was fastest in the opening qualifying session, but a grid penalty saw him start sixth as Tarnvanichkul inherited pole position ahead of Caranta and Colnaghi. The Italian tried taking the lead at the start of race one, but was forced to slot back into third as Caranta now tried to move past the race leader. Tarnvanichkul held on to the lead, before the race settled down and he was able to take his maiden win. Colnaghi secured pole position for the second race, ahead of Sztuka and Rinicella, with the two bonus points now meaning he would have to score only two points in the race to seal the championship title. That did not deter him from securing an unchallenged lights-to-flag victory to claim the title in style, with Sztuka coming second and Rivera third after moving past Rinicella at the start of the race.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Allen |first=Peter |date=2025-09-21 |title=Colnaghi crowned Eurocup-3 champion with a win at Jerez |url=https://formulascout.com/colnaghi-crowned-eurocup-3-champion-with-a-win-at-jerez/134741 |access-date=2025-12-29 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

With his title already wrapped up, Colnaghi opted to skip the final round at [[Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya|Barcelona]] to compete in the [[2025 Macau Grand Prix|Macau Grand Prix]]. Carrasquedo Jr. was fastest in qualifying for the first race,<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-11-15 |title=Carrasquedo claims pole for Eurocup-3 race one at Barcelona |url=https://formulascout.com/carrasquedo-claims-pole-for-eurocup-3-race-one-at-barcelona/136161 |access-date=2025-12-30 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> but penalties saw Sztuka inherit pole position ahead of Fittipaldi Jr. and Belov. Fittipaldi Jr. had a slow start and dropped out of contention right away, with Belov slotting into second ahead of Gładysz. A lengthy safety car for two separate incidents followed, and Sztuka pulled away at the restart to secure victory. Belov behind retired with a mechanical issue, allowing Rinicella to take second. A post-race penalty for Gładysz then promoted Caranta onto the podium.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-11-15 |title=Sztuka streaks to victory in Eurocup-3's first Barcelona race |url=https://formulascout.com/sztuka-streaks-to-victory-in-eurocup-3s-first-barcelona-race/136164 |access-date=2025-12-30 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Cárdenas was on pole for the second race and led throughout two safety car phases to claim his maiden victory. Rinicella in second secured the runner-up spot in the championship, while Carrasquedo Jr. claimed third place.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-11-16 |title=Eurocup-3 season ends at Barcelona with pole and victory for Cardenas |url=https://formulascout.com/eurocup-3-season-ends-at-barcelona-with-pole-and-victory-for-cardenas/136215 |access-date=2025-12-30 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

The opening part of the 2025 Eurocup-3 season was closely fought, with Colnaghi, Sztuka, Rivera and Rinicella all in the fight and each leading the standings during different parts of the campaign. But when Colnaghi won the second race in Monza, he would never step off the podium afterwards, distancing himself from his competitors to claim the title with two races to spare in the end. Colnaghi's season saw him take five victories, two more than any other driver could claim, as well as five further podiums, despite only competing in 16 of the 18 races. He also finished every race he started, and only did so without scoring points on one occasion. With everyone of his competitors retiring at least once, Colnaghi was able to consistently outscore his opposition during the second half of the year to claim the title as a rookie.

== Winter series ==
{{Motorsport season|series=Eurocup-3|title=[[Eurocup-3]] Spanish Winter Championship|year=2025|previous=none|footer=<small>Feeder series:<br>[[2025 F4 Spanish Championship#Winter series|Eurocup-4 Spanish Winter Championship]]</small>|subheader=<small>Drivers' Champion: </small>[[Maciej Gładysz]]<br><small>Rookie Champion: </small>[[Maciej Gładysz]]<br><small>Teams' Champion: </small>[[MP Motorsport]]|previouslink=|nextlink=2026 Eurocup-3 season#Winter series}}
[[File:Eurocup-3 2025 Spielberg Nr. 7 Gładysz.jpg|thumb|287x287px|The Eurocup-3 Spanish Winter Championship was won by [[MP Motorsport]]'s [[Maciej Gładysz]].]]
After Eurocup-3 held a one-off non-championship winter event in 2024, plans for a full winter championship were revealed in late September of the same year.<ref name="SWC" /> This winter championship, together with its Eurocup-4 counterpart, was then officially launched a few days later, with its calendar and event format also confirmed.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |date=4 October 2024 |title=The Eurocup-3 Spanish Winter Championship is born and confirms its 2025 calendar |url=https://eurocup3.org/new/the-eurocup-3-spanish-winter-championship-is-born-and-confirms-its-2025-calendar |access-date=2024-10-04 |website=eurocup3.org}}</ref>

[[MP Motorsport]] won the winter series' Teams' Championship at the penultimate round of the season, while [[Maciej Gładysz]], driving for MP Motorsport's KCL-branded satellite team, won the Drivers' Championship at the final race of the season and in doing so also became the Rookie Champion.

=== Teams and drivers ===
Teams utilized the same [[Tatuus F3 T-318]] chassis with a 270&nbsp;hp [[Alfa Romeo]]-Autotecnica engine and [[Hankook]] tires used in the main series.<ref name="TheCar"/>

=== Season report ===
The first edition of Eurocup-3's Winter series commenced at [[Circuito de Jerez]] with [[MP Motorsport]]’s [[Mattia Colnaghi]] taking pole position for the first race. At the start, [[Álex Palou|Palou Motorsport]]’s [[James Egozi]] challenged Colnaghi, but the Italian kept first place. He controlled the race afterwards to secure victory, while Egozi was closely pursued by KCL/MP's [[Maciej Gładysz]] throughout the event.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-02-08 |title=Spanish F4 champion Colnaghi makes perfect start to life in Eurocup-3 |url=https://formulascout.com/spanish-f4-champion-colnaghi-makes-perfect-start-to-life-in-eurocup-3/128166 |access-date=2025-04-03 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> The second race saw MP's [[Andrés Cárdenas]] starting from reverse-grid pole position. His teammate [[Emerson Fittipaldi Jr.]] lined up alongside him before being overtaken by [[Drivex]]’s [[Juan Cota]] on the opening lap. That allowed Cárdenas to establish a lead and he went on to claim victory, while Fittipaldi was unable to regain second place despite persistent efforts.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-02-08 |title=MP's Andres Cardenas keeps it cool for Eurocup-3 winter series win |url=https://formulascout.com/mps-andres-cardenas-keeps-it-cool-for-eurocup-3-winter-series-win/128175 |access-date=2025-04-03 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> In the final race of the weekend, Gładysz secured pole position in qualifying. However, [[Campos Racing]] drivers [[Jules Caranta]] and [[Nikola Tsolov]] overtook him on the opening lap. The pair spent the next laps battling, before they collided. That allowed Gładysz to reclaim the lead, while Caranta dropped to second and Tsolov retired. Gładysz went on to win, with Colnaghi finishing third and concluding the opening weekend with a four-point lead in the standings.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Scout |first=Formula |date=2025-02-09 |title=Gladysz wins after Red Bull juniors collide in Eurocup-3 winter series |url=https://formulascout.com/gladysz-wins-after-red-bull-juniors-collide-in-eurocup-3-winter-series/128218 |access-date=2025-04-03 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

The second round at the [[Algarve International Circuit]] featured a wet qualifying session, in which Rinicella secured pole position. The first race took place on a damp track, leading to an early reshuffling as Rinicella fell behind Egozi and Gładysz. Following a restart, Cárdenas joined the battle for the lead. Gładysz initially took first place while Egozi dropped to fourth, with Rinicella moving into second. The leading duo pulled away from the field before Rinicella reclaimed first place on the final lap to secure victory. In the second race, Egozi redeemed himself after his earlier loss. Starting from fourth, he overtook Rinicella and MP's [[Alexander Abkhazava]] before passing Drivex's pole-sitter [[Oscar Wurz]] on the second lap. Colnaghi and Rinicella also advanced past Wurz in the following laps but were unable to challenge Egozi for the win.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-02-22 |title=Rinicella pips Gladysz then Egozi gets redemptive Eurocup-3 Algarve win |url=https://formulascout.com/rinicella-pips-gladysz-then-egozi-gets-redemptive-eurocup-3-algarve-win/128494 |access-date=2025-04-04 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> The second qualifying session saw Rinicella claim another pole position. However, a slow start allowed Colnaghi to take the lead, with Gładysz slotting into third. The trio remained closely matched until Gładysz retired due to a technical issue, promoting Griffin Core's [[Kacper Sztuka]] to third. This result enabled Colnaghi to establish a 28-point advantage over Gładysz ahead of the final two races.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-02-23 |title=Rinicella's slow start hands Eurocup-3 victory to Colnaghi at Algarve |url=https://formulascout.com/rinicellas-slow-start-hands-eurocup-3-victory-to-colnaghi-at-algarve/128522 |access-date=2025-04-04 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

The final round of the Winter Championship took place at [[MotorLand Aragón]]. Gładysz was not ready to give up his title hopes, securing pole position for the first race and converting it into a lights-to-flag victory ahead of Griffin Core's [[Jesse Carrasquedo Jr.]] and Egozi. Colnaghi began the race in fourth but dropped to eighth following a slow start and later sustained damage in a collision, ultimately finishing 24th. With Gładysz also earning an additional point for the fastest lap, he and Colnaghi were tied on points heading into the final race.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-03-29 |title=Gladysz's perfect Saturday sets up winner-takes-all Eurocup-3 finale |url=https://formulascout.com/gladyszs-perfect-saturday-sets-up-winner-takes-all-eurocup-3-finale/129311 |access-date=2025-04-11 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> In qualifying for the final, Egozi secured pole position, with Gładysz lining up second. Colnaghi endured a difficult session and qualified 23rd, putting him at a significant disadvantage. That allowed Gładysz to be content with following Egozi home to finish second and secure the championship title.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-03-30 |title=MP's Maciej Gladysz crowned Eurocup-3 winter champion at Aragon |url=https://formulascout.com/mps-maciej-gladysz-crowned-eurocup-3-winter-champion-at-aragon/129330 |access-date=2025-04-11 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

The inaugural Spanish Winter Championship was a success by various metrics. Despite facing competition from established Formula Regional championships in the Middle East and Oceania, the series attracted a high number of entries from the outset, benefiting from the momentum generated by the Eurocup-3 series over the previous two years. Although the championship comprised only eight races held over three weekends, it featured a closely contested title battle between Gładysz, Colnaghi and Egozi.
