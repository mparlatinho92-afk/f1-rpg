The '''2025 Formula Regional European Championship by Alpine''' was a multi-event, [[Formula Regional]] [[Open-wheel car|open-wheel single seater]] [[Motorsport|motor racing]] championship held across Europe. The championship featured a mix of professional and amateur drivers, competing in Formula Regional cars that conform to the [[FIA]] Formula Regional regulations for the championship. This was the seventh season of the championship and the fifth after a merger with [[Formula Renault Eurocup]] which resulted in the change of the engine supplier to [[Automobiles Alpine|Alpine]].

[[Prema Racing]]'s [[Freddie Slater (racing driver)|Freddie Slater]] won the Drivers' Championship title at the penultimate race of the season, while [[Van Amersfoort Racing]]'s [[Dion Gowda]] clinched the Rookie Championship title at the same race. [[R-ace GP]] won the Team's Championship title for the second time.

== Teams and drivers ==
All teams compete using identical [[Tatuus FR-19]] cars powered by 1.8L Alpine-badged turbocharged [[Nissan MR engine|Nissan MR18]] engines on [[Pirelli]] tyres. === Team changes ===
[[Iron Dames]], who entered the championship in 2024 with a two-car all-female driver lineup, did not continue their entry into 2025 to focus on their endurance racing ventures.<ref name=":0" />

[[MP Motorsport]], a series mainstay ever since its inception, also left the championship. The team's entry was replaced by a new Italian-based Chinese team called CL Motorsport.<ref name=":0" /><ref name="MPCL">{{Cite web|work=Formula Scout|url=https://formulascout.com/bohra-joins-r-ace-gp-in-frec-as-david-leaves-team-for-cl-motorsport/126680|title=Bohra joins R-ace GP in FREC as David leaves team for CL Motorsport|date=30 December 2024|last=Wood|first=Ida|access-date=30 December 2024}}</ref>

Emirati team AKCEL GP joined the championship, becoming the first Asian team to enter the series.<ref>{{cite Instagram|postid=DHErOuKzgyR|title=We are glad to announce that AKCEL Gp will be the first team from Asian continent racing in FRECA 2025.|user=akcel_gp|author=AKCEL GP|date=11 March 2025}}</ref>

Despite being among the pre-selected teams for the 2025 season, [[KIC Motorsport]] discontinued their FRECA programme after having competed in the series since 2019.

[[Race Performance Motorsport|RPM]] signed two new drivers as reigning Rookies' Champion [[Noah Strømsted]] joined Trident in FIA F3 and Edgar Pierre moved to R-ace GP in the [[2025 Le Mans Cup|Le Mans Cup's LMP3 class]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Trident sign Noah Stromsted for the 2025 Formula 3 campaign|date=7 October 2024|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/4LlfkIS8huTUCcrkaxm86P/trident-sign-noah-stromsted-for-the-2025-formula-3-campaign|access-date=7 October 2024|website=FIA_Formula 3® - The Official F3® Website|language=en}}</ref><ref name="SeasonEntries">{{cite web|url=https://storage.googleapis.com/doc-prod/OTFK-DQIZciWatOb8Hd3e_yW.pdf|title=2025 Michelin Le Mans Cup - Season Entry List|website=[[Google APIs]]|publisher=[[Automobile Club de l'Ouest]]|date=4 February 2025|access-date=4 February 2025}}</ref> To replace them, RPM signed [[Enzo Peugeot]], who finished the 2024 season in 15th with Saintéloc Racing, and [[Enzo Yeh|Rui-Heng Yeh]], who stepped up to Formula Regional after taking two podiums in two years of F4 competition.<ref name="RPM"/>

[[Trident Motorsport|Trident]] also took on two new drivers as [[Alpine Academy]] driver [[Nicola Lacorte]] and [[Roman Bilinski]] both stepped up to FIA F3 with [[DAMS]] and [[Rodin Motorsport]] respectively.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Nicola Lacorte joins DAMS Lucas Oil for 2025 season|url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/7cBVzRDMwVDpHyKidEBrWR/nicola-lacorte-joins-dams-lucas-oil-for-2025-season|access-date=29 October 2024|website=FIAFormula3® - The Official F3® Website |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Roman Bilinski completes Rodin Motorsport’s 2025 Formula 3 lineup |url=https://www.fiaformula3.com/Latest/61CdDa63ZKiFpDY2aBJaX/roman-bilinski-completes-rodin-motorsports-2025-formula-3-lineup |access-date=2024-11-05 |website=FIAFormula3® - The Official F3® Website |language=en}}</ref> The team signed two drivers switching from other teams in [[Matteo De Palo]], who finished his debut season in 2024 17th with Saintéloc Racing, and [[Nandhavud Bhirombhakdi]], who came 24th with KIC Motorsport in 2024.<ref>{{Cite web|date=11 December 2024 |title=Trident announce De Palo for 2025 season |url=https://www.tridentmotorsport.com/en/news/trident-announce-de-palo-for-2025-season.html|access-date=11 December 2024 |website=Trident Motorsport}}</ref><ref name=":11">{{Cite web|title=Bhirombhakdi to race with Trident in the 2025 FRECA |url=https://www.tridentmotorsport.com/en/news/bhirombhakdi-to-race-with-trident-in-the-2025-freca.html|date=13 December 2024|access-date=13 December 2024|website=Trident Motorsport}}</ref>

G4 Racing saw [[Kanato Le]] leave the team to join ART Grand Prix, while Romain Andriolo switched to the [[2025 GT World Challenge Europe Endurance Cup|GT World Challenge Europe Endurance Cup]] with [[Ford Motor Company|HRT Ford Performance]] and none of the team's three part-time competitors rejoined the series in 2025.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.dailysportscar.com/2025/04/03/mardenborough-return-headlines-hrt-ford-endurance-cup-roster.html|title=Mardenborough Return Headlines HRT Ford Endurance Cup Roster|website=Dailysportscar|last=Goodwin|first=Graham|date=3 April 2025|access-date=3 April 2025}}</ref> G4 signed Édouard Borgna, who graduated from French F4 after finishing the 2024 season in 19th, as well as taking on [[2025 Ultimate Cup European Series|Ultimate Cup Series Formula Cup]] driver Arthur Aegerter and signing [[2025 Eurocup-3 season|Eurocup-3]] driver [[Kacper Sztuka]] to compete in the opening round as well as the round at Imola.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Edouard Borgna Joins G4 Racing for a New Chapter in FRECA 2025|url=https://g4-racing.com/edouard-borgna-joins-g4-racing-for-a-new-chapter-in-freca-2025/|date=28 March 2025|access-date=28 March 2025|website=G4 Racing}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Nicola |first=Francesco Di |date=2025-04-30 |title=G4 Racing Welcomes Kacper Sztuka for the Opening Round of the 2025 FRECA Season |url=https://formularegionaleubyalpine.com/g4-racing-welcomes-kacper-sztuka-for-the-opening-round-of-the-2025-freca-season/ |access-date=2025-05-01 |website=Formula Regional by Alpine |language=it-IT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Nicola |first=Francesco Di |date=2025-04-30 |title=G4 Racing confirms Arthur Aegerter for the opening round of the 2025 FRECA season |url=https://formularegionaleubyalpine.com/g4-racing-confirms-arthur-aegerter-for-the-opening-round-of-the-2025-freca-season/ |access-date=2025-05-01 |website=Formula Regional by Alpine |language=it-IT}}</ref>

KIC announced no drivers ahead of the 2025 season, while [[Nandhavud Bhirombhakdi]] left the team to join [[Trident Motorsport|Trident]], [[Alex Sawer]] switched to [[2025 Formula 4 South East Asia Championship|Formula 4 South East Asia]] with [[Evans GP]] and none of the other five drivers the team fielded in 2024 returned to the series.<ref name=":11"/><ref>{{Cite web|last=Intan|first=Maiya|date=2025-05-01|title=F4 South East Asia: 2025 season guide|url=https://feederseries.net/2025/05/01/f4-south-east-asia-2025-season-guide/|website=Feeder Series}}</ref>

New team CL Motorsport signed [[Zachary David]], who embarked on his second season in the championship after coming 13th with [[R-ace GP]] in 2024.<ref name="David"/> [[Valerio Rinicella]], who came 20th driving for MP Motorsport, drove for the team at the opening round in place of David, as he prioritized his [[2025 Super Formula Lights|Super Formula Lights]] campaign with [[B-Max Racing Team]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://formularegionaleubyalpine.com/valerio-rinicella-to-face-for-cl-motorsport-at-misano/|title=Valerio Rinicella to race for CL Motorsport at Misano|website=formularegionaleubyalpine.com|date=28 April 2025|access-date=28 April 2025|language=en}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |date=20 February 2025 |title=2025 SFライツ参戦体制 |url=http://www.b-maxracing.co.jp/superformulalights/20250220sfl_team_structure/ |access-date=20 February 2025 |work=B-Max Racing Team}}</ref>

New team AKCEL GP signed two FRECA debutants in Aditya Kulkarni, who made his category debut by coming 20th in [[2025 Formula Regional Middle East Championship|FRMEC]], also driving for AKCEL GP, and [[Saqer Al-Maosherji]], who previously competed in the [[2024 F4 Saudi Arabian Championship|F4 Saudi Arabian Championship]], coming sixth.<ref>{{Cite web |title=ACI Racing Weekend Misano, 3-4 Maggio 2025 - Formula Regional EU Championship by Alpine - Entry List |url=https://livedataacisport.perugiatiming.com/Iscritti/60048fdf-7ffc-4ecd-87d8-2db9a05a2077/10 |access-date=2025-05-01 |website=livedataacisport.perugiatiming.com}}</ref>

Departing team [[MP Motorsport]] saw [[Nikita Bedrin]] move to [[Saintéloc Racing]], and [[Valerio Rinicella]] join its [[2025 Eurocup-3 season|Eurocup-3]] outfit, with whom he already contested the one-off non-championship round in 2024.<ref name="Bedrin"/><ref>{{Cite web|date=18 December 2024|title=Rinicella to continue with MP Motorsport in Eurocup-3|url=https://www.mpmotorsport.com/valerio-rinicella-joins-eurocup-3-mp-motorsport-2025/|access-date=18 December 2024|website=MP Motorsport|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20241219190313/https://www.mpmotorsport.com/valerio-rinicella-joins-eurocup-3-mp-motorsport-2025/|archive-date=19 December 2024|url-status=dead}}</ref>

Departing team [[Iron Lynx|Iron Dames]] saw [[Doriane Pin]] moving to [[Prema Racing]], while [[Marta García (racing driver)|Marta García]] exited [[Single-seaters|single-seater]] motorsport to join the team's outfit in the [[Le Mans Cup]].<ref name=":1" /><ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-01-30 |title=Iron Dames reveal 2025 Michelin Le Mans Cup line-up |url=https://www.irondames.ch/news/iron-dames-reveal-dynamic-2025-michelin-le-mans-cup-line-upmarta-garcia-to-partner-vanina-ickx/ |access-date=2025-01-30 |website=IRON DAMES |language=en}}</ref>

==== Mid-season ====
CL Motorsport expanded to a two-car lineup ahead of the round at Spa, but with Rinicella only entering the first round and David still prioritizing his Super Formula Lights campaign, the team fielded [[Michael Belov]] and [[Macéo Capietto]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Fasano |first=Emanuele |date=2025-05-13 |title=CL Motorsport lines up with two-car entry at Spa Francorchamps |url=https://formularegionaleubyalpine.com/cl-motorsport-lines-up-with-two-car-entry-at-spa-francorchamps/ |access-date=2025-05-13 |website=Formula Regional by Alpine |language=}}</ref> Capietto left the team after just one round, leading to CL Motorsport only fielding Belov at Circuit Zandvoort.

After only fielding two cars in the second and third round, G4 Racing announced ahead of the round at the Hungaroring that [[Edu Robinson]], who had made his FR-level debut in Eurocup-3 in June, would join the team for the rest of the season.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-06-25 |title=Edu Robinson joins G4 Racing in FRegional Europe for rest of season |url=https://formulascout.com/edu-robinson-joins-g4-racing-in-fregional-europe-for-rest-of-season/132333 |access-date=2025-06-25 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Aegerter meanwhile left the team and was replaced by another UCES Formula Cup driver, reigning champion [[Enzo Richer]], for the fourth round of the year.<ref>{{Cite web |date=30 June 2025 |title=G4 Racing on Instagram: 'G4 Racing welcomes Enzo Richer for the Hungaroring Round of the 2025 Formula Regional European Championship by Alpine' |url=https://www.instagram.com/g4racing_/p/DLhV_vAt7DV/?hl=de |access-date=30 June 2025 |website=[[Instagram]]}}</ref> AKCEL GP also saw a driver change as the team signed Eurocup-3 runner-up [[Javier Sagrera]] for the remainder of the campaign.<ref>{{Cite web |date=25 June 2025 |title=Feeder Series on X: 'DRIVER ANNOUNCEMENT {{!}} 🇪🇸 Javier Sagrera joins Akcel GP for the remainder of the 2025 FRECA season!' |url=https://x.com/feeder_series/status/1937921714874208701 |access-date=25 June 2025 |website=X}}</ref> [[Enzo Peugeot]] left [[Race Performance Motorsport|RPM]] and was replaced by Formula Regional debutant [[Ean Eyckmans]] for rounds four and five.<ref>{{Cite web |date=1 July 2025 |title=Race Performance Motorsport on Instagram: 'We are pleased to welcome Belgium driver @ean_eyckmans to the RPM team 🇧🇪🤝' |url=https://www.instagram.com/p/DLkOXs_NNQK/?hl=de |access-date=1 July 2025 |website=[[Instagram]]}}</ref> Saintéloc's Tim Gerhards was forced to sit out the round after sustaining an injury in a bicycle accident.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2 July 2025 |title=Feeder Series on X: 'NEWS {{!}} 🇳🇱 Tim Gerhards will miss this weekend's FRECA round at the Hungaroring, after he was injured in a bicycle accident.' |url=https://x.com/feeder_series/status/1940328801201959203 |access-date=2 July 2025 |website=X}}</ref> He was replaced by [[Maya Weug]], who returned to FRECA where she contested 22 races over two seasons.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2 July 2025 |title=Saintéloc Racing on Instagram: '@maya_weug to join @sainteloc_racing for FRECA Hungaroring race.' |url=https://www.instagram.com/p/DLnT4JhReaA/?hl=de |access-date=2 July 2025 |website=[[Instagram]]}}</ref>

Both Gerhards and Aegerter returned for round five at Circuit Paul Ricard, while all three AKCEL GP drivers skipped the event.<ref>{{Cite web |date=17 July 2025 |title=Feeder Series on X: 'NEWS {{!}} 🇫🇷 Here is the entry list for this weekend's FRECA round at Le Castellet!' |url=https://x.com/feeder_series/status/1945802421151612942 |access-date=17 July 2025 |website=X}}</ref>

Round six saw the return of both AKCEL GP and Sztuka at G4 Racing, who this time stepped in for the once again absent Aegerter.

CL Motorsport entered three cars in round seven at Spielberg, with the entry of the once again absent David as well as the team's third car piloted by F4 graduates [[Zhenrui Chi]] and [[Enea Frey]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=1 September 2025 |title=CL MOTORSPORT SET FOR THREE-CAR LINE-UP AT ROUND 7 IN SPIELBERG – CL Motorsport |url=https://clmotorsport.it/cl-motorsport-set-for-three-car-line-up-at-round-7-in-spielberg/ |access-date=1 September 2025 |website=CL Motorsport |language=en}}</ref> AKCEL GP meanwhile withdrew its entry into the series ahead of the Spielberg round, with [[Saqer Al-Maosherji]] moving over to G4 Racing.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Fasano |first=Emanuele |date=2 September 2025 |title=The Formula Regional European Championship by Alpine returns at the Red Bull Ring for Round 7 of the 2025 season |url=https://formularegionaleubyalpine.com/the-formula-regional-european-championship-by-alpine-returns-at-the-red-bull-ring-for-round-7-of-the-2025-season/ |access-date=2 September 2025 |website=[[Formula Regional European Championship]] |language=en}}</ref> Prema Racing also welcomed a new driver, with [[Alex Powell]] making his Formula Regional debut as a guest driver in place of Doriane Pin.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2 September 2025 |title=PREMA returns to FRECA action in Spielberg, adds Powell as wildcard entry |url=https://premaracing.com/en/news/19611-r07-spielberg-freca-race-preview |access-date=2 September 2025 |website=[[Prema Racing]] |language=en}}</ref> [[Enzo Yeh|Rui-Heng Yeh]] did not enter the round at Spielberg and was replaced by [[Reno Francot]], another Formula Regional debutant.<ref>{{Cite web |date=4 September 2025 |title=2025 - FRECA - ROUND 07 - RED BULL RING - PROVISIONAL ENTRY LIST V5 |url=https://formularegionaleubyalpine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/2025-FRECA-Rd07-REDBULLRING-02-Provisional-Entry-List_v5.pdf |access-date=4 September 2025 |website=[[Formula Regional European Championship]]}}</ref>

Ahead of round eight at Barcelona, Prema Racing announced it would field [[Mercedes Junior Team|Mercedes junior]] [[Yuanpu Cui]] in the car previously piloted by Powell and Pin.<ref>{{Cite web |date=17 September 2025 |title=PREMA Racing set for Barcelona FRECA event |url=https://premaracing.com/en/news/19623-r08-barcelona-freca-race-preview |access-date=17 September 2025 |website=[[Prema Racing]] |language=en}}</ref> Francot and Eyckmans left RPM and were replaced by the team's former drivers, FIA F3 driver [[Santiago Ramos (racing driver)|Santiago Ramos]] and Macéo Capietto, who previously competed in round two for CL Motorsport.<ref>{{Cite web |date=17 September 2025 |title=Race Performance Motorsport on Instagram: 'Welcome back Maćeo and Santiago! 🤩🇫🇷🇲🇽' |url=https://www.instagram.com/p/DOtl69qjHrr/?hl=en |access-date=18 September 2025 |website=[[Instagram]]}}</ref> That team meanwhile was down to one car again as both Chi and Frey skipped the event.

RPM brought a new lineup to round nine, with Ramos and Capietto replaced by Sztuka, who had earlier competed for G4 Racing, and [[Eurocup-3]] driver [[James Egozi]], who made his series debut. Both Chi and Frey meanwhile returned to CL Motorsport.<ref>{{Cite web |date=1 October 2025 |title=kacpersztuka_racing and rpm.team_ on Instagram: 'I love coming to you with news like this! 🔥 This weekend, I'll be racing at the Hockenheimring for the first time in my career 🇩🇪 I'll be racing in the FRECA series with @rpm.team_!' |url=https://www.instagram.com/kacpersztuka_racing/p/DPRSGQ2iLqI/ |access-date=1 October 2025 |website=[[Instagram]]}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=3 October 2025 |title=Feeder Series weekend preview and schedule: 03–05 October|url=https://feederseries.net/2025/10/03/feeder-series-weekend-preview-and-schedule-03-05-october/|access-date=3 October 2025 |website=Feeder Series}}</ref>

For the final round of the season, RPM changed their lineup again: Sztuka and Egozi were superseded by Formula 4 graduates [[Tomass Štolcermanis]] and [[Jan Przyrowski]]. Egozi meanwhile joined [[Saintéloc Racing]] in place of [[Tim Gerhards]], who ended his campaign a round early.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://formularegionaleubyalpine.com/corsa/monza-2025/2025-freca-rd10-monza-02-provisional-entry-list/|title=2025 FRECA-Rd10-MONZA -#02-Provisional Entry List|website=formularegionaleubyalpine.com|date=22 October 2025 |access-date=22 October 2025 |language=en}}</ref>

== Race calendar ==
Ten planned circuits for the 2025 season were first announced in August of 2024.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Fasano |first=Emanuele |date=2024-08-20 |title=Formula Regional European Championship by Alpine: The key numbers driving its success |url=https://formularegionaleubyalpine.com/formula-regional-european-championship-by-alpine-the-key-numbers-driving-its-success/ |access-date=2024-09-27 |website=Formula Regional by Alpine |language=it-IT}}</ref> The provisional calendar, with two events yet to be dated, was announced on 27 September 2024,<ref>{{Cite web |last=Fasano |first=Emanuele |date=2024-09-27 |title=The 2025 Formula Regional European Championship by Alpine: A revamped calendar for the upcoming season |url=https://formularegionaleubyalpine.com/the-2025-formula-regional-european-championship-by-alpine-a-revamped-calendar-for-the-upcoming-season/ |access-date=2024-09-27 |website=Formula Regional by Alpine |language=it-IT}}</ref> before being finalized on 2 October 2024. [[Mugello Circuit|Mugello]] was not part of the circuit lineup for the first time in series history, with the championship instead opting to return to [[Misano World Circuit Marco Simoncelli|Misano]], where it last raced in 2020.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2024-09-27 |title=FREC swaps Mugello for Misano as 2025 calendar is revealed |url=https://formulascout.com/frec-swaps-mugello-for-misano-as-2025-calendar-is-revealed/124258 |access-date=2024-09-27 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Three pre-season tests were held at [[Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya|Barcelona]], Misano and [[Circuit Paul Ricard|Paul Ricard]].

== Season report ==

=== Opening rounds ===
The 2025 Formula Regional European Championship began with the series' return to [[Misano World Circuit Marco Simoncelli|Misano Circuit]], and [[Prema Racing|Prema]]'s [[Freddie Slater (racing driver)|Freddie Slater]] set the pace in the opening qualifying session to secure pole position for the first race ahead of [[Trident Motorsport|Trident]]’s [[Matteo De Palo]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gascoigne |first=Roger |date=2025-05-03 |title=Freddie Slater flies to pole in first qualifying of FREC season |url=https://formulascout.com/freddie-slater-flies-to-pole-in-first-qualifying-of-frec-season/130363 |access-date=2025-11-05 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> However, Slater lost the lead off the line at the start of the first race. He was then tagged into a spin by [[R-ace GP]]’s [[Jin Nakamura]] at Turn 1, eliminating both and bringing out the safety car. De Palo inherited first place ahead of [[ART Grand Prix]]’s [[Evan Giltaire]] and maintained a stable gap of around two seconds to the flag to claim Trident’s maiden Formula Regional Europe win. Prema's [[Rashid Al Dhaheri]] finished third.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gascoigne |first=Roger |date=2025-05-03 |title=De Palo wins as Slater spins out of FREC opener at Misano |url=https://formulascout.com/de-palo-wins-as-slater-spins-out-of-frec-opener-at-misano/130400 |access-date=2025-11-05 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Giltaire then topped the second qualifying session to start from pole position for race two, ahead of Slater.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gascoigne |first=Roger |date=2025-05-04 |title=ART GP's Giltaire snatches pole for FREC's second Misano race |url=https://formulascout.com/art-gps-giltaire-snatches-pole-for-frecs-second-misano-race/130445 |access-date=2025-11-05 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> In a race punctuated by three safety car periods following separate incidents for [[Race Performance Motorsport|RPM]]'s [[Giovanni Maschio]], [[Van Amersfoort Racing|VAR]]'s [[Dion Gowda]] and [[Saintéloc Racing|Saintéloc]]'s [[Tim Gerhards]], Giltaire led every lap to take victory ahead of Slater and De Palo to leave the first round leading the championship.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gascoigne |first=Roger |date=2025-05-04 |title=Giltaire "bounces back" with controlled FREC race two win at Misano |url=https://formulascout.com/giltaire-bounces-back-with-controlled-frec-race-two-win-at-misano/130475 |access-date=2025-11-05 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

Qualifying for race one at [[Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps|Spa]] was abandoned after AKCEL GP's [[Saqer Al-Maosherji]] crashed and track repairs were needed. With Group B unable to run, the grid was set from free practice times, handing Slater pole position ahead of R-ace GP's [[Enzo Deligny]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-05-17 |title=FREC qualifying cancelled at Spa due to track maintenance |url=https://formulascout.com/frec-qualifying-cancelled-at-spa-due-to-track-maintenance/130968 |access-date=2025-11-05 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> At the start, Deligny snatched the lead on the Kemmel Straight before a safety car neutralised the race. Slater reclaimed the advantage on lap eight after briefly cutting Les Combes earlier and yielding the position back. De Palo moved into second to complete the podium three laps from the end, passing Deligny in identical fashion.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-05-17 |title=Slater wins, De Palo reclaims points lead in FREC race one at Spa |url=https://formulascout.com/slater-wins-de-palo-reclaims-points-lead-in-frec-race-one-at-spa/131015 |access-date=2025-11-05 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Sunday’s qualifying saw Al Dhaheri secure pole with in foggy conditions, ahead of VAR's [[Hiyu Yamakoshi]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-05-18 |title=Al Dhaheri takes pole for FREC race two at Spa in dramatic qualifying |url=https://formulascout.com/al-dhaheri-takes-pole-for-frec-race-two-at-spa-in-dramatic-qualifying/131069 |access-date=2025-11-05 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> In race two, Deligny overtook both of them before Les Combes before the first of multiple safety cars interrupted proceedings. Yamakoshi moved back to the front later on, briefly leading the race, but finished second as Deligny secured victory. ART Grand Prix's [[Taito Kato]] finished third, while misfortune for Slater and Giltaire saw De Palo claim the championship lead.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-05-18 |title=Deligny delivers victory in FREC race two at Spa-Francorchamps |url=https://formulascout.com/deligny-delivers-victory-in-frec-race-two-at-spa-francorchamps/131116 |access-date=2025-11-05 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

VAR's [[Pedro Clerot]] secured his maiden pole position in mixed conditions in qualifying at [[Circuit Zandvoort|Zandvoort]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gascoigne |first=Roger |date=2025-06-07 |title=Clerot claims maiden FREC pole on home soil for VAR at wet Zandvoort |url=https://formulascout.com/pedro-clerot-claims-maiden-frec-on-home-soil-for-var-at-wet-zandvoort/131723 |access-date=2025-11-18 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Slater started third and made the fastest launch in race one to move between Clerot and R-ace GP's [[Akshay Bohra]] into Tarzan and take the lead before a safety car appeared for a collision between [[G4 Racing]]'s Édouard Borgna and AKCEL GP's Aditya Kulkarni. He controlled the restart and, after a second neutralisation caused by Saintéloc's [[Nikita Bedrin]] stopping on track, held position to win, while Clerot resisted sustained pressure from Bohra to finish second by 0.029 seconds.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gascoigne |first=Roger |date=2025-06-07 |title=Super start sets up Slater for controlled FREC win at Zandvoort |url=https://formulascout.com/super-start-sets-up-slater-for-controlled-frec-win-at-zandvoort/131771 |access-date=2025-11-18 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Clerot then claimed another pole position in qualifying for race two ahead of Yamakoshi, with Slater third.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gascoigne |first=Roger |date=2025-06-08 |title=VAR's Clerot and Yamakoshi fill front row for FREC race two at Zandvoort |url=https://formulascout.com/vars-clerot-and-yamakoshi-fill-front-row-for-frec-race-two-at-zandvoort/131808 |access-date=2025-11-18 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> In the race, Clerot maintained the lead through an early safety-car period for Gerhards’s stoppage and headed a VAR one-two, with Yamakoshi holding off Slater, who in turn kept Deligny at bay. Slater ended the weekend as the third different championship leader in as many rounds, leading the standings on 83 points, five ahead of De Palo on 78.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gascoigne |first=Roger |date=2025-06-08 |title=Clerot leads VAR 1-2 in FREC race two at Zandvoort |url=https://formulascout.com/clerot-leads-var-1-2-in-frec-race-two-at-zandvoort/131835 |access-date=2025-11-18 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

=== Mid-season rounds ===
Qualifying at the [[Hungaroring]] brought another maiden polesitter in De Palo, who secured pole position for race one ahead of Clerot.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-07-05 |title=De Palo ends long wait for first FREC pole in Q1 at Hungaroring |url=https://formulascout.com/de-palo-ends-long-wait-for-first-frec-pole-in-q1-at-hungaroring/132757 |access-date=2025-11-18 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> In the first race, he kept the lead into turn one, while Deligny passed Al Dhaheri for third. De Palo maintained control through two safety-car periods, leaving only a single green-flag lap. Clerot resisted repeated attacks from Deligny for second, and Al Dhaheri kept Slater at bay for fourth. De Palo won ahead of Clerot and Deligny to retake the championship lead.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-07-05 |title=De Palo dominant at Hungaroring to move into FREC points lead |url=https://formulascout.com/de-palo-dominant-at-hungaroring-to-move-into-frec-points-lead/132781 |access-date=2025-11-18 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Deligny earned pole position for race two,<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-07-06 |title=R-ace GP's Enzo Deligny earns his first FREC pole at Hungaroring |url=https://formulascout.com/r-ace-gps-enzo-deligny-earns-his-first-frec-pole-at-hungaroring/132868 |access-date=2025-11-18 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> but after leading the early laps he ceded the position on lap 13 when he slowed unexpectedly before turn 12 and Slater moved past. Deligny briefly retook the lead into turn one but dropped behind Slater, Al Dhaheri and De Palo when the same issue recurred. Slater managed the remaining laps to win. Deligny initially recovered to second, but a technical infringement saw him disqualified after the race. De Palo therefore moved up to third to retain a three-point championship lead over Slater.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-07-06 |title=Slater wins after Deligny has two slow moments in FREC race two |url=https://formulascout.com/slater-wins-after-deligny-has-two-slow-moments-in-frec-race-two/132924 |access-date=2025-11-18 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

The first half of the season concluded at [[Circuit Paul Ricard|Paul Ricard]], where Slater led De Palo in qualifying for the first race. He controlled race one from the start and steadily extended his advantage, eventually winning by 7.9 seconds. The top two remained steady as CL Motorsport's [[Michael Belov]] moved into third at the start. That place was short-lived, however, as Kato retook the place around the outside on lap two. Bohra and Clerot also passed Belov as the race settled, with the pair fighting for fourth place, allowing Kato to retain the podium. Slater started race two from pole position again, this time ahead of Rashid Al Dhaheri, who defended from Bohra on the opening lap. A collision between Prema's [[Doriane Pin]] and Maschio at turn one brought out the safety car and then the red flag, with racing resuming on lap eight. Slater retained control at the restart, while De Palo’s attempt to pass Bohra ended with an off-track excursion that allowed Clerot and then Giltaire through. Slater's double win saw him leave Paul Ricard with a 20-point lead over De Palo.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-07-26 |title=Slater takes two wins and FREC points lead at Paul Ricard |url=https://formulascout.com/slater-takes-two-wins-and-frec-points-lead-at-paul-ricard/133224 |access-date=2025-12-09 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

Round six at [[Imola Circuit|Imola]] saw Deligny claim pole position for the first race. The Frenchman led away in race one as Slater slipped momentarily behind Clerot and Bohra; early incidents for Hiyu Yamakoshi and CL Motorsport's [[Zachary David]] brought out a red flag and the race was further interrupted by two safety-car periods, including one for Al Dhaheri’s late exit. Deligny managed both restarts and took the victory, Slater recovered to finish second and Bohra completed the podium, while De Palo finished in the top five.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-08-02 |title=Enzo Deligny delivers in FREC on Saturday at Imola |url=https://formulascout.com/enzo-deligny-delivers-in-frec-on-saturday-at-imola/133549 |access-date=2025-12-09 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Bohra then edged Slater to take pole position for race two. He kept the lead from the start despite wheelspin, but the race was neutralised twice following heavy incidents involving Maschio and then contact between Nakamura and Prema's [[Jack Beeton]]. Bohra won, with Slater crossing the line second, but he was disqualified after the race for a technical infringement. That promoted De Palo to second and elevated Bedrin to third. Slater’s exclusion reduced his championship cushion to 15 points over De Palo, with Deligny remaining third.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-08-03 |title=Akshay Bohra turns first FREC pole at Imola into victory |url=https://formulascout.com/akshay-bohra-turns-first-frec-pole-at-imola-into-victory/133587 |access-date=2025-12-09 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

The [[Red Bull Ring]] hosted round seven, and qualifying saw Slater's woes continue: A lap time deletion saw him qualify 18th for the opening race, while pole position went to De Palo.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-09-06 |title=De Palo on pole, Slater 18th in qualifying for FREC Red Bull Ring race one |url=https://formulascout.com/de-palo-on-pole-slater-18th-in-qualifying-for-frec-red-bull-ring-race-one/134351 |access-date=2025-12-25 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> The Italian led Bohra and Clerot, before Bedrin took third and a safety car interrupted the race. Bohra dropped down the order after the restart, allowing Bedrin into second and Deligny onto the podium, but no one challenged De Palo as he drove to victory, reclaiming the championship lead in the process.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-09-06 |title=De Palo into FREC lead with Red Bull Ring win, Nakamura goes on charge |url=https://formulascout.com/de-palo-into-frec-lead-with-red-bull-ring-win-nakamura-goes-on-charge/134366 |access-date=2025-12-25 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Qualifying for the second race saw Clerot claim pole position ahead of Deligny, with Slater starting sixth and De Palo eleventh.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-09-07 |title=Clerot clinches FREC race two pole ahead of Deligny at Red Bull Ring |url=https://formulascout.com/clerot-clinches-frec-race-two-pole-ahead-of-deligny-at-red-bull-ring/134385 |access-date=2025-12-25 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> De Palo's race unravelled right at the start, after heavy defending from Bohra ahead saw him sustain damage. He finished the race three laps down after multiple pit stops for repairs. At the front, Clerot defended his lead from Deligny as Nakamura slotted into third. That order remained static until the finish, with Clerot winning the race as Slater took fourth place to retake a three-point championship lead over De Palo.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Scout |first=Formula |date=2025-09-07 |title=Clerot wins as De Palo non-score hands FREC lead back to Slater |url=https://formulascout.com/clerot-wins-as-de-palo-retirement-hands-frec-lead-back-to-slater/134410 |access-date=2025-12-25 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

=== Closing rounds ===
Slater beat De Palo by 0.029 seconds in qualifying for the first race at [[Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-09-20 |title=Slater takes pole for FREC race one at Barcelona by 0.029s |url=https://formulascout.com/slater-takes-pole-for-frec-race-one-at-barcelona-by-0-029s/134685 |access-date=2025-12-26 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> That race started with four cars side-by-side into the first corner, with De Palo slotting into the lead, Slater consigned into second and Nakamura taking third. The Japanese lost that place to Clerot on lap four, with the Brazilian then attacking Slater for second, allowing De Palo to build a gap. Clerot attempted a move on Slater on lap 18, but could not find a way past, and the order at the front remained static afterwards. De Palo's win saw him move back into the championship lead.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Ida |date=2025-09-20 |title=De Palo returns to FREC lead by beating Slater to Barcelona win |url=https://formulascout.com/de-palo-returns-to-frec-lead-by-beating-slater-to-barcelona-victory/134713 |access-date=2025-12-26 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Qualifying for race two was held in heavy rain, and Deligny claimed pole position. The race was started behind the safety car, which was called back out straight away after a crash. Deligny spent the race fending off Clerot, holding on to claim victory, with Bohra third. Slater looked set to finish fourth, before running off track and dropping to fifth. With De Palo finishing second, that saw the two title protagonists now level on points with four races left in the season.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Allen |first=Peter |date=2025-09-21 |title=Deligny beats Clerot to Barcelona FREC win as title rivals move level |url=https://formulascout.com/deligny-beats-clerot-to-barcelona-frec-win-as-title-rivals-move-level/134730 |access-date=2025-12-26 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

The penultimate round was held at the [[Hockenheimring]], where Deligny set the fastest time in qualifying for race one, but a grid penalty saw De Palo inherit pole position ahead of Bohra.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gascoigne |first=Roger |date=2025-10-04 |title=Deligny tops FREC qualifying again but De Palo lands Hockenheim pole |url=https://formulascout.com/deligny-tops-frec-qualifying-again-but-de-palo-lands-hockenheim-pole/134981 |access-date=2025-12-27 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Rain ahead of the race caused a safety car start, and when it was withdrawn, Slater started moving up the order. He started fourth, moved past Deligny on the first green flag lap, before attacking and passing Bohra on lap nine. The top two then began to battle, both ran wide and dropped behind Bohra. Slater was now ahead of De Palo, and he reclaimed the lead to take victory.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gascoigne |first=Roger |date=2025-10-04 |title=Slater battles to thrilling win and FREC points lead at Hockenheim |url=https://formulascout.com/slater-battles-to-thrilling-win-and-frec-points-lead-at-hockenheim/135035 |access-date=2025-12-27 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Deligny led De Palo again in the second qualifying session.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gascoigne |first=Roger |date=2025-10-03 |title=Deligny dominates qualifying for FREC's second Hockenheim race |url=https://formulascout.com/deligny-dominates-qualifying-for-frecs-second-hockenheim-race/134955 |access-date=2025-12-27 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> It rained again before the race, causing slippery conditions. Deligny converted his pole position into the lead, heading De Palo through an early safety car as Giltaire moved into third. A second safety car was called later on, under which heavy rain started to fall. That prompted a red flag, and the race was not resumed, awarding Deligny victory and allowing De Palo to close the championship gap to Slater to four points.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gascoigne |first=Roger |date=2025-10-05 |title=Deligny wins rain-affected second FREC race at Hockenheim |url=https://formulascout.com/deligny-wins-rain-affected-second-frec-race-at-hockenheim/135057 |access-date=2025-12-27 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

Monza hosted the season final, and Slater topped his group in the first qualifying session to start second, only behind Giltaire, while De Palo lined up in ninth.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gascoigne |first=Roger |date=2025-10-25 |title=Giltaire beats Slater to FREC pole at chilly Monza |url=https://formulascout.com/giltaire-beats-slater-to-frec-pole-at-chilly-monza/135565 |access-date=2025-12-28 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> The Italian's race began with a collision that caused damage to his front wing, necessitating a pit stop and dropping him to the back of the field. He began fighting back up the order while Slater attacked Giltaire for the lead, eventually taking it in the final stages of the race. De Palo used two safety car phases to climb back up the order, but eleventh place at the finish was not enough to deny Slater, who won ahead of Giltaire and Clerot, the title with a race to spare.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gascoigne |first=Roger |date=2025-10-25 |title=Freddie Slater takes FREC title with stylish Monza race one victory |url=https://formulascout.com/freddie-slater-takes-frec-title-with-stylish-monza-race-one-victory/135603 |access-date=2025-12-28 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> Fresh off the back of that success, Slater took pole position for the season final, leading De Palo.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gascoigne |first=Roger |date=2025-10-26 |title=Slater takes sixth FREC pole of season in Monza finale |url=https://formulascout.com/slater-takes-sixth-frec-pole-of-season-in-monza-finale/135632 |access-date=2025-12-28 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref> The championship top two battled all race, with the newly crowned champion coming out on top when the race finished under safety car conditions to round out his campaign with another win. De Palo took second, matching his place in the standings, while Giltaire took third after having been in that position all race.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gascoigne |first=Roger |date=2025-10-26 |title=Slater rounds off FREC season at Monza with eighth victory |url=https://formulascout.com/slater-rounds-off-frec-season-at-monza-with-eighth-victory/135666 |access-date=2025-12-28 |website=Formula Scout |language=en-US}}</ref>

Slater entered Formula Regional as a double Formula 4 champion, making him a title favorite despite failing to take the [[2025 Formula Regional Middle East Championship|Formula Regional Middle East Championship]] title earlier in the year. And despite taking eight wins, four further podiums and six pole positions on the way to a deserved title success, his campaign was far from straightforward. Two retirements in the opening four races, a disqualification in Imola and a non-score at the Red Bull Ring kept sophomore driver De Palo right on his tail all year long, with the pair trading the championship lead seven times across the season. Still, Slater delivered when it mattered, scoring a brilliant double win at the season final to deny De Palo any chance at claiming the title, even without the misfortune that happened to the Italian in the penultimate race of the year.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Brusa |first=Francesca |date=2025-11-08 |title=‘It’s never over until the final lap’: The mindset that led Slater to four titles in three years |url=https://feederseries.net/2025/11/08/its-never-over-until-the-final-lap-the-mindset-that-led-slater-to-four-titles-in-three-years/ |access-date=2026-01-02 |website=Feeder Series |language=en}}</ref>
