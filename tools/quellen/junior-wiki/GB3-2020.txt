The '''2020 BRDC British Formula 3 Championship''' was a [[Motorsport#Motor racing|motor racing]] championship for [[Open-wheel car|open wheel]], [[Formula racing|formula racing cars]] held across [[England]]. The 2020 season was the fifth and final organised by the [[British Racing Drivers' Club]] in the United Kingdom. The championship featured a mix of professional motor racing teams and privately funded drivers, and also featured the 2-litre 230-bhp Tatuus-Cosworth single seat race car in the main series. The season commenced at [[Oulton Park]] on 1 August and ended on 8 November at [[Silverstone Circuit]], after seven triple and quadruple header events for a total of twenty-four races.<ref name="Calendar">{{cite news|title=2020 BRDC British F3 Championship calendar revealed|url=https://www.britishf3.com/british-f3-news/2020-brdc-british-f3-championship-calendar-revealed/|date=14 September 2019|publisher=BRDC British F3 Championship|accessdate=12 October 2018|archive-date=12 October 2019|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20191012193435/https://www.britishf3.com/british-f3-news/2020-brdc-british-f3-championship-calendar-revealed/|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref name="Oulton">{{Cite news|url=https://www.britishf3.com/british-f3-news/brdc-british-f3-season-opener-postponed-due-to-coronavirus-outbreak/|title=BRDC British F3 season opener postponed due to Coronavirus outbreak|work=BRDC British F3 Championship|date=17 March 2020|access-date=17 March 2020|archive-date=17 March 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200317231715/https://www.britishf3.com/british-f3-news/brdc-british-f3-season-opener-postponed-due-to-coronavirus-outbreak/|url-status=dead}}</ref> [[Kaylen Frederick]] won the drivers' championship ahead of [[Kush Maini]] and British driver [[Louis Foster]].<ref>{{Cite web|date=2020-11-08|title=British F3 - Frederick surges to ninth win to secure championship|url=https://www.britishf3.com/british-f3-news/2020-silverstone-race-three-report/|access-date=2020-11-08|website=BRDC British F3 Championship|language=en|archive-date=2020-11-08|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201108161209/https://www.britishf3.com/british-f3-news/2020-silverstone-race-three-report/|url-status=dead}}</ref>

== Championship standings ==

; Scoring system

Points were awarded to the top 20 classified finishers in races one, three and four, with the second race awarding points to only the top 15. Race two, which reversed the order of the race one finishers, providing they set a lap time within 103% of the fastest driver, awarded extra points for positions gained from the drivers' respective starting positions.
