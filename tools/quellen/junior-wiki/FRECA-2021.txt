The '''2021 Formula Regional European Championship by Alpine''' was a multi-event, [[Formula Three|Formula 3]] [[Open-wheel car|open-wheel single seater]] [[Motorsport|motor racing]] championship held across Europe. The championship featured a mix of professional and amateur drivers, competing in Formula 3 cars that conform to the [[FIA]] Formula 3 regulations for the championship. This was the third season of the championship and the first after a merger with [[Formula Renault Eurocup]] which resulted to the change of the engine supplier to [[Alpine F1 Team|Alpine]].

The season commenced on 17 April at [[Imola Circuit]] and concluded on 31 October at [[Autodromo Nazionale di Monza]], after ten rounds.

Swiss driver [[Grégoire Saucy]], driving for [[ART Grand Prix]], took the drivers' title in Round 9 at [[Mugello Circuit|Mugello]].<ref>{{Cite web|last=Wood|first=Ida|date=2021-10-09|title=Saucy crowned FREC champion at Mugello as Aron gets first win|url=https://formulascout.com/saucy-crowned-frec-champion-at-mugello-as-aron-gets-first-win/85033|access-date=2021-10-09|website=Formula Scout|language=en-US}}</ref>

==Teams and drivers==
Thirteen teams had been pre-selected to the 2021 championship. Teams had a maximum of three cars, but were allowed to run a fourth car if they enter with a female driver.<ref name=":0">{{cite news|date=9 December 2020|title=FREC calendar grows to 10 rounds and two F1 support slots for 2021|work=formulascout.com|publisher=Formula Scout|url=https://formulascout.com/frec-calendar-grows-to-10-rounds-and-two-f1-support-slots-for-2021/72505|access-date=10 December 2020}}</ref> G4 Racing took over [[Bhaitech]]'s entry<ref name=":3" /> and [[M2 Competition (team)|M2 Competition]] withdrew as only twelve teams are allowed to compete in the series.<ref>{{Cite web|date=16 March 2021|title=12 teams and 32 drivers ready for testing in Imola|url=https://formularegionaleubyalpine.com/12-teams-and-32-drivers-ready-for-testing-in-imola/|url-status=live|website=Formula Regional by Alpine|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210316115557/https://formularegionaleubyalpine.com/12-teams-and-32-drivers-ready-for-testing-in-imola/ |archive-date=16 March 2021 }}</ref>
