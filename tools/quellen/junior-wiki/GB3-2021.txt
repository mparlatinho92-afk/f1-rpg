The '''2021 GB3 Championship''', known as the '''2021 BRDC British Formula 3 Championship''' until August 2021, was a [[Motorsport#Motor racing|motor racing]] championship for [[Open-wheel car|open wheel]], [[Formula racing|formula racing cars]] held across [[England]]. The 2021 season was the sixth organised by the [[British Racing Drivers' Club]] in the United Kingdom. The championship featured a mix of professional motor racing teams and privately funded drivers, and also featured the 2-litre 230-bhp Tatuus-Cosworth single seat race car in the main series. The season ran over eight triple- and quadruple-header rounds, it started on 22 May at [[Oulton Park|Brands Hatch]] and ended on 17 October at [[Donington Park]].<ref>{{Cite web|last=Wood|first=Elliot|date=2020-10-16|title=BRDC British F3 reveals provisional 2021 calendar|url=https://formulascout.com/brdc-british-f3-reveals-provisional-2021-calendar/69551|access-date=2020-11-08|website=Formula Scout|language=en-US}}</ref> The series was rebranded mid-season from BRDC British Formula 3 Championship.<ref>{{Cite web|date=2 August 2021|title=British F3 rebranded as GB3 Championship|url=https://www.gb-3.net/british-f3-news/british-f3-rebranded-as-gb3-championship/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210802190720/https://www.gb-3.net/british-f3-news/british-f3-rebranded-as-gb3-championship/|archive-date=2 August 2021|website=GB3 Championship}}</ref>

[[Zak O'Sullivan]] took the drivers' championship at the final round at [[Donington Park]], as [[Carlin Motorsport|Carlin]] took the inaugural teams' championship.

== Race calendar and results ==
The provisional calendar was revealed on 15 October 2020. After being cancelled because of the COVID-19 pandemic in 2020, the round at [[Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps|Spa-Francorchamps]] returned to the series. The series supported the [[2021 British GT Championship|British GT Championship]] at seven of its eight meetings.<ref>{{Cite web|date=2020-10-15|title=British F3 - BRDC British F3 announces provisional 2021 calendar|url=https://www.britishf3.com/british-f3-news/brdc-british-f3-announces-provisional-2021-calendar/|access-date=2020-11-08|website=BRDC British F3 Championship|language=en|archive-date=2020-11-08|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201108141735/https://www.britishf3.com/british-f3-news/brdc-british-f3-announces-provisional-2021-calendar/|url-status=dead}}</ref> The calendar was slightly changed later on to ensure eight rounds could be run.<ref>{{Cite web|date=2021-01-19|title=British F3 - BRDC British F3 issues revised 2021 calendar|url=https://www.britishf3.com/british-f3-news/2021-amended-calendar/|access-date=2021-01-19|website=BRDC British F3 Championship|language=en|archive-date=2021-01-19|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210119170723/https://www.britishf3.com/british-f3-news/2021-amended-calendar/|url-status=dead}}</ref>
